# Technical views — inspected, classified, and deliberately NOT art direction

Generated by `node tools/views.mjs` with a deterministic pure-Node PNG writer
(`tools/png.mjs`). No browser, no third-party package, no network, no new tileset.

## Classification

- **`MAP_ANALYTICAL_VIEW`** — 15 images generated here from physical rasters plus authored
  vector geometry. Full-world views are 1000×700 (250×175 grid at 4 px per 8 au cell);
  detail and scale views are exactly 640×360. Titles/keys are drawn with a built-in 3×5
  bitmap font. These are **schematic / technical**, not Web renders and not art direction.
- **`REAL_WEB_RUNTIME_CAPTURE`** — 6 byte-preserved genuine 640×360 Web captures from
  Gameplay LR02 M4, in `evidence/web_real/` with SHA-256 in
  `evidence/web_captures_index.v1.json`. They are **not** Map analytical views.
- `ACTUAL_WEB_RENDERED_INTEGRATION_PROOF = NOT_AVAILABLE__GAMEPLAY_OWNED`.

## Actually inspected

`world_terrain_full.png`, `detail_po_academy.png`, `scale_U20_camera.png` and
`detail_br4_crossing.png` were opened and read back as images during this run. The full
world shows 8 distinguishable landmasses including snow/ash islands, authored roads and
rivers and the 10×7 chunk grid; POI details resolve individual rooms, roof polygons,
openings and the vertical shaft; the BR-4 detail shows the road/river/elder crossing and
the proposed non-canonical deck. No visual approval is claimed.
