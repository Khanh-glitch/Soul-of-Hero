## 2. NPC kinds and identity

- `TRANSIENT`: a roadside person who may speak, trade, give/receive a finite physical item once, leave, or stay. No assumption of recruitability.
- `RECRUITABLE`: a run-unique actor with a region/role profile and a situational reason to join; no individually written biography is required. Discoverable as an optional encounter on accessible existing roads/POI exteriors, not universally purple/boss locked. Not every positive interaction results in consent.
- `RESIDENT`: a recruitable actor who physically arrives, receives a valid home/bed, persists as a member of the player's settlement; retains a profession but may be idle.
- Existing `vendor` NPCs with real prices/stock continue as vendors, NOT automatic free-drop residents. Conversation, trade and recruitment rights are separate capabilities on one actor.
- Stable `survivor_id` for the entire run, independent of local-window entity allocation; identity, name, trade stock, starting equipment, ownership, condition, unique actions and remembered interactions are saved. On runtime reload or reprojecting the Veyra window, **reconstruct** the same actor; never create a second resident. NPCs are **NOT inventory items**, recipes, pets or cloned `Creature` aliases.
- Encounter sources should be authored by family/POI purpose and deterministic seed with exact physical coordinates; spawn in navigable cells that do not block stairs, doors, route clearance, vendor access or 640×360 interaction. No new geography needed.

## 3. Recruitment and movement state machine

`UNMET -> MET -> (UNWILLING | OPEN_TO_JOIN) -> FOLLOWING / WAITING -> ARRIVED -> RESIDENT -> (AWAY | LEFT | INJURED | DEAD | EVACUATED)`. Injury, inventory, trust/consent and work status are orthogonal, not extra quest checkpoints.
- Free conversation provides **observable information**. Item help, medical treatment or local machine fixes operate on existing entities and physical inventory. Completing the condition BEFORE speaking counts; no `quest_started` flag, fabricated accept/reward step or mandatory NPC dialogue.
- Recruitment requires expressed NPC consent, player opt-in, a reasonably viable destination and an actually viable physical route. A human may decline if protecting another person, seriously injured or unwilling to abandon a place. Not all NPCs can be recruited; no arbitrary affinity XP bar.
- Escort choices are **Follow / Wait / Release**. Resident navigation uses genuine path/collision/floor restrictions and weather/hazard costs where supported; player can interrupt without resetting progress. No NPC teleport home, no remote rescue from map menu, no NPC picked up in inventory or added to home on click.
- If the player dies, resident/escort remains at its last causally justified position or takes a **known legal** fallback; death respawns only the player. If crossing a window or offline boundary, preserve global coordinate, floor, planned path and progress; advance only with certified traversability and travel time. A blocked route pauses with an actionable explanation, not fabricated arrival.
- NPC ownership/control is voluntary companionship: `Release` and `Change Home` must be available with actual paths; NPC may physically leave under comprehensible conditions. No arbitrary kidnapping, NPC loot farming, vendor stock exploit or involuntary conscription.

## 4. Settlement eligibility and resident needs

- A settlement is a **player-built owned shelter anywhere K6 allows**; no cabinet or new special building required for first resident. Assign an owned intact `bed_straw` or later authorized real bed, in a sheltered/roofed enclosed reachable room. **One resident per dedicated sleeping slot**, player rest bed constraints unchanged; do not conjure extra sleeping slots. A cupboard/claim improves existing territory rules but does not become a recruitment gate.
- Home identity is a stable set of physically adjacent/accessible owned structures (bed, roof, door, station, containers). When a structure collapses, loses valid ownership or becomes unreachable, resident job/shelter eligibility updates; resident actor persists at its true location. No arbitrary reset/removal of occupants.
- Five **observable functional conditions**, not five monetized gauges: **Health**, **Food/Water supply**, **Rest**, **Exposure/Shelter**, **Safety/Threat**. Hunger/thirst and wounds must originate from actual consumption/events. Temperature/radiation/climate are the same K2/K5 laws as applicable, with proven differences only.
- Each resident consumes **actual eligible food and actual potable/charged water** from explicitly assigned supplies on a clocked schedule; no invisible global pantry. Personal carried rations may be consumed once. Work slows then stops as needs deteriorate. No unexplained starvation death on load, nor immortal invulnerability in real combat/hazard. At safely sheltered offline home, lack of supplies first produces a clear `UNFED/DEHYDRATED -> UNFIT_FOR_WORK` condition; any later critical harm/departure requires a documented causal timeline and advance warning, never a blind login punishment.
- Rest requires a valid assigned bed and enough scheduled time; sleeping/work/guarding cannot all occur at once. Residents have short idle/social/home animations whose physical state matches their actual action; no extra cosmetic NPC multiplying work.
- No complex global Morale, romance, marriage, taxation, reputation economy, personality leveling, infinite social gifts or base census minigame in V1. Social memory is a **bounded ledger of real events** (rescued, treated, invited, worked together, lost home, survived attack), not a mandatory affection meter.

## 5. NPC occupation model — exact capabilities, inputs and blocked states

Every resident can have a profession and a single active assignment + bounded standby policy. A profession is an **affordance**, not an unlimited passive multiplier. Skills can affect **work handling time/accuracy within a measured baseline**, but not create output, skip recipe knowledge or violate durability/upkeep.

| Salvager | Owned Recycler/authorized salvage bench | Specific item UID to recycle, station availability, allowed material semantics | Exactly one existing recycler yield, with recorded loss; never automatic Core/card/unique disassembly | No item, tool, station or authorized item class |
| Mechanic | Owned Workbench / repair-compatible station | Damaged tool UID + existing per-item repair BOM (e.g. Salvager Axe 1 Refined Iron +1 Parts) | Existing repair action completes, same durability and charge law | Missing tool, materials, bench, access, resident fit |
| Cook | Campfire/authorized food station | Existing recipe, food item stacks, real fuel/water/time and slot | Existing edible output physically occupies actual output slot | No fuel/food/slot, fire extinguished |

| Guard | Assigned reachable patrol/guard post in owned home footprint | Actual weapon UID, durability, arrows/charges if relevant, rest, line of sight | Actual attacks against **observed** hostile actor, ammo and durability debited | No range/visibility/ammo, injury, retreat, rest |

**Hard rules:**
1. No NPC auto-harvest/loot/fight in unloaded distant POIs or conjure remote freight. Inside-base carrying must follow authorized custody and physical access; no pulling any item from any chest anywhere.
2. Station jobs use existing queue IDs, owner, input reservations, fuel/water/power and output slots. Player and NPC never both finish/pay the same job; output is not minted when job paused. Rev 6 item recipes and frozen old-job migration are untouched.
3. A resident may **queue**, **service**, **complete** or **maintain** a job only when that specific action is allowed by the ordinary player/station contract; do not bypass recipe-knowledge tier, station upgrade, power gate, per-unit batch cost or item uniqueness.
4. Auto-treatment of the player requires explicit opt-in and physical reach; by default medic treats legitimately assigned residents. Auto-repair of damaged structures is **intentional paid work**, not magic free regeneration after attack. Guard AI defends, not automatically raids/spawns opponents.
5. Work sharing at home is **local logistics**, not a separate clan shared-recipe system; the run's existing recipe knowledge ledger remains sole authority.

**Creator clarification 2026-11-16:** NPC should fundamentally feel like **neutral combat-capable world actors**: the player and an NPC can both attack, wound or kill each other under the same physical combat principles used for hostile creatures; unlike beasts, NPCs may also talk, trade, recruit or participate in a community depending on identity. Valuable equipment can be physically owned by someone far stronger than the player. The price or requested barter goods for that equipment may be difficult to obtain, and the owner can refuse to trade; stealing or defeating the owner is a genuine alternative with genuine danger and **lasting, locally grounded revenge**. Do not reduce this to a scripted 'steal dialogue', a player karma meter, a universal pardon button or a safe deterministic robbery minigame. The rest of §16 defines engineering safeguards for this simple gameplay model.

**Allowed player actions:** Direct weapon strike, projectile, AoE/Core effect on a legally hittable NPC target, threat/intimidation when physically in reach with credible equipped weapon, item theft from a physically accessible NPC-owned object/container, and robbery of surrendered/captured **carried** possessions. These are actions inside K1/K5 combat and K1 custody, not new loot-chest classes. An armed intimidation attempt may be rejected, trigger flight/combat or lead to relinquishing existing carried items depending on threat, cover, nearby help and NPC condition; no forced 100% surrender, free item or fabricated dialogue reward. Theft of unattended property is visible if discovered; the actor is not magically notified over the whole map.

**Commerce and rarity:** NPCs differ by real personal role, equipment, trading willingness, actual stock and valuation. A merchant may own an excellent item and ask for uncommon, physically obtainable payment or refuse because it is their only weapon/essential tool. An ordinary villager is not required to be a merchant, and a recruitable guard does not owe their rare gear to a friendly conversation. Valuation, buyer affordability, barter eligibility and willingness must be player-legible, consistent with existing item ownership and the 157-item economy; do not conjure new barter currencies or force every named valuable item to be tradeable. The same physical high-value gear has one authoritative UID whether kept, sold, stolen, dropped after death or recovered later.

**Property rights (critical economy split):** Track explicit ownerId, location and custody for (a) on-person equipment/pack; (b) authored home/settlement storage or shop stock; (c) world G1B chests; (d) restricted SPECIAL_AUTHORITY cash/cards/Elder stones/escrow sources. **Killing an NPC can drop their actual personal owned gear in exactly one persistent recoverable bag**, and only what actually belonged to them. A vendor's abstract current vendorStock balance is NOT a secret carried backpack that spills in full on death; migrate actual stock to a secured, physically present shop container/ledger before allowing theft of it. Locked storeroom access requires real breach/door permissions; any legitimately stolen stock depletes the **same source budget** as sales and remains at most one copy. Do not automatically place 157 items or unlimited currency on bodies.

**Never violate special-source policy:** Ragnarok escrow/purchase pathway, profile secured cash, run-unique cards and Core odds are not downgraded to 'kill trader for free key/Core/escrow'. They are only physically stealable when the exact authored owner/source and special authority independently authorize the item, with source UID consumed once. A corpse cannot recreate the vendor's Ragnarok Cash sales inventory or bypass a required profile transaction. Ordinary G1B chests stay separate; nearby NPC presence alone does not secretly change approved chest loot/access.

**Reaction and proportionality:** An NPC actually attacked may defend itself using equipped weapon and ammunition, flee via legitimate routes, surrender goods, call for help if perceived/able, hide, refuse trade or decline recruitment. **NPC combat power is not scaled down to ensure the robbery is winnable**: skills/equipment/health/terrain/cover/companions can make an NPC stronger than the player and realistically kill them. Other people react only if they see/hear a real event, see a victim's injury, receive an actual local warning or investigate evidence. Guards choose physical interception/protection/retreat; they are not immortal police or a PvP faction with spawned squads. Direct attacks have priority over work. Avoid aggression loops where NPC and player no longer have true mutual detection.

**Memory:** Store bounded incident facts (victim, aggressor identity when known, witnesses, time, place, property/source IDs, injury/death, recovery/restitution). No global red/green morality meter, faction XP or fast amnesia after scene reload. Theft, threat, serious injury and murder must produce meaningfully different local consequences. A victim of real injury is not obliged to join the attacker. A frightened recruited resident may refuse commands, abandon dangerous home or leave via a real route after a witnessed act. Any possible reconciliation must arise from a **separate authored, causally meaningful event**; giving a random gift or waiting one hour never automatically removes an assault/murder record. See §21 for the remaining discretionary reconciliation boundary.

**Damage, downing and death:** Human HP/status/hitbox use the established actor-combat contracts, weapon wear/ammo and credible cover; block attacks through walls, closed doors, roofs and different floors. **Defeat does not automatically mean death.** Specify a real downed/incapacitated state with injury, possible bleeding/recovery/aid, limited action and the existing personal-item custody. A seriously wounded opponent may remain lying on the ground as a living survivor, be rescued or treated by their community, and remember the attacker. Downed humans cannot magically stand, pursue or call allies without an actual recovery/communication event. Lethal damage after a valid damage/recovery threshold can cause final in-run death, which no new NPC instance replaces. The body/gear bag gets exactly one custody transfer, is persistent across windows and reload, and belongs to source person. Other residents do not instantly clone the killed worker. Hostility does not grant extra ordinary rations, Core levels, special cards or money.

**Revenge is a principal gameplay consequence, not an optional dialogue refusal:** A directly attacked/wounded/robbed survivor who lives forms a **persistent retaliatory intent**, and allies who actually witness, discover the injury or receive an actual report can form their own response. A survivor may (1) become hostile when the player later crosses their genuine home/work/patrol territory, (2) set a watch/ambush on a route the survivor legitimately knows the player uses, or (3) **physically track the player out of the encounter, discover the player's home and attack that base**, alone or with actual informed allies. Different personalities, injury, available weapons, group support, danger and travel path can produce different strategies. Retaliatory intent survives save/window changes/player death; the **specific attack occurs only if the victim is capable, knows where to go and can physically arrive**. For an NPC with a community, discovered assault can lead to treatment, a watch and a finite local reprisal; for a solitary NPC, there may be a solo ambush/pursuit after recovery. A dead NPC cannot take revenge personally; living comrades may if they learn of the act. Revenge that cannot yet reach a target remains a persistent *investigation/pursuit intent*, not an invented hit or automatic absolution.

**Pursuit and base finding MUST reuse K5 trace/observation epistemology:** an NPC learns the player's current position from real line-of-sight/hearing or a **concrete footprint/track on a substrate they can detect**, with age, weather loss, interruption and local search; a known route may justify a believable wait. NPCs do not know player coordinates, construction positions or online/offline state by reading RunState. Discovering a base requires actually following credible evidence/route or having observed it earlier. Chasing to the base can result in direct NPC-vs-player/resident combat and damage to **reachable, destructible structures** only when the NPC has the actual weapon/tool/intent/capability; base turrets and resident guards can defend with real ammo and line-of-sight. Combat difficulty comes from existing actors, their gear and terrain, not new spawned raid waves.

**No synthetic consequences:** No random 'villager anger raid', hostile NPC teleport to player's home, global heat timer, guards omniscient to an unseen crime, mob waves after theft, permanent crime jail that locks solo progression, or forced quest. A pursuing NPC must have true information, travel and real line of attack. Player death does not reset witnessed history within the same run; finalize erases run-local crime ledger together with residents under K8.

An independently living miner can reserve an accessible known ore source, travel to it, spend tool/time/hazard cost, obtain the legitimate yield, carry and store/trade/consume it, returning with a material change to the exact source. The same source stock is visible to and depleted for the player: **no parallel invisible NPC inventory drop table**, no duplicated harvest.

**Authority of an autonomous NPC's action** is the tuple `actorUid + sourceId + capability + actual position/floor + legal inventory/tool + action time + output custody/receipt`. Independent schedules choose among presently feasible real actions; they never assert a successful transaction merely because a narrative says 'the villager went to work'. If the engine lacks a valid water fill, mining or repair action for a prop, show non-economic atmosphere, not claimed item production.

**Distinguish resource categories:**
- Public/authored natural nodes (ore, plants, firewood, water) may be competed for if physically accessible. Long-term depletion/recovery obeys the existing source rule, not a new NPC refresh.
- Player-owned land, crop plots, workbenches, storage, laid-down equipment and household goods are **not** NPC's free supplies. Residents get only explicit assigned access; robbers require the same physical theft/breach/perception rights as other actors.
- Approved **G1B 16-type chest loot** remains a separate finite/renewable loot contract. Ordinary roaming NPCs do **not** silently open every unopened chest in unloaded POIs. A specific local NPC opening a specific chest, if later authorized as a visible real action, must compete for the *same* G1B source stock and be economically modeled; never create an NPC-only mirror chest.
- Rare, Ragnarök, locked card-room and Field-Core sources are not 'ambient NPC resources'. A live NPC may acquire a special item only from explicit allowed authored source, exact access and custody; never claim general scavenging rights.
- NPC-made food and equipment are ordinary physical output. The item exists in one actual store or pack, not simultaneously in a village's abstract economic tally and on a trader.
  
For far-away situations: compact per-actor event ledgers may advance **known physical work** through a certified path, owned source and funded station; unknown journeys and hostile encounters become PENDING with a reason. No full-island invisible NPC who is simultaneously mining, fighting and trading. The current no-autonomous-unloaded-distant-POI rule becomes **no unproven or unconstrained distant resource action**, not a prohibition on B2's real autonomy.

### 24.3 Arrivals, village history and people who do not respawn (3B)

Each site maintains living people and finite arrival candidates separately: `resident UIDs + vacant real homes/beds + local safety/access + finite origin candidate IDs + bounded incoming route states`. An arrival has an own personal history/possessions/role and starts at an actual origin, with global position and physical transit. **Being short of a profession does not automatically spawn a replacement of that profession.** A village can lose its only smith/guard/medic within a run. People may leave because they genuinely lack shelter/safety/supplies, or move in because they have a viable origin, need and journey. No new births/children subsystem in V1 and no instant immigration waves. A settlement need not 'recover' its starting population after a massacre.

If an arriving NPC owns gear/Core, it comes from the **arrival's separately allocated, audited initial source budget**, not a new grant rolled on every regional re-entry. Arrival stock is exactly-once and has visible global origin. Communities may shelter, refuse or become hostile to a newcomer in response to their own knowledge and available resources. None of this replaces the player's ability to recruit consenting people into a player-built home.

### 24.4 NPC-held Creator Cores and trading economics (4B)

A Core belongs to the exact NPC's physical item UID before a trade. They must carry sufficient lawful physical energy/ammo for cast, can exhaust it, suffer shared Core durability wear, and only use LMB/Q/E available at the Core's actual Lv1/Lv2/Lv3. NPC use is not an alternative unmetered source of energy. A defeated owner may lose one equipped Core through the same robbery/death custody path as any other item, and that instance is removed from the owner's gear/stock. A survivor may later want their exact stolen Core returned.

**Core sourcing must be explicit**: `npc-core-source-id -> type/level -> actor UID -> instance UID -> transfer history`. NPC ownership is a newly authorized class of physical source by 4B, but **does not itself approve arbitrary numeric rates or an infinite series of arrivals carrying rare Cores**. Budget NPC-held Core instances separately and compare with approved 16 G1B chest odds and existing recipe/unique POI sources. Retain the exact 16 chest distributions and per-tier refresh contracts; balance NPC count/type/access/arrival provenance, not silent chest odds edits. Distinguish initial world-owned item from a Core crafted/acquired using legitimate recipe/loot with an existing mint receipt. Four Field Cores cannot be casually replaced by Creator Core roll rules.

A Core or valuable weapon may be unavailable for sale because the owner needs it, or available only for a difficult but physically attainable deal. Offer/acceptance checks actual owned stock, visible demands, ownership and transfer receipts; no forced 'trade anything for Parts', no hidden auto-gift upon positive dialogue.

### 24.5 Live bandits, retaliators and player-base theft (1B + 5B)

**Not all violent humans are revenge-driven.** Some authored civilians/guards are neutral; some extortionists/ambushers have independent motives and real loot desires. They can initiate violence only after physically detecting and reaching the player/cargo, or credible prepared route encounter. A harmless sleeper on another floor cannot attack because the player crossed a POI centroid. Avoid infinite road-banding spawn loops and arbitrary auto-aggression at New Game bootstrap.

The **distinct retaliation chain** remains: actual injured/robbed person or informed ally -> durable grievance -> actionable evidence -> recovery/equipment -> real pursuit/route/ambush -> actual physical contact. If a trace/last-known trail becomes invalid, the chase can fail while grievance persists. No death, theft, loss of wall HP or forced base loss is fabricated on login.

**Base encounter and theft:** humans can use their actual attack/tool/lock/access capability to pass a legitimate reachable entrance or break a destructible obstruction. A nearby locked chest is not open merely because the NPC is adjacent. Once a physical container or item becomes accessible by actual authorized rules, item stacks/UIDs may be taken from it into the NPC's limited carried slots, with explicit unit-by-unit transfer and capacity. Their tools/weapon/energy/consumables are exhausted as normal. A human who retrieves an object can physically carry it home or lose it in their own later death. Theft of **run-local physical Ragnarok Cash** still uses K1/K8 source/custody; protected profile secured cash, completed escrow, anti-farming rights and scores cannot be materialized as stealable gold. A thief who cannot reach the chest or carry the item takes nothing. More NPCs do not become hostile and teleport to the base simply because the victim has reported a crime.

**Offline handling:** a known grievance or heading does not prove a successful raid. Simulate verified movements/access events under time order when actually supported; preserve unresolved conflict if contact/combat would require unavailable physical simulation; no omniscient successful raid, free invulnerability, unreasoned kills or remote loot transfer. Human play remains authoritative for whether pursuit is legible and satisfying.

### 24.6 Party/expedition play (6B) without a second combat system

A consenting recruit's `FOLLOWING` is a real capable companion state, not just a transit animation. Expedition orders form a **small context action vocabulary**: Follow / Hold / Defend / Engage a legitimate observed target / Retreat / Return Home / Help carry. These are intents and permissions, **not** new MMO party stats. A companion will only engage enemies they can see or have a trustworthy report/waypoint for, using the same real hitbox, LOS, ammo/durability/energy and survival costs. The player may equip them by transferring item UIDs and can inspect exactly what they carry. Their ability to help is limited by individual skill, health, willingness, weight, terrain, floor and route. A Core-armed companion can cast only at authorized target and pays the real energy and wear.

A participant who is wounded, overburdened, exhausted or loses safe access can slow, wait, flee, be abandoned or die; never rubber-band teleport through a closed gate or appear beside the player after changing floors. A downed ally requires real rescue/medicine, and their pack is a single physical custody source. Other companions do not generate loot. Player death does not despawn or insta-recall the party; they remain at last justified point and may respond causally.

Residents may be switched between home-work and party duty, never duplicate their presence and work production during a trip. A worker on expedition cannot keep producing food at home as though they never left. Bringing a companion with legitimate carried goods back home still requires unloading to a specific reachable store and actual transfer.

**RESOLVED by later Creator choice B:** companions have their own will; an order to attack a neutral person is accepted, rejected or physically opposed according to personal ties, stable violence policy, observed danger and actual knowledge. Section 25.5 is authoritative; this is NOT pending.

## 25. Full-world production NPC system: combat, health, roles, consent and coverage (design review 2026-11-16)

**Scope correction from Creator:** The feature is not a three-NPC demonstration followed by an open-ended corrective cycle. Greywater, Slagworks and a roadside actor remain diagnostic scenarios, but the **intended product is a complete, general system operating across all relevant traversable Veyra spaces and route networks** in the same production New Game. A few authored characters, tests or screenshots are not a substitute for population coverage, equipment, injury, combat, occupation, daily autonomy, communities, recruitment, expedition companions, migration, robbery, response, revenge and offline continuity. Static content census and targeted tests are required quality gates **inside** full implementation, not excuses to postpone half the approved feature. Exact density is derived from place and source; not 1 NPC per POI, not a mandatory number of people.

**Creator approval:** companions have individual will (choice B): a recruited person can refuse to assault an uninvolved neutral civilian, choose to defend their own acquaintances, or willingly join a robbery, based on coherent identity and observed facts. This does not authorize an unreliable free-form LLM or a random re-roll on each command. **The algorithm below is a testable CONTROL_TOWER_DESIGN_PROPOSAL**, not proof of bug-free code.

**Basis verified at PR #61 SHA 176cdd19:** BAL.player.maxHp=100; stamina=100; speed=72 px/s. ITEMS use explicit damage, range, arc, swing duration and durability: Stone Axe 11/420 ms, Wood Spear 15/380 ms, Cleaver 17/330 ms, Bow 16/520 ms, Reinforced Spear 23/390 ms, Machete 24/330 ms. ### 25.1 One human, five independent axes: no 'eight professions = eight health classes'

A Survivor is an independent persistent adult combat-capable K5 actor, not an invulnerable quest object or a skin over a loot crate. Its gameplay can be understood through **five orthogonal axes**:
1. **Livelihood/occupation**: one main specialty among the eight approved jobs, plus ordinary learned affordances. 'Vendor', 'traveler', 'bandit' and 'resident' are social/encounter conditions, **not** ninth through twelfth item-producing profession systems.
2. **Combat capability**: physical conditioning, equipment UID, armor, weapon proficiency, line-of-sight and tactics. An unarmored Guard can be weak; a Cook may fight proficiently if correctly equipped; Core ownership is gear, not a HP class.
3. **Disposition/relationships**: protective, nonviolent, opportunistic or predatory tendencies and bounded actual personal relationships; community membership and known recent facts affect what the actor is willing to do. Do not add morality currency, faction XP or a universal reputation bar.
4. **Needs/condition**: current HP, wound severity, rest, stamina, food/water, temperature/radiation/status and perceived local threat. This controls activity capability, not magical invisible rank bonuses.
5. **Custody/schedule**: global location/floor, unique item UIDs, known physical sources, active order, route, last observed threat and timestamp; no duplication between world windows.

**Required data schema, as interfaces not a separate economy:** actorId/runId/sourceId; origin/arrival source; current global x,y,floor; home and bed/worksite; occupation and capabilities; baseline hpMax/staminaMax/speed and current HP/stamina/status/wounds; exact five equipment slots + carry pack; weapon and proficiency; Core UID/level/durability/cooldowns and carried Energy/Essence; community/member links; disposition, known and protected people; relationships and incident ledger; job authorization/queue/source/output; path and observed stimuli; hostile/revenge intent; residence/party state; downed/dead/evacuated; lastCommittedEvent/lastSimAt. Version and migrate additive save fields. One authoritative actor record, one render projection.

### 25.2 First-pass quantitative NPC combat balance (SOH_PROVISIONAL, not Creator-locked)

**Reference rule:** Start with actual player 100 HP and actual item weapon damage instead of invented 'NPC damage × class multiplier'. The table is a **concrete baseline for complete implementation**, then adjust only with measured actual fight durations and source economy, as a documented revision. These are authored physical/experience profiles, **not** fixed occupational bonuses:

| Physical/combat profile | Max HP | Max stamina | Walk speed px/s | Example use | Combat posture |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Civilian, untrained | 80 | 85 | 68 | inhabitant, patient, unarmed seller | seeks cover, flees when threatened |
| Laborer / artisan | 95 | 100 | 70 | mechanic, cook, miner, builder, medic | defend self with actual equipped tool |
| Traveler / scout | 90 | 105 | 76 | independent courier, pathfinder | avoid, reposition, flee or skirmish |
| Community guard | 110 | 110 | 72 | patrol/escort/defender | hold safe angles, cover others |
| Raider / robber | 105 | 110 | 76 | dangerous roadside human | approach with purpose, threaten, fight/retreat |
| Veteran / skilled fighter | 125 | 120 | 72 | exceptional guarded character | disciplined stance, tactical weapon/Core use |

An actor's actual assigned profile must be justified by origin/training/condition and recorded. A rare item does not grant +300 HP. A very powerful unique human can be stronger through equipment, proficiency, supported combat tactics and allies, rather than boss-inflated HP. Numeric profiles may be tuned using actual time-to-defeat and player feedback; no undisclosed adaptive scaling by player inventory, day or desire to steal the actor's item.

**Damage authority (all actors):** use the very same item damage, swing interval, hit arc/range, actual collision, LOS/floor checks, stamina/weapon wear, ammo, status and worn armor cap as player combat, with an item-authorized source event exactly once per hit. Provisional training modifiers may change handling speed/aim/reaction within measured bounds, **not silently multiply item damage**. No fake ranged hit at distance, no shoots through solid walls/closed gates/roof levels, no invulnerability for essential merchants. If the body has no weapon, a weak unarmed hit may use a documented low provisional damage such as 4 at 650 ms swing, not a magical hidden sword. Do not introduce a firearm class unless it has a legitimate item definition/source. Hostile humans also damage each other, defenders, creatures and authorized destructible structures via actual attack effects, subject to friendly-fire rules and actual hostility.

**Illustrative breakpoints, not guaranteed fight duration:** At 24 base Machete damage per valid hit, an unarmored 80 HP civilian takes 4 hits, 95 HP artisan 4; a 90 HP traveler with Scrap Helm (-10%) takes approximately 5; 110 HP guard with Leather Jerkin + Scrap Helm (-30%) takes approximately 7; a 125 HP veteran wearing a complete Iron Plate set (-54%) takes approximately 12. Player with 100 HP and no armor can be downed after about 5 valid 24-damage hits from an armed NPC. Positioning, swing timing, dodging, multiple attackers, wounds and gear durability can dramatically alter actual outcomes. **This is why testing only whether a damage number appears is inadequate.**

**Proficiency 0–3 (provisional, not a level treadmill):** untrained 1.15× item attack windup/recovery; trained 1.0×; skilled 0.92×; specialist 0.85×, provided animation and authoritative cooldown have not already encoded the modifier. Track proficiency per weapon family, not as global damage scaling or an XP bar automatically gained for every AI tick. A Guard may specialize in Bow, an artisan in hand tool, and a Core-bearing veteran in the particular Core's targeting; role never magically grants ammo, energy or higher Core level. Attacks need visible anticipatory frames (weapon draw/aim/stance), hit and recovery; NPC combat must be readable at 640×360 and survivable by a skilled, cautious early-game player where appropriate.

**Thresholds from condition, not duplicate classes:** >50% HP can act normally if otherwise fit; at 25–50%, wounded movement/attack handling and willingness to fight decline; <25% means critical risk, so many civilians/raiders retreat or surrender, while a determined guard may hold a position. Exact movement/aim penalties belong to tuneable balance, not secret hardcoded changes per occupation. No normal dialogue/trade/craft while downed.

### 25.3 Downed, injury, healing, death and physical looting

All human actors share a **non-binary** survival state: healthy/wounded -> critical -> DOWNED (alive, incapacitated, physical body) -> rescued/stabilized/recovering OR final DEAD if a genuinely lethal follow-up/wound/hazard occurs. An ordinary single hit that crosses zero HP can down rather than automatically kill; exceptionally destructive valid hits and persistent hazards may be genuinely fatal under a **documented common rule**. The existing monster kill-at-zero function is NOT reusable unchanged for humans.

The first down receipt stores hit event, attacker/source, position/floor, injury severity and initial downed condition. Downed bodies do not stand, flee, cast or report crimes magically. Bleeding must have a **clocked severity event chain** and a medically observable treatment window; an illustrative first balance is 8 real minutes to stabilize a critical bleeding wound and up to 20 minutes before fatal untreated escalation under the right injury conditions. These numbers are SOH_PROVISIONAL and must be validated against 336h actual survival, 24-minute fictional day/night and available medicine. If an actor had a nonbleeding injury, do not kill them simply because 20 minutes elapsed. Valid additional hits, live fire, poison and other existing hazards may kill if their documented combat rules apply. No synthetic remote fatality on login without events and known conditions.

A present Medic, player or other capable rescuer may treat real bleeding with actual authorized Bandage/Medkit/Syringe and accessible time/LOS, and may help a stabilized actor move to legitimate shelter. If the patient is left in place, their global position/floor persists while window streams and through separate-process reload. A recovered victim retains wounds, missing equipment, memory and relationships. They do not instantly regain full HP or get new copies of lost gear; the community needs a supply source to heal or replace it.

**Robbery does NOT require death:** an incapacitated or surrendered human can lose specific accessible, physically owned equipped/pack item UIDs via an atomic transfer. That same item is removed from their record, not re-rolled from a body. Robbery may occur under combat threat and be resisted; it creates an incident with actor identity, property source, time, location and observation. On actual final death only, one persistent recoverable bag is made from **remaining carried property**; no duplicate bag after returning/reload/kill. No automatic spill of merchant shop stock, profile secured Ragnarok cash or unrelated G1B chest contents.

### 25.5 Willful companions: deterministic, explainable, no random moral roulette (Creator choice B)

**The desired quality is a stable character decision, not simulated free-form thought.** Give each actor a compact, authored/seeded but persistent disposition:
- Protects: the actual community/group or specific observed people they currently recognize as protected; per-person family trees and bespoke biographies are NOT required.
- Violence policy: avoids unprovoked violence / conditional opportunist / predatory; distinct from being brave or skilled.
- Risk posture: cautious/ordinary/bold, relative to **perceived** (not omniscient) target power and available cover/gear.
- Loyalty/consent: what the person explicitly agreed to when joining, and known subsequent real events; not a universal XP-based affection meter.

**A single order is resolved once** from actor snapshot + visible target + known relationships + item/equipment + event history + stable orderId. Persist answer and reason for that order; do not reroll randomly every tick, scene entry, or reload. When world state materially changes (target attacks, actor wounded, rescuer intervenes), cancel/reconsider at an explicit new event boundary, not in an infinite evaluation loop. Order results are **OBEY / REFUSE_WITH_REASON / PROTECT_TARGET / RETREAT_OR_HOLD / CANNOT_EXECUTE**.

~~~text
evaluateCompanionOrder(actor, order, observedWorld):
  if actor.dead/downed/physically unreachable: CANNOT_EXECUTE
  if target cannot be identified or hit by current weapon/floor/LOS: CANNOT_EXECUTE
  if order targets actor's protected family/community/known close ally:
       REFUSE; if a protected person is under actual attack, PROTECT_TARGET
  if target is visibly attacking player/companion and no protected-person conflict:
       defend player if actor can do so; else retreat/cover with reason
  if player commands an unprovoked strike on a neutral person:
       if actor.violencePolicy forbids this: REFUSE
       else if actor.perceivedRisk too high for actor.riskPosture: RETREAT_OR_HOLD
       else: OBEY (actor knowingly joins a physical assault and its consequences)
  if order targets a known actual hostile and has valid reachable combat route:
       OBEY if physically able and within understood risk
  otherwise: REFUSE_WITH_REASON or CANNOT_EXECUTE; no silent arbitrary change
~~~

**Important variants:** a Greywater resident can refuse to attack a recognized community member and may intervene; a hired opportunistic fighter may agree to rob a stranger; a Medic may prioritize stabilizing a downed patient over a vindictive attack; a guard may defend the player from a genuine robber but balk at attacking a passive merchant. A selfish mercenary can abandon a suicidal fight and physically withdraw. **Do not treat actor profile as infallible omniscient psychology**: only actual observed and remembered facts can affect the decision. Show one concise contextual line: 'I won't hurt my neighbor', 'I have no arrows', 'I cannot reach that floor', 'That fight will kill us', or 'I'll help'. Character identity should remain consistent even when the user reloads.

**Technical zero-bug aspiration, not a guarantee:** exhaustively test an ordered decision matrix for all 5 result codes; same input+seed+orderId yields same response after restart; changing distant unseen world variables cannot alter the choice; protected relation overrides robbery enthusiasm; defender reaction needs actual aggression; no ally shoots its protected target through AOE without handling friendly-fire; command processing never mints item/damage/knowledge. Use property-based multi-seed tests and adversarial interactions as pre-release proof, not a 'we can fix it later' strategy.

### 25.6 Other social and combat roles: things people do, not mandatory ninth profession

- **Civilian/pacifist:** may speak, trade personal goods if willing, work and flee; owns real possessions, not always easy to rob if protected.
