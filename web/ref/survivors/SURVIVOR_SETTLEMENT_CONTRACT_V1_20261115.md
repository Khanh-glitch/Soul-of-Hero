## 0. Fantasy and nonnegotiable scope

- SOH is a free PvE survival sandbox. The player begins alone, scavenges and builds anywhere physically permitted; may meet, help, recruit, house, employ and protect **living survivors**. A community should create a home worth returning to, not compulsory management.
- Every core route remains viable **SOLO** (survival, crafting, research, base building, exploration, ordinary defense, non-boss Ragnarok cash path, evacuation). Recruitment is always optional; never a stage-gate, mandatory tutorial, daily quest or precondition for access to the ordinary 157-item economy.
- No settlement hub prefab, permanent village, required recruitment center, new terrain/biome, invented POIs, NPC recruitment-token item, morale currency, free companion summon, automatically successful remote missions or invisible random raids.
- The player builds the actual shelter and provides physical materials and time. Residents have identity, needs, autonomy, skills, tools, work and vulnerability; they do not become chests of loot or passive production buffs.

## 2. NPC kinds and identity

- `TRANSIENT`: a roadside person who may speak, trade, give/receive a finite physical item once, leave, or stay. No assumption of recruitability.
- `RECRUITABLE`: a named, run-unique actor with a plausible reason to join. Discoverable as an optional encounter on accessible existing roads/POI exteriors, not universally purple/boss locked. Not every positive interaction results in consent.
- `RESIDENT`: a recruitable actor who physically arrives, receives a valid home/bed, persists as a member of the player's settlement; retains a profession but may be idle.
- Existing `vendor` NPCs with real prices/stock continue as vendors, NOT automatic free-drop residents. Conversation, trade and recruitment rights are separate capabilities on one actor.
- Stable `survivor_id` for the entire run, independent of local-window entity allocation; identity, name, trade stock, starting equipment, ownership, condition, unique actions and remembered interactions are saved. On runtime reload or reprojecting the Veyra window, **reconstruct** the same actor; never create a second resident. NPCs are **NOT inventory items**, recipes, pets or cloned `Creature` aliases.
- Encounter sources should be authored by family/POI purpose and deterministic seed with exact physical coordinates; spawn in navigable cells that do not block stairs, doors, route clearance, vendor access or 640×360 interaction. No new geography needed.

## 3. Recruitment and movement state machine

`UNMET -> MET -> (UNWILLING | OPEN_TO_JOIN) -> FOLLOWING / WAITING -> ARRIVED -> RESIDENT -> (AWAY | LEFT | INJURED | DEAD | EVACUATED)`. Injury, inventory, trust/consent and work status are orthogonal, not extra quest checkpoints.
- Free conversation provides **observable information**. Item help, medical treatment or local machine fixes operate on existing entities and physical inventory. Completing the condition BEFORE speaking counts; no `quest_started` flag, fabricated accept/reward step or mandatory NPC dialogue.
- Recruitment requires expressed NPC consent, player opt-in, a reasonably viable destination and an actually viable physical route. A human may decline if protecting another person, seriously injured or unwilling to abandon a place. Not all NPCs can be recruited; no arbitrary affinity XP bar.
- Escort choices are **Follow / Wait / Release**. Resident navigation uses genuine path/collision/floor restrictions and weather/hazard costs where supported; player can interrupt without resetting progress. No NPC teleport home, no remote rescue from map menu, no NPC picked up in inventory or added to home on click.
- If the player dies, resident/escort remains at its last causally justified position or takes a **known legal** fallback; death respawns only the player. If crossing a window or offline boundary, preserve global coordinate, floor, planned path and progress; advance only with certified traversability and travel time. A blocked route pauses with an actionable explanation, not fabricated arrival.
- NPC ownership/control is voluntary companionship: `Release` and `Change Home` must be available with actual paths; NPC may physically leave under comprehensible conditions. No arbitrary kidnapping, NPC loot farming, vendor stock exploit or involuntary conscription.

## 4. Settlement eligibility and resident needs

- A settlement is a **player-built owned shelter anywhere K6 allows**; no cabinet or new special building required for first resident. Assign an owned intact `bed_straw` or later authorized real bed, in a sheltered/roofed enclosed reachable room. **One resident per dedicated sleeping slot**, player rest bed constraints unchanged; do not conjure extra sleeping slots. A cupboard/claim improves existing territory rules but does not become a recruitment gate.
- Home identity is a stable set of physically adjacent/accessible owned structures (bed, roof, door, station, containers). When a structure collapses, loses valid ownership or becomes unreachable, resident job/shelter eligibility updates; resident actor persists at its true location. No arbitrary reset/removal of occupants.
- Five **observable functional conditions**, not five monetized gauges: **Health**, **Food/Water supply**, **Rest**, **Exposure/Shelter**, **Safety/Threat**. Hunger/thirst and wounds must originate from actual consumption/events. Temperature/radiation/climate are the same K2/K5 laws as applicable, with proven differences only.
- Each resident consumes **actual eligible food and actual potable/charged water** from explicitly assigned supplies on a clocked schedule; no invisible global pantry. Personal carried rations may be consumed once. Work slows then stops as needs deteriorate. No unexplained starvation death on load, nor immortal invulnerability in real combat/hazard. At safely sheltered offline home, lack of supplies first produces a clear `UNFED/DEHYDRATED -> UNFIT_FOR_WORK` condition; any later critical harm/departure requires a documented causal timeline and advance warning, never a blind login punishment.
- Rest requires a valid assigned bed and enough scheduled time; sleeping/work/guarding cannot all occur at once. Residents have short idle/social/home animations whose physical state matches their actual action; no extra cosmetic NPC multiplying work.
- No complex global Morale, romance, marriage, taxation, reputation economy, personality leveling, infinite social gifts or base census minigame in V1. Social memory is a **bounded ledger of real events** (rescued, treated, invited, worked together, lost home, survived attack), not a mandatory affection meter.

## 5. NPC occupation model — exact capabilities, inputs and blocked states

Every resident can have a profession and a single active assignment + bounded standby policy. A profession is an **affordance**, not an unlimited passive multiplier. Skills can affect **work handling time/accuracy within a measured baseline**, but not create output, skip recipe knowledge or violate durability/upkeep.

| Specialty | Physical assignment | Inputs and costs | Concrete outcome | When it stops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salvager | Owned Recycler/authorized salvage bench | Specific item UID to recycle, station availability, allowed material semantics | Exactly one existing recycler yield, with recorded loss; never automatic Core/card/unique disassembly | No item, tool, station or authorized item class |
| Mechanic | Owned Workbench / repair-compatible station | Damaged tool UID + existing per-item repair BOM (e.g. Salvager Axe 1 Refined Iron +1 Parts) | Existing repair action completes, same durability and charge law | Missing tool, materials, bench, access, resident fit |
| Cook | Campfire/authorized food station | Existing recipe, food item stacks, real fuel/water/time and slot | Existing edible output physically occupies actual output slot | No fuel/food/slot, fire extinguished |
| Gardener | Owned reachable garden plots | Real seeds, water, climate, elapsed growth; individual labor time | Single manual plant/water/harvest step | Plant not ready, absent seed/water, work conflict |
| Medic | Accessible patient and clinic/bed area | Bandage/Medkit/Syringe etc. and valid treatment condition | Item heal effect / injury treatment to a physically present patient | No consumable, unreachable/ineligible patient |
| Guard | Assigned reachable patrol/guard post in owned home footprint | Actual weapon UID, durability, arrows/charges if relevant, rest, line of sight | Actual attacks against **observed** hostile actor, ammo and durability debited | No range/visibility/ammo, injury, retreat, rest |
| Engineer | Generator, electric network or valid industrial station | Real components/fuel/power/maintenance inputs; correct network/access authority | Existing electrical/maintenance or paid station job advanced legitimately | No connection/fuel/access, damaged device |
| Builder | Actual player-owned structure in reachable home | Existing physical repair materials debited; same HP & repair semantics | Repairs only after a committed paid repair task | Insufficient materials, unreachable/destroyed structure |
| Research aide (later V1+ capability) | Existing Research Table | Same authenticated Parts/specimen/blueprint and learning rules as K4 | Run-local single `learned_recipes` grant after true transaction | Knowledge already known, insufficient materials, tier gate |

**Hard rules:**
1. No NPC auto-harvest/loot/fight in unloaded distant POIs or conjure remote freight. Inside-base carrying must follow authorized custody and physical access; no pulling any item from any chest anywhere.
2. Station jobs use existing queue IDs, owner, input reservations, fuel/water/power and output slots. Player and NPC never both finish/pay the same job; output is not minted when job paused. Rev 6 item recipes and frozen old-job migration are untouched.
3. A resident may **queue**, **service**, **complete** or **maintain** a job only when that specific action is allowed by the ordinary player/station contract; do not bypass recipe-knowledge tier, station upgrade, power gate, per-unit batch cost or item uniqueness.
4. Auto-treatment of the player requires explicit opt-in and physical reach; by default medic treats legitimately assigned residents. Auto-repair of damaged structures is **intentional paid work**, not magic free regeneration after attack. Guard AI defends, not automatically raids/spawns opponents.
5. Work sharing at home is **local logistics**, not a separate clan shared-recipe system; the run's existing recipe knowledge ledger remains sole authority.

## 6. Resident priorities, workday and meaningful autonomy

**Default deterministic priority (within observed world state):** immediate physical danger/escape > urgent injury/triage > maintain own safety/seek valid shelter > scheduled sleep/meal > already-paid job > assigned maintenance/patrol > idle/social interaction. The user may configure guard duty and emergency policies without forcing suicidal behavior.
- Work is action-based: resident walks to target, visibly performs an animation, settles an existing mechanical action/receipt; a station can continue a **pre-funded autonomous device job** only when that station's actual rules allow it, independently of NPC presence.
- Shift timings, hunger/water per hour, walk speed, job handling speed, healing time, max community size and preferred standby radius remain **SOH_PROVISIONAL/MEASURED**. Never invent “LI official” values for these new NPC systems. No worker-cap rigidly tied to arbitrary new progression level; bed/food/workspace/path capacity are natural constraints.
- UI: tap/press F on resident -> small context panel `Talk / Trade (if capable) / Follow / Wait / Home / Assign Work / Equipment / Release` filtered by valid state. No multi-screen mandatory recruitment flow. Settlement summary shows residents, assignments, supplies, blocked reason and known threats, never psychic enemy intel. Render at native 640×360; no clipped panels or non-interactive portraits.

## 7. Guard and creature perception — absolute no-raid contract

- Residents may fight only by physically plausible action range, aim, damage, stamina/durability/charge and real hitbox, same combat source authority as K5. Guard can see/hear/observe within documented senses, patrol locally with actual collision, stand watch, respond to visible enemy or confirmed trail/stimulus, and physically retreat/seek shelter if overmatched. No guard `DPS buff aura`, ghost ammo or shoots through roofs/doors/floors.
- Hostile creatures keep their authored habitat, detection and trace epistemology. A resident visible to a creature can be spotted by **real line of sight**; any NPC-generated traces, if implemented, require real footsteps and lifetime/weather like player traces. **No "has residents -> roll invasion chance", no "NPC working -> magically spawns raiders", no omniscient location alert.** This **does not prohibit** an existing, aggrieved human actor from physically tracking the player to the house and intentionally attacking it with their real gear/known allies; this is source-grounded pursuit, not a random raid.
- Player-placed turret and resident guard are independent: turret consumes funded power/ammo, resident consumes their weapon/ammo and time. They can defend together without double-counting one hit or sharing the same arrow stock.
- If a structure/guard/resident is harmed while online, actual attack and damage receipt required. Offline combat/hazards can be resolved **only** from known physical initial conditions, path, detection and discrete event ordering; if engine cannot prove consequences, preserve a named unresolved/pending threat, do not synthesize a dead resident or free safe state. No full 60FPS rendering while offline.
- Resident death is **final within the run** after actual damage/status and any physically valid rescue window; downed/injured state permits medical treatment using real items. On actual death, equipment/inventory becomes one recoverable physical custody bag; no duplicate gear/recruit instance. Player death does not automatically kill, despawn or revive residents.

## 15. B2: continuing work is a bounded, explicit standing authorization

**Meaning of B2:** A player can assign 'continue producing/cooking/repairing/guarding within these constraints' rather than click a button per item. This is not a passive income bonus. Work **must** use one existing recipe/action authority, a specific real station/plot/site, eligible labor, genuine input stacks and output slots, travel time, fuel/power/water, equipment durability, learned recipe and *one durable queue/transaction*. The player may interrupt or reconfigure at any time. NPCs never bypass the station's own unattended device job semantics.

**StandingWorkOrder required fields:** orderId; residentId; homeId; stationId or reachable work area; allowed task/recipe/item class; repetition ON/OFF; inputSources[] (explicitly assigned named containers and reserved personal gear only); outputTarget; maxUnits or stop-after-time; minimum retained reserves (e.g. keep some food/water and special gear for player); allowed hours/sleep policy; available materials/energy budget; maintenance danger policy; state and reason; version; lastEvaluatedAt. Never treat 'any chest in the base' as authorized. The order can remain active over logout; it can run only while all constraints are satisfied.

**Transaction per unit:** validate NPC exists/is fit/at station; verify access and station owner; select one recipe/action legally known; reserve source item UIDs/quantities, fuel and output capacity **atomically**; establish unique queue/action ID; advance real time (including actual station fuel/power/weather interruptions); produce one existing output into specified physical container exactly once; debit worker/tool wear and update remaining budget; inspect constraints, then repeat only if still valid. On cancellation or source/station loss, apply the same documented player job refund/return rules, not a second free grant. Two NPCs and the player may not complete/claim one queue unit twice; recipe version changes must migrate funded jobs.

**Role-specific permissions, all eight (§5):**
- Salvager: repeat only **whitelisted** recyclable classes, with exact existing recycler yield and loss. Never autonomously consume a unique Core, badge, research-only specimen, card or gifted heirloom.
- Mechanic: repair only pre-assigned item UIDs or player-authorized classes, paid by existing repair BOM; stop at target durability/repair cap. Never create extra item UID by repairing and never silently repair a Core without approved Core bill.
- Cook: prepare allowed known recipes until output limit/food reserve threshold or fuel/water exhaustion. Cooking does not automatically eat all food reserved for survival.
- Gardener: repeated manual plant/water/harvest on **assigned reachable plots**, paying seeds/water and obeying growth/season/time, not bypassing optional physical farm controller's own capabilities.
- Medic: treat specified resident patients with actual supplies and reach. Automatic treatment of player remains opt-in. No free revive/teleport.
- Guard: patrol assigned reachable points, spend actual equipped weapon UID/Arrow/Core ammo and durability, observe and engage genuine physical threats, retreat/rest when necessary; obey §17 if player attacks civilians.
- Engineer: keep authorized equipment supplied/repaired only with real parts, maintenance action and device electricity; cannot unlock POI security remotely or draw Fuel Canister from unassigned shops.
- Builder: undertake pre-authorized paid repair/maintenance of specific owned structures with current damage and required input; never repair everything at once for free.

**Standby and safety:** Only one active work intent per NPC; defend/evacuate/heal/need before work. A task blocked by full output, missing input, unlearned recipe, damaged station, stopped power, path/door/floor, stolen gear, owner conflict, insufficient rest or attack is **paused with an observable reason**, not marked completed or looped with infinite retries. A hired person cannot be an unbreakable fully unattended 336-hour factory. If an employee is attacked/afraid, they may interrupt work, flee and change consent using real event memory.

**Offline model:** Event-driven replay with source timestamps: consumption / station timer / crop readiness / guard perception events and environmental hazards ordered deterministically, with each fund-and-produce receipt once. When the engine cannot prove distant travel, new enemy contact or safety, mark the specific action PENDING/UNRESOLVED, do not fabricate success, a dead villager or a magical safe home. The player returns to an observable report 'produced 4 meals, then stopped—fuel empty at time T', not an unsolicited login gift.

## 16. B3: NPC as neutral combat-capable humans, valuables and durable vendetta

**Creator clarification 2026-11-16:** NPC should fundamentally feel like **neutral combat-capable world actors**: the player and an NPC can both attack, wound or kill each other under the same physical combat principles used for hostile creatures; unlike beasts, NPCs may also talk, trade, recruit or participate in a community depending on identity. Valuable equipment can be physically owned by someone far stronger than the player. The price or requested barter goods for that equipment may be difficult to obtain, and the owner can refuse to trade; stealing or defeating the owner is a genuine alternative with genuine danger and **lasting, locally grounded revenge**. Do not reduce this to a scripted 'steal dialogue', a player karma meter, a universal pardon button or a safe deterministic robbery minigame. The rest of §16 defines engineering safeguards for this simple gameplay model.

**Allowed player actions:** Direct weapon strike, projectile, AoE/Core effect on a legally hittable NPC target, threat/intimidation when physically in reach with credible equipped weapon, item theft from a physically accessible NPC-owned object/container, and robbery of surrendered/captured **carried** possessions. These are actions inside K1/K5 combat and K1 custody, not new loot-chest classes. An armed intimidation attempt may be rejected, trigger flight/combat or lead to relinquishing existing carried items depending on threat, cover, nearby help and NPC condition; no forced 100% surrender, free item or fabricated dialogue reward. Theft of unattended property is visible if discovered; the actor is not magically notified over the whole map.

**Commerce and rarity:** NPCs differ by real personal role, equipment, trading willingness, actual stock and valuation. A merchant may own an excellent item and ask for uncommon, physically obtainable payment or refuse because it is their only weapon/essential tool. An ordinary villager is not required to be a merchant, and a recruitable guard does not owe their rare gear to a friendly conversation. Valuation, buyer affordability, barter eligibility and willingness must be player-legible, consistent with existing item ownership and the 157-item economy; do not conjure new barter currencies or force every named valuable item to be tradeable. The same physical high-value gear has one authoritative UID whether kept, sold, stolen, dropped after death or recovered later.

**Property rights (critical economy split):** Track explicit ownerId, location and custody for (a) on-person equipment/pack; (b) authored home/settlement storage or shop stock; (c) world G1B chests; (d) restricted SPECIAL_AUTHORITY cash/cards/Elder stones/escrow sources. **Killing an NPC can drop their actual personal owned gear in exactly one persistent recoverable bag**, and only what actually belonged to them. A vendor's abstract current vendorStock balance is NOT a secret carried backpack that spills in full on death; migrate actual stock to a secured, physically present shop container/ledger before allowing theft of it. Locked storeroom access requires real breach/door permissions; any legitimately stolen stock depletes the **same source budget** as sales and remains at most one copy. Do not automatically place 157 items or unlimited currency on bodies.

**Never violate special-source policy:** Ragnarok escrow/purchase pathway, profile secured cash, run-unique cards and Core odds are not downgraded to 'kill trader for free key/Core/escrow'. They are only physically stealable when the exact authored owner/source and special authority independently authorize the item, with source UID consumed once. A corpse cannot recreate the vendor's Ragnarok Cash sales inventory or bypass a required profile transaction. Ordinary G1B chests stay separate; nearby NPC presence alone does not secretly change approved chest loot/access.

**Reaction and proportionality:** An NPC actually attacked may defend itself using equipped weapon and ammunition, flee via legitimate routes, surrender goods, call for help if perceived/able, hide, refuse trade or decline recruitment. **NPC combat power is not scaled down to ensure the robbery is winnable**: skills/equipment/health/terrain/cover/companions can make an NPC stronger than the player and realistically kill them. Other people react only if they see/hear a real event, see a victim's injury, receive an actual local warning or investigate evidence. Guards choose physical interception/protection/retreat; they are not immortal police or a PvP faction with spawned squads. Direct attacks have priority over work. Avoid aggression loops where NPC and player no longer have true mutual detection.

**Memory:** Store bounded incident facts (victim, aggressor identity when known, witnesses, time, place, property/source IDs, injury/death, recovery/restitution). No global red/green morality meter, faction XP or fast amnesia after scene reload. Theft, threat, serious injury and murder must produce meaningfully different local consequences. A victim of real injury is not obliged to join the attacker. A frightened recruited resident may refuse commands, abandon dangerous home or leave via a real route after a witnessed act. Any possible reconciliation must arise from a **separate authored, causally meaningful event**; giving a random gift or waiting one hour never automatically removes an assault/murder record. See §21 for the remaining discretionary reconciliation boundary.

**Damage, downing and death:** Human HP/status/hitbox use the established actor-combat contracts, weapon wear/ammo and credible cover; block attacks through walls, closed doors, roofs and different floors. **Defeat does not automatically mean death.** Specify a real downed/incapacitated state with injury, possible bleeding/recovery/aid, limited action and the existing personal-item custody. A seriously wounded opponent may remain lying on the ground as a living survivor, be rescued or treated by their community, and remember the attacker. Downed humans cannot magically stand, pursue or call allies without an actual recovery/communication event. Lethal damage after a valid damage/recovery threshold can cause final in-run death, which no new NPC instance replaces. The body/gear bag gets exactly one custody transfer, is persistent across windows and reload, and belongs to source person. Other residents do not instantly clone the killed worker. Hostility does not grant extra ordinary rations, Core levels, special cards or money.

**Revenge is a principal gameplay consequence, not an optional dialogue refusal:** A directly attacked/wounded/robbed survivor who lives forms a **persistent retaliatory intent**, and allies who actually witness, discover the injury or receive an actual report can form their own response. A survivor may (1) become hostile when the player later crosses their genuine home/work/patrol territory, (2) set a watch/ambush on a route the survivor legitimately knows the player uses, or (3) **physically track the player out of the encounter, discover the player's home and attack that base**, alone or with actual informed allies. Different personalities, injury, available weapons, group support, danger and travel path can produce different strategies. Retaliatory intent survives save/window changes/player death; the **specific attack occurs only if the victim is capable, knows where to go and can physically arrive**. For an NPC with a community, discovered assault can lead to treatment, a watch and a finite local reprisal; for a solitary NPC, there may be a solo ambush/pursuit after recovery. A dead NPC cannot take revenge personally; living comrades may if they learn of the act. Revenge that cannot yet reach a target remains a persistent *investigation/pursuit intent*, not an invented hit or automatic absolution.

**Pursuit and base finding MUST reuse K5 trace/observation epistemology:** an NPC learns the player's current position from real line-of-sight/hearing or a **concrete footprint/track on a substrate they can detect**, with age, weather loss, interruption and local search; a known route may justify a believable wait. NPCs do not know player coordinates, construction positions or online/offline state by reading RunState. Discovering a base requires actually following credible evidence/route or having observed it earlier. Chasing to the base can result in direct NPC-vs-player/resident combat and damage to **reachable, destructible structures** only when the NPC has the actual weapon/tool/intent/capability; base turrets and resident guards can defend with real ammo and line-of-sight. Combat difficulty comes from existing actors, their gear and terrain, not new spawned raid waves.

**No synthetic consequences:** No random 'villager anger raid', hostile NPC teleport to player's home, global heat timer, guards omniscient to an unseen crime, mob waves after theft, permanent crime jail that locks solo progression, or forced quest. A pursuing NPC must have true information, travel and real line of attack. Player death does not reset witnessed history within the same run; finalize erases run-local crime ledger together with residents under K8.
