## A. INVARIANT: ONE PERSON, NOT EIGHT CASTES
Each human owns one run-unique actorId, sourceId, global position/floor, body/HP/stamina/wounds, occupation proficiency, actual weapon/armor/Core UIDs, pack/cart, relevant observed knowledge, persistent stable disposition, trust/relationship/consent, group affiliation, short finite incident ledger, active job/command and one physical death/downed state. Occupation does NOT set a fixed damage/HP multiplier; 80/95/90/110/105/125 HP physical archetypes are SOH_PROVISIONAL, not mandatory jobs. Vendor/raider/traveler are social/activity overlays. Unique actor cannot be projected twice by floor/window.

## B. TALK, HELP, TRUST, RECRUIT
Existing timed aid charges real player supplies at valid 1.5s food/water or 6s treatment completion and grants bounded first meaningful trust evidence (in fixture, 50→53→56). Preserve rollback on movement, no double reward, no credit for self-inflicted victim harm.
Implement natural exposure: Talk gives limited observed facts about needs/work/place, not omniscience; Help actions are available only when actual person needs them and item is owned/usable/reachable. Actual defense/medicine, meaningful paid repair and beneficial barter can contribute documented bounded evidence; repeated talk/unchanged swaps cannot farm Trust. Maintain actual initial 50 and consent threshold 55 where law applies, plus risk/community duties. Recruitment must verify actor consent, viable route/home conditions where required, never auto-grant items or ownership of entire community. The player can simply leave without forced quest.

## C. MULTI-ITEM ATOMIC BARTER (CURRENT SOURCE IS ONLY TWO STACKS)
Current HumanPanel selects one player stack and one NPC stack, evaluateHumanExchange computes relative value and a textual counter-offer, exchangeHumanStacks swaps exact complete stacks. Required finished API: proposal includes multiple (containerId, UID, qty) for EACH side, per-UID quantities within current owned available stock, no duplicate line, capacity/weight/rare-source checks, context-dependent valuation/acceptable reserve, actual NPC counterproposal as structured editable offer, and explicit final confirmation.
Transaction algorithm:
1. Generate immutable proposal ID and snapshot source inventory revisions, actor/location/floor, live trader eligibility, all UID+quantities and price contexts.
2. Preview cost/value/need/acceptance; no mutation.
3. NPC can accept/reject/counter with executable stack+quantity offer; avoid fabricated item lines, credit/debt or omniscient access to community/shop stock. Merchants' shop, carried pack and community warehouse are distinct custodians.
4. Commit validates proximity, both still alive/consenting/not fighting, all current UIDs/charges/condition, carrying weight/slots and all rights. Stage transfers into a single atomic authoritative transaction, remove source quantity before destination grant; exact rollback of slots/UID on any failed debit or insufficient capacity. Splitting stack requires fresh UID with parent/source provenance and conservation; full stack keeps original UID.
5. One durable event receipt with both sides and final quantities, no partial trade on failure; repeated command ID/reload not double transfer. Trade Trust evidence earned only for genuinely beneficial distinct transactions per approved policy.
Negative cases: item taken after preview, damaged Core swapped, full pack, multiple items same source, stale quote, concurrent robbery, disconnect/reload between acceptance and commit, human DOWNED, wrong floor or out of reach.

## D. CONFRONTATION / ROBBERY / CRIME
Actual demand targets: back off, disarm, stop attack, surrender specifically accessible carried/equipped UID(s), conditional negotiation. Victim decision from observed credible weapon, relative risk, personal violence policy/protected group, memory and escape route; respond COMPLY/NEGOTIATE/REFUSE/FLEE/CALL_HELP/FIGHT. The world must execute the response: release goods by exact custody, flee using physical route, fight under shared combat, summons only after real heard/seen incident, or hold ground.
Record ONE harm/theft incident per actual committed event with stable ID and witness knowledge based on floor, real LOS or meaningful hearing/noise source; grievance references original event, not duplicate assault. No global wanted counter or psychic tracking. Victim, allies and retaliators may prepare, physically find an observed/known player trail, approach legal world road, breach only legitimate access, reach and physically steal only accessible player-owned goods; they may fail, be killed, retreat or lose track. Person/supply/incident memory persists across travel/reload. No spawn-teleport raids or automatic enemy waves. Protect solo freedom; hostile humans can initiate encounters by genuine perception/disposition, not obligatory quests.
Negative: witness behind wall never remembers; attacker dies before raid; protected door/power gate refuses entry; base theft blocked by no capacity; object already moved is not stolen; same assault produces one incident.

## E. COMPANION WILL AND SEVEN COMMANDS
Approved commands: Follow / Hold / Defend / Engage / Retreat / Return Home / Carry. Five results: OBEY / REFUSE_WITH_REASON / PROTECT_TARGET / RETREAT_OR_HOLD / CANNOT_EXECUTE.
Existing resolveOrder removed order-ID-derived roulette and checks observed target, protected group, known aggression, HP/stamina/rest and ammo; preserve persistent decisions and stable semantics for equivalent orders. Be aware current commandHuman('carry') maps to basic follow with cargo controls and is NOT yet a complete carrying/logistics command; 'defend' currently creates a fight intent and does not by itself prove guarding behavior. A declared result must translate to actual action OR explicit no-action reason.
- Follow: traverse real stairs, windows, doors and hazards at actual speed/LOS/capacity; no camera snap.
- Hold: cancel work/movement; stay on reachable physical tile, still respond to self-defense/need.
- Defend: protect identified position/person through perception/interposition and shared K5; don't blindly initiate unprovoked attacks.
- Engage: identify actual visible known target, moral consent/refusal, legal route, turn, telegraph/strike with ammo and contact; no retaliation-free harm.
- Retreat: select reachable safer tile based on perceived threats, no teleport.
- Return Home: path to legitimate home/bed, end expedition only on actual arrival; resume work from new state and real supplies.
- Carry: transfer legal stacks/cargo by UID/quantity to companion/cart, enforce weight and space, follow real expedition/delivery location, unload through legal destination; after death/robbery cargo is one physical custody chain.
One companion cannot work at home while traveling. Downed/dead actor can't execute commands or produce output. On player ordinary death or disconnection, independent humans persist, do not vanish.

## F. ONE SHARED K5 COMBAT AUTHORITY
Baseline human melee has K5 reach/arc/floor/cadence/wear, and some direct human-target damage. Current stepHumanCombat around lines 1907–1971 treats Bow as ammo debit plus direct HP strike; this is NOT a physical projectile/collision implementation. Live player/creature projectile/hazard handlers already exist in sim.ts; adapt owner/hit eligibility to human actors and pass correct source/UID. No shadow NPC combat engine, especially not duplicated Elder HP.
Canonical attack phases: DETECT (actual LOS/hearing/knowledge) → SELECT (self, ally, community, player hostile) → APPROACH/REPOSITION through lawful graph → FACE + TELEGRAPH → WINDUP → PROJECTILE or HITBOX CONTACT at actual time → K5 damage, armor, knockback/status → recovery/cooldown → new decision (retreat, defend, chase, surrender, reload/rest).
Player→NPC, NPC→player, NPC→NPC, NPC→creature, creature→NPC, guard/companion, projectile and Core AoE must obey same collision, floor, LOS, timing, fixed source and one damage receipt per contact. No attacks through doors, no repeated creature swing multiple damage, no Bow arrow disappearing directly into target; arrow/projectile may miss/strike prop/hostile/friend as shared K5 permits, charges one ammo UID quantity only on genuine launch. Core uses actual owned Core UID, energy/essence, level, cooldown, wear and attack source; no free Core for NPC. Equipment durability and stamina affect outcomes, no invulnerable merchants.
Melee only if proper range and arc, turned toward target. Telegraphed reaction visible at 640×360; animation frames are projections of actual damage timeline, not invented delayed damage.
DOWNED remains same human/body/floor/pack, alive and treatable/robbable. Timed bleeding window from actual wound conditions, treatment with medicine, repeat-safe recovery, lethal follow-up creates exactly one corpse bag of carried property, not shop/warehouse. The entire 336h offline clock must respect harm and treatment chronology.
Negative combat: wrong floor, occluded LOS, empty arrows, broken weapon, protected ally, Bow missed, dead attacker, same hit repeated after reload, Core lacking energy, wounded retreat refusal, patient dies during treatment.
