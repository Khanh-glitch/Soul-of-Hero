## 2. Population placement — verified geometry, no invented census

G1B's `G1B_SPACE_CENSUS.json` records **81 distinct POIs / 811 space records / 145 road segments**. The updated `NPC_81_POI_PHYSICAL_AUDIT_SEED.csv` enumerates **every site and measured G1B spatial metadata**, with region-use candidate classes. It does **not** infer an authorized human, usable bed, water station or certified NPC path.

Illustrative groups from actual POI archetype only: 10 village/hamlet community candidates; 12 port/pier coast-work candidates; 18 industrial-work candidates; 10 research/observatory/facility expedition candidates; 5 roadstops; 6 Elder/tomb risk zones; 5 ruin sites; 6 Ragnarök cache sites, 2 proposed vendor shells and 3 non-operational evacuation assembly points. These counts are not population numbers, ratings or guaranteed live services. `rest48.ts` explicitly marks both vendor shells *proposals* and evac shells without transport. `arch/sites.ts` places several real graphical `bed` props and `well` props (Greywater, Hook, Mirage), not real authorized NPC beds/water access.

**Population implementation rule:** make initial per-run source IDs by actual habitable building and available lawful food/water/work/carry/storage/route. Assign generic `region × role × gear × small stable traits`, not biography. If a region needs an actual bed/station, create/activate a legitimate world object and validate its physical site before minting people. Isolated ruins may have none. New arrivals from finite origin tokens, valid route and travel time; no automatic profession replacement. Player can kill/rob/recruit/ignore in legitimate neutral PvE.

## 3. Needs economics — measured raw baseline, not Creator-signed balancing

Current `balance.v1.json`: `BAL.player.offlineHungerPerHour=4.1666666667`, `offlineThirstPerHour=6.25` (seasonal water multipliers apply). `catalog.ts`: raw Sunroot = +22 Hunger / +6 Thirst, `crop` plot returns 3 Sunroots + same seed, Water Flask sip = +30 Thirst, cooked Root Stew = +45 Hunger/+20 Thirst. Over a full **336 REAL HOURS**, an adult at baseline demands around **1,400 Hunger** and **2,100 Thirst points** (minus the finite starting stat reservoir; adjusted for climate/actions). If supplied solely by Sunroot, ~64 tubers/person/336h, requiring ~22 three-tuber harvests; drink only via flask charge needs 70 uses/person/336h (ignoring Sunroot hydration). Six such people roughly demand 384 Sunroots/~128 harvests and 420 separate drink charges over a full run, before climate and work — a *capacity example*, not an approved NPC population or source grant. Never translate 24-minute fictional dawn to a full day's ration.

For EACH actual human community `supplyBudget=starting_custody+physically_paid_production+verified_trade_in-actual_consumption-exports-loss`. Every WaterNode/FarmStation requires actual position, global tile/floor, source and state; do not assume `well` decorative prop yields water or source all 81 communities. A robust sample should test drought, frozen water, broken store, loss of worker, visitor eating shared provisions, route interdiction, and real-time idle/offline food output once.

## 4. Concrete minimum-architecture decisions (Control Tower, not new hidden lore)

**One authoritative ledger:** `run.humanWorld?: { version:'human-world-v1', sourceLayout:1, people:Record<actorId,Human>, communities:Record<id,Community>, arrivals:Record<sourceId,Arrival>, evacuation:Record<transportId,Trip>, elderPressure:Record<elderId,Assault>, events:Record<eventId,HumanEvent>, lastResolvedAt:number }`. Avoid separate actor clones in `r.entities` and `veyraOffscreenEntities`. Project only into the active window; K5 and UI may adapt the projection for targeting. Human source IDs and item UIDs remain globally unique. Never add a user-facing NPC item that behaves like a pet inventory stack.

**Commit order:** sort all deterministic intent/event boundaries by `(realTime,priority,sourceId,eventId)`; time-unit keyed (do not use fictional day for food). Reserve legal item inputs, path/permissions/worker, consume at genuine commit, create owned output once. No duplicated work while in party or sleeping. `lastProcessedAt` is a high-water mark, and exact receipts prevent replay on reload.

**NPC social AI is deliberately small:** 1) survive current danger/bleeding, 2) defend self/protected group, 3) obtain lawful food/water/rest, 4) complete committed job/transport/party order, 5) routine local idle. Risk/caution and group ties can modify priorities, but no bespoke persona/romance/quest or global karma currency.

**Elder worldwide danger:** six real Primordial source IDs; each with unique alive/defeated state, current global place, h312 awakening, a route/physical range/influence, event timestamp and one damage authority. Local visual/VFX uses native host; unloaded zone resolves only causally supported route, contact, armor, station, wall/HP and medical effects with the same damage semantics. Each threatened district becomes exposed according to real distance, route, time and Elder type, not one random attack per POI. Boss defeat cancels *future* events for that source, not retroactive past casualties. Any unresolved geometry remains a visible technical blocker; it cannot be silently treated as protected.

**Transport and player endpoint:** three currently statically marked evac sites become genuine service locations only after the transport/vehicle/boarding model is implemented and visually represented. A single departure law handles humans and the player: `board(actorId,tripId)` checks location/floor/seat/consent/carrying; `depart(tripId)` is a unique receipt, marks human EVACUATED and removes them from the threatened island; **does not end player's run** unless the player actually departs. For the player, call existing `endRun('evacuated')` only after lawful departure. Solo player never needs NPC help; no magical last-72h unlock unless a source states it.

**Outcome categories and reporting:** exactly one terminal `EVACUATED`, `SURVIVED_ON_ISLAND` (proved alive at h336 after the final assault interval), `DEAD` or `UNRESOLVED` per human per run. Healing/housing/recruiting is NOT terminal rescue. At early player evacuation/abandonment, those who remained in-world and lack final fate remain UNRESOLVED. Keep `assistedByPlayer` receipts separately from final survival, without a false counterfactual assertion that every living village member was personally rescued. Expose people alive, departed, dead, unknown, and people directly helped; no automatic leader score or Ragnarok Cash. Preserve only approved summary history across K8 wipe.

**Old-run compatibility decision:** old saves without `humanWorld` remain LEGACY_NO_HUMANS rather than suddenly receiving full initial population/stock many hours into a run. Their original solo/player game remains playable; new runs get seeded source-authorized humans. If a separately designed one-time legacy activation is desired later, it must be a known-source migration without retroactive 336h simulation or fabricated deaths.
