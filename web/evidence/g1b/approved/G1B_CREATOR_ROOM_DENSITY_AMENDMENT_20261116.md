# G1B CREATOR AMENDMENT — Room-aware and route-aware chest ecology (2026-11-16)

**Authority:** direct Creator change after approving G1B V1 and after reviewing initial 130-source runtime. **Applies immediately to the current G1B corrective batch.** Supersedes the previous **hard placement census / quotas** (85 POI + 26 ROAD + 19 CARD_ROOM = 130). The 130 manifest is a historical candidate/compatibility baseline, NOT a final ceiling, target, or accepted density. Do NOT misinterpret this amendment as approval to alter the already-approved 16 family/grade loot tables, item drop rates, quantity rules, 14 Core BOMs, per-grade refresh cycles, or Creator Core odds.

> Creator intent: evaluate **EACH ACTUAL ROOM/SPACE**, not POIs as one container. A POI may have outdoor grounds, scattered single rooms, multi-room and multi-storey buildings, externally accessible upper floors and rooftop areas. Ordinary rooms in the Last Island experience often already contain a chest; valuable secure rooms may contain several valuable chests. Roads feel underpopulated with only 26 scattered sources; the Creator's Last Island reference is roughly 100 road chests as an experiential benchmark, **not a mandatory numeric quota**. The agent shall independently compute appropriate density from geometry, danger, route difficulty and how the world is played.

## 1. Replace POI quotas with individual physical lootable-space inventory

Inspect the **current compiled runtime Veyra world**, not only the GOLD map POI list or one centroid per site. For each authored POI, recursively inventory its accessible places: open yard/courtyard, open-air sheds/porches, detached huts, building ground floor, individual rooms and storage nooks, corridors/landings, interior stairs, separate upper floors, exposed mezzanines, roof decks reached from outside or inside, basement/deep rooms, gates and the route out. **A single POI is not one loot area; a multi-floor POI can have many independent lootable rooms.**

Create a per-space manifest with stable IDs: `poiId / buildingId / floorId / roomId-or-yardId`; record footprint/bounds, actual floor/height layer, usable tile count, contents/function, existing chests and other props, safety/cover, danger, difficulty, required door/card/power/PPE/height traversal, reachable ingress and egress, and physical evidence. **Do not invent nonexistent rooms or claim a higher floor exists because the GOLD metadata says multi.** Exterior elevated platforms and walkable roofs must be considered in their own level/space, not skipped because they are outdoors.

The investigator owns a transparent, documented scoring mechanism for **room-specific** challenge and loot value: e.g. room area and realistic storage capacity; distance/travel cost from safe approaches; enemy/hazard exposure and sightlines; actual gating expense/sequence; vertical traversal/fall risk; strategic utility and environmental story. Scores are a design aid, NOT a bot substitution for user enjoyment.

**Room allocation policy:** a meaningful ordinary room with storage opportunities should ordinarily contain at least one sensible container; small/empty/transit/non-storage spaces may contain none and require an explicit reason. Valuable/large/carefully guarded/secure rooms can hold multiple, potentially 3–5 high-grade containers where physical capacity and risk justify it. Neither every room gets one nor every elite room gets five automatically. A room with one chest and an equally meaningful neighbouring room with none must be explainable.

Select family and grade by **what the room is**, not only global difficulty: workshop → Toolbox, kitchen/pantry → Food, clinic/infirmary → Medical, warehouse/roadside supply → Supply, barracks/guardhouse/secure armoury → Military, with contextual mixtures. Preserve low/mid/high tiers and 16 legal combinations; ensure meaningful high rewards never appear in a trivially reachable outdoor corner by accident. Do not spawn any source in collision walls, doors, stairs, escape corridors, actor/workbench interaction footprints, or where it blocks the player's access to a structure.

## 2. Reassess *every* traversable road and route segment, not 11 sampled roads

Old 26 ROAD sources spread across a limited route subset are **not a defensible final representation of Veyra**. Inventory the actual playable road/trail/bridge/waystation/dock-edge network, classify passability, traffic, settlements, wrecks, salvage evidence, route danger, terrain and travel distance; then distribute containers across the actual journey. The Creator's approximate hundred roadside chests from Last Island is a **qualitative density reference, not a target, cap, or required integer**. Do not place a uniform grid of boxes, one every N tiles, or inflate counts just to hit 100. Prefer plausible roadside contexts (broken carts, camps, wrecked supply drops, checkpoints, bridges, pullouts, abandoned vehicles, freight loading bays, footpaths) and coherent clusters/quiet stretches so exploration yields regular but non-mechanical finds. **Ensure roads that cross a POI do not double-count POI interior loot as roadside**; assign each source to one actual space and explain ambiguous border crossings.

Make a **route-experience audit** a player can understand: per route/road segment, length and traversable distance, intended chest encounters, journey-to-encounter intervals, source categories and risk levels; note notable dead zones and repetitive clusters. Human player, not a long simulated bot, judges whether the roads feel worth exploring.

## 3. Physical access, verticality and card rooms

Keep Green/Blue/Purple card-room identities and the previously approved 6/6/7 compositions as **layout content candidates**, subject to genuine room size, vertical access, gate custody, power and PPE. Preserve finite card cases. A room is NOT 'secure' merely because a door has a code-side intent: prove no bypass via the **actual stairs, roof, exterior window/landing, adjacent floors** and that a player can leave without softlock. For every locked room, separately list which exact gate the chest is behind and prove path with and without authorization; avoid fake blocked-door proofs or teleport-to-room as gameplay evidence.

The previous report cites **nine geometry variances**, internal stairs in three gated rooms and road-over-POI overlaps; resolve or explicitly measure these against actual play movement and architecture. If a room is unreachable, fix physical routing without deleting it to make tests green. If the renderer only shows an upper floor from outside, establish whether it is *walkable and interactable* before giving it inventory.

## 4. Preserve contracts while changing population: no silent economy explosion

**Do NOT alter:** 16 chest grade-family definitions; 219 approved positive item odds rows; each grade's per-item/qty rules; mutually exclusive Core-type/level outcomes; 12–72 h per-grade real-time restock; no pity, no Core cap; partial-loot persistence; explicit 7 Creator `BOTH` and 4 Field separate; 14 upgrade BOMs and Core V2 300/+75. These remain Creator-approved.

Adding world sources **changes item/energy/Core throughput even when per-chest odds remain exactly the same**. Compute before/after expected output and accessible progression, grouped by difficulty/room type, biome and real-life refill cycle, with separate common/medium/rare Core expectations. Identify easy-loot clusters that would undermine crafting, risks and high-grade scarcity. Fix by relocating/adjusting number/grade/access contextuality, **not by silently changing item odds or introducing hidden source caps/pity.** If data proves the approved odds and increased source density are fundamentally incompatible, clearly present that conflict to Control Tower/Creator for a separate specific decision; never quietly solve it.
