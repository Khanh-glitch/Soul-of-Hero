**Art.** No third-party sprites or tiles are used. Every sprite, tile texture, icon and VFX is original pixel art generated by `src/game/art.ts` and `src/game/render.ts`.

**Audio.** The sound files in `public/sfx/` (489 Ogg Vorbis files, 8.9 MB) are cut from 235 recordings in the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles, by 26 sound makers. Slots without a file still use the sound synthesized at runtime by `src/game/audio.ts`.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Source | Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles (the owner's copy on Google Drive). https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc |
| License | "The Sonniss #GameAudioGDC Bundle Licensing Agreement" (`License - GDC Game Audio.pdf`, shipped with the bundles) |
| What it allows | Worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free use in an unlimited number of projects, personal and commercial; the sounds may be modified; no attribution is required; use in games is named explicitly. |
| What it forbids | Selling the sounds as they come (they may be sold only as part of the project); modifying a sound in order to claim authorship of the recording; using the sounds to train or develop artificial intelligence technologies. |
| Copyright | Stays with each sound maker. |
| What was done to the sounds | Each file is a cut of one recording: trimmed, faded, sometimes filtered, slowed or reversed, levelled, mixed to mono (beds stay stereo) and encoded to Ogg Vorbis. Ordinary signal processing (`scripts/sfx-build/make.py`); no model was trained on them and nothing was generated from them. |
| Per-file record | `docs/sfx/NGUON_AM_THANH.md` lists, for every file, the recording, the library and the maker. `public/sfx/sources.json` carries the same recording names for the sound test board. |
| Not in this repository | The original WAV recordings. They stay in the owner's library. |

Attribution is not required by the license; the makers are listed anyway, in `docs/sfx/NGUON_AM_THANH.md`.

**Candidates considered but not adopted.** NinjaAdventure, Kenney, simplex-noise, easystar and idb were not used. Instead, the build has its own seeded value noise, A* for roads and creatures, and a raw IndexedDB wrapper. That was enough for the needs here and kept the art style in one consistent hand.
