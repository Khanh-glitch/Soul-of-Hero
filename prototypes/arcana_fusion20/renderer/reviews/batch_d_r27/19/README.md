## Causal lead (analytic, not isolated pixel proof)

Original standalone `FLOOR_FS` explicitly calls the `w.w * 0.10 * sin(vUV.y * 180 + uTime * 8)` term **heat shimmer banding**. The native live-world shader carries that term onto the much brighter actual terrain, and `DFx.bend` accumulates the authored finale's `chroma = 1.4 * su` into a decaying float field. Under the ideal center-pixel recurrence (one fixed step, decay `exp(-3.4/60)`, peak falloff 1), the accumulated alpha is approximately 21.0 at 8.15s and 24.5 at 8.6s, so its sinusoidal multiplier amplitude is approximately 2.1 and 2.45 respectively, before OUT fade. The original renderer uses additive float warp buffers and source dark floor; this estimate is not a measured pixel and does not isolate light/post or prove native/source parity. It does identify a specific source-authored operation and nonlinear bright-world interaction worth a same-frame shimmer-off/on/off probe; do not silently delete the source term or assert that its removal is a faithful correction.
