## World MASTERPLAN V0 (functional, NON-SPATIAL proposal)
**Deliberately do NOT choose fixed 11 regions, 22 biome, 118 POIs, 1024×768 or historical Last Island 8000×8000 as design requirements.** A1 pinned world is implementation source/legacy preservation and optional baseline, not creative authority. New geography requires creator sign-off, A0 migration and G3 source/native gate.

**Seven roles across one connected continent (not seven mandatory regions):**
1. Multiple independent genuinely safe settlement/home ecosystems each with differentiated soil/road/water/resource/risk and return loops.
2. Connected roadside salvage and fixed trader/recycle/deposit node network, road hierarchy and alternate routes.
3. Stone/ore/quarry/industry belt from geology, tool wear and production logistics.
4. Crop/orchard/riverside/farm valley, water, gatherable medicine/food and later offline return.
5. Alpine snow, boreal cold, mountain pass with protection and alternate exposed short vs sheltered long crossing.
6. Wet/poison mangrove, karst and volcanic/heat other hazard ecologies with actual preparation/access/crafting reasons.
7. Six **non-identical** deep Elder territory clusters and physical multistage POI/escape/custody connectors, scattered by plausible world history, not all at center or all behind identical level gates.

**Route network**: road main loop/spine with multiple parallel side routes, cart/bridge/boat freight shortcuts, real POI rooms and retreat; no single mandatory spawn or gated one-lane story. Distance must be measured **effective round trip** (time + load + fuel + danger + tool wear + knowledge value + exit risk), not straight line length.

**POI footprint classes**: roadside micro, local utility Waystation/merchant/Orchard, regional Quarry/industry/port, multistage power/key/lab, six pre-/post-awakening elder zones. Each POI needs approach/exit/clearance, physical interaction/source/colliders, low-level outer reward, later preparation requirement, lore and reason to revisit.
