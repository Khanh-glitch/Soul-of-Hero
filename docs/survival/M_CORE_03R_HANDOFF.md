## Economy and risks — assumptions vs observations

**Provisional/TUNABLE, not a balance approval.** Each car wreck has exactly 8 finite successful yields and 1 scrap each. Independent per-yield rolls on split policy: 0.30 direct Spare, 0.20 Drive. At recycle fraction `f`, expected Spares/yield `0.30 + 0.40f`, i.e. 0.30/0.50/0.70 for f=0/0.5/1, or 2.4/4.0/5.6 per exhausted wreck; Drives average 1.6 per wreck. Approximately 16.8% of wrecks yield no Drive (`0.8^8`), 5.8% no direct Spares (`0.7^8`), assuming the rolls are independent as implemented. Not guaranteed stock; losses, travel and output access lower realized f. Fallback averages 5.6 direct Spares/wreck, no new Drive. Other bonus rolls and finite scrap are not a renewable conversion loop.

Actual controlled cloned harvests: 400 wreck scenarios per policy / 3,200 yields each: split 980 Spares (30.625%), 645 Drives (20.156%), 3,200 scrap; direct 2,229 Spares (69.656%), no Drives, 3,200 scrap. Veyra U1.6/U2/Oasis projected source counts 24/19/14; 30 cloned finite samples each gave 240 scrap, 166 Spares, zero Drives. These are tests, not 400 player runs; all three use the fallback, not a Veyra Recycler proof.

| Choice | Real input and output | Opportunity cost and practical constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Public Recycler | 1 Drive → 2 Spares, 12 s, no fuel/base | Drive cannot also become a part. Pays for half of 4-Spare random research; directed Field Repair Kit costs 9, research table itself 6. Requires discoverable accessible POI, return journey, output space, enemy exposure. |
| Owned Lv1 Workbench | 1 Drive + 1 scrap → 1 part, 12 s | Forfeits 2 Spares; claim + bench cost 24 wood + 9 stone. Existing alternative 2 scrap + 1 ingot → 2 parts, 12 s: per part 1 scrap + half ingot but batch/ingot cost matters. |

A Drive weighs 3 and stacks to 1, while 2 Spares weigh 0.6 and stack to 60, and 1 part weighs 0.5 and stacks to 30: carrying before processing costs 2.4 or 2.5 extra weight and one slot per Drive; observed inventory had several single-Drive slots. Character carry limit is 60; cart 24 slots/240 weight. No actual cart trial. A no-base character can physically reach the public Recycler in the assisted shipping run but cannot claim a no-base research/Workbench loop. One can bypass Drive processing through remaining direct Spares, other sources, looted parts and the existing bench recipe, but that is not quantified for other starts or old/Veyra play. Real travel observed 49 assisted route steps / 9.974 s in one case; return/site routes 11 and 9 steps were local continuations, not independent runs. Combat was materially costly: repeated projectile deaths and bag-recovery trips; the base deliberately near the Foundry is unsafe, and stationary UI did not stop the world. No valid encounter-free/expected-time estimate or tuning change is inferred.
