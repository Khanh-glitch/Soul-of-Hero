## Verification and limits

- `web/tests/b2-chronology.test.ts`: 24 focused tests, including ordered multi-station results/UIDs and traces under long versus partitioned settlement; online versus offline autonomous work for the same dry interval; rain before/after/tied to burnout; roof and water exhaustion; furnace fuel/output-full and always-burning campfire; expiry and post-expiry command rejection; throttle, hitstop, and a transaction before the resumed frame; real paid reservations, save/Continue twice; offline survival boundaries; no offline fire spread/hits or wildlife; backwards clock; and 336 h bounded execution. All pre-existing 60 B1/B2-A/Gameplay tests are unchanged.
- `settle` uses at most 20,160 ordinary one-minute slices for 336 h, plus weather/finite resource, fire, terrain and survival event boundaries; it scans actual run entities/sources per slice. The 336 h state test completed in roughly 0.1–0.4 s in this sandbox with its test world; this is not a hard guarantee for arbitrarily large or hostile save data. It does not replay millions of 60 Hz frames.
- Same initial state and weather produce the same **implemented autonomous** station/garden/fire/trace outcomes and event times in the focused comparisons, within floating-point tolerance. Each real Continue may still add its own `offline.settle` receipt; those session receipts are not duplicate production. Online active fire spread/lightning, physical actors and their interactions intentionally differ from offline freeze; player offline drains intentionally use the existing offline factor, not online rates.
- `clock.simMs` remains the single simulation clock, now advances with wall time for autonomous deadlines even during hitstop; `lastSimAt` prevents replay. Older saves remain loadable with no schema change. Backwards device time is not replayed, but client wall time, storage, anti-rollback and cross-tab races are not trusted/verified.
- No long browser-bot campaign was run. Local unit/state tests, typecheck and build are evidence, not human play acceptance or exact-SHA CI. Existing dependency audit reported six vulnerabilities; dependencies were not changed.
