## 1. The COMPLETE locked SOH product you are protecting

SOH is a broad-authored **single-player top-down pixel survival action RPG** whose player can enjoy quiet, confident KNIFE-FIRST exploration without bosses, Core or compulsory quests. Distinct Male/Female Adventurer sprites must truly embody movement/combat; NOT the legacy Knight. A new run starts at broadly distributed random safe cells of one geographically stable continent, not a tiny predetermined picker. Roads/biomes/resources/river/settlements/POIs, weather, day/night, terrain speed, temperature/toxin/radiation and enemy physics produce geographic, economic and survival decisions.

The home is a **paid, physical, one-storey, room-based build**: foundation/wall/door/window, one contextually fading roof, bed, chest, stations, smelting/forge, garden, fuel/energy/comfort/traps, genuine footprint/nav/path/collision/storage efficiency, repair and realistic logistical outposts/cart/rare pets/mounts/transport when they improve physical routes/cargo. No abstract permanent Operation Base or prefab-icon-only house. Valid sheltered offline starvation may reach 1 HP but cannot kill solely from hunger; wilderness exposure risk is real, telegraphed and recoverable. Ordinary monsters can reach home only via actual surviving player **footprints/tracks**, not GPS knowledge. A death creates one recoverable bag; recovery and respawn happen within the same run.

Economy requires a source→inventory/carrying capacity→transformation→sink→future opportunity network, including low-risk roadside salvage and Spares, recipe vs physical blueprint vs actual item separation, **three knowledge methods** (bounded random research, targeted research, destructive specimen reverse engineering), complex competing intermediate input chains, wear/repair and fuel/queue costs. Research may make yesterday's saved junk valuable. The player decides which cargo to retain, where to stage supplies, how to fund work while offline and whether a planned trip is net-profitable after time, danger, tool wear and consumption.

A truly scarce **found physical finite-charge Skill Core** is usable IMMEDIATELY, distinct from knowing its recipe. USE vs STORE vs sacrifice for RESEARCH must all be meaningful, with unique ownership and atomic charge cost only for committed original-Gold effect. AVFX owns exact original top-down Gold skill: cast phases/timing/movement, caster/enemy/terrain/ground/ambient effects, contrast on bright maps, clean teardown. No substitute energy projectile, approximate redraw or showing only attractive emitters while ignoring authored skill mechanics. Different Core families should enable tactics, access and future plans rather than simply increase DPS.

POIs form layered physical discovery/access graphs: enter perimeter, find clue, then later credential/key, power source, hazard mitigation, alternative routes, optional enemy/boss, physical rooms, meaningful partial rewards, durable world change, repeat return; avoiding bosses/dungeons can still yield productive progress. The existing ten Chapter-1 sources are preserved as reusable sources without being compulsory survival-world checkboxes. Novice starter guidance is non-blocking, recognizes out-of-order actions, and is optional/reward-oriented on new runs.

**One run lasts at most 336 real UTC hours**, counts time offline, may end early by user abandon or physical evacuation. Ordinary death and app exit do not end run. There is an internal accelerated season/day-night/world calendar consistent across online/offline, but fictional clock-rate and resource rates require evidence-based tuning, not guesswork. Day1–11 supports many concurrent home/research/exploration/combat/logistics goals; after a long run, many exciting things remain *unseen*, not hidden behind artificial 100,000-click grind. A funded farm/forge/research completion on login is an earned payoff, with supplies and time consumed exactly once.

**Last 72 real hours:** days12–13 climate/hostile/territory awakening, day14 six DIFFERENT Elder forms whose weaker pre-event regions are already relevant; six unique run-local stones milestone1–6, optional exceptional six-stone capstone. They are not mandatory for normal success. Special rare final-72h **physical chests only** generate Ragnarok currency, subject to actual carrying/bag risk and legitimate repeatable physical fixed-site deposits before evacuation. Fixed vending/disassembly stations have real stock and item costs. A physical evacuation may conclude the run immediately under locked default; early abandon is a separate non-winning action. At any ending, **all run-local house, materials, gear, Core, learned recipe, POI state, crops, quests and unbanked money reset**; ONLY earned achievements, immutable run history and deposited secured Ragnarok currency survive to next run. Never allow score, boss defeat, recycling or summary screen to mint currency.

A first invited complete creator playtest is **E2**, with all admitted full-product routes present. B0/B1/B2 are internal development gates. A single short first-hour sample is neither the full run nor E2.
