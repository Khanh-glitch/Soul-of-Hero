## 1. Emotional/player promise — hard product gate
SOH is a SINGLE-PLAYER top-down pixel survival action RPG preserving the deep 2019–2020 Last Day Rules/Last Island BEHAVIORS (not its PvP/grief, monetization or every later patch), replacing found firearms with original-Gold-fidelity charged physical Skill Cores.
- **Quiet freedom is a primary success state.** A knife/basic-melee player can enjoy walking, roadside scavenging, POI perimeter, growing, building and ordinary exploration WITHOUT a Core, boss or forced quest.
- **The rare discovery moment matters:** finding a usable scarce Core should feel like unexpectedly acquiring a powerful gun, desirable to TRY right away. USING, STORING and researching a physical Core for RUN-LOCAL replacement knowledge are genuinely different choices. Do not require prior research to try a found Core.
- **A home is a meaningful place:** a physically constructed single-storey room, one simple fading roof, beds, storage, stations, farm, comfort, preparation, return and offline completion; not a menu/prefab icon.
- **Material, knowledge and geographic exploration progress reinforce each other.** Discover locked/high-hazard sites before being able to conquer them; prepare and revisit. An ordinary quiet session must yield valuable progress and a visible next goal.
- **World breadth beats fake grind.** After a typical ~14-real-day season much of the meaningful map/POI/rare Core/research remains untouched, but the player has experienced a FEW distinctive, delightful discoveries. Do not inflate playtime with timers, blank sites, mandatory repetitive farms, arbitrary level locks or scripted repetitive boss raids. Several independent aims remain enticing.
- **Offline return creates anticipation:** already-funded bounded farming, forging, research or other physical processes can finish while away. World chronology and weather are continuous; supplies/fuel/time genuinely limit outputs. Do not grant items for unprepared work.
- **No PvP, compulsory raids or unattended omniscient monsters.** Physical threats, footprints, terrain, hazard, distance, risk/recovery and advance warning create solo consequences without arbitrary grief.
- **Gold VFX is source-of-truth:** full original timing/particles/enemy/body/terrain effect, readable 720p/2K/4K; no imitative redraw or omitted environmental stages.
- **Full-E2 experience before FIRST creator playtest.** Internal engineering slices B0/B1/G-R are NOT the first invited full game.

## 2. World / player / POI / progression
- One causally authored broad continent designed as a complete world masterplan; stable geography between runs; varied biome/hazards, settlements, weather, rivers, resource economies. NEW RUN starts at a broad geographically distributed RANDOM SAFE walkable spawn; no lethal/hazard/hostile trapped spawn or tiny start picker. The player's chosen house location matters.
- Prior **ten** Chapter-1 dungeon sources are PRESERVED and may be adapted selectively; it is NOT mandatory to mount ten survival-world dungeons in the opening quarter. Never delete the reusable source/legacy test route, but never make obsolete 'exact ten' geography a product gate.
- Layered physical POIs and roads must yield real loot, recipes/keys, clues and usable routes. Power, credentials, real hazards/protection, collision and enemies govern deeper access. Non-boss roadside and perimeter scavenging remains rewarding. Pre-Ragnarok boss territories already exist as worthwhile dungeons with weaker manifestations/seals/loot.
- Two real user-selected starting Male/Female Adventurer player bodies (D051) are player avatars, not legacy Knight; different cosmetics do not require different gameplay performance.
- Real wall/door/roof/bed/chest/forge/research/garden/logistics, movable cargo cart and long-horizon meaningful rare pets/mounts/transport where geographic role warrants. Player-invoked bounded hostile challenges permitted; ordinary monsters may FOLLOW detectable unexpired PLAYER TRACKS to a home and then path/breach repairable structures, NEVER know home coordinates automatically.
- Physical Core instance/charges is NOT a learned run-local recipe, nor one 'skill registry entry'. Research pathways (random Spares research, directed research, specimen reverse engineering) have real prices and source identities. No generic unearned top-tier magic unlock. A1/A3/A4 must synchronize sources, materials, combat and entire original Gold effects.

## 3. Run lifecycle — 14-day maximum, optional early exit
- `run_start_utc` establishes a maximum of 1,209,600 elapsed real seconds (14 days); expiry continues offline. It is NOT a requirement to keep the application open or stay in a run for all 14 days.
- Player may voluntarily END/ABANDON any run at any point, with clear confirmation and disclosure of lost unbanked progress, then start a new run at a new safe random spawn. Manual abandon is NOT successful evacuation or a six-stone victory.
- Normal game exit merely closes the app, NOT the run; ordinary death respawns within the same run via owned bed/appropriate default and a single recoverable bag, NOT season end.
- On run closure, a single A0-authoritative atomic finalize archives immutable history/earned achievements; deletes/reinitializes all run gameplay: world/POI/boss/harvest state, structures, stations, crops, inventory, worn gear, physical Core, learned formulas/research, missions, map discovery and unbanked currency. The ONLY enduring product benefits: proven `profile.achievements`, `profile.run_history`, and **`profile.secured_ragnarok_currency`**. Do not invent persistent recipe, Core, base, permanent power attributes, inherited item, or season score conversion.
- Winning evacuation means the avatar physically LEAVES the island: **DEFAULT DESIGN LOCK:** the evacuation interaction itself FINALIZES the current run immediately after awarding only legitimately earned and physically collected evacuation treasure/custody; player can choose when to perform it during the eligible endgame, not be online at day-14 midnight. Pre-deposit can happen many times and NEVER forces ending the run. This evacuation-end rule is a product design decision made under creator's delegated authority, NOT a quoted direct vote; show this consequence clearly in UX.
- At expiry while offline, finalize ONCE on next legitimate resume/settlement with recorded end boundary; do not invent rewards or simulate fraudulent money after expiry. Voluntary abandon never grants evacuation reward.

## 4. Ragnarok 72h event and endings — HARD LOCK
- Ragnarok occupies the LAST three 24-hour real days: days 12–13 are awakening and regional escalating climate/hazard/monster changes; day 14 awakened elder bosses extend destruction into the island. World remains playable and causal, not a sudden arena swap or all POIs forced closed. In-world clock/day-night also runs offline.
- Six elder bosses have ordinary weaker sealed/manifestation encounter states before the event and DISTINCT fully-awakened forms in six deep territories; each guardian yields one unique **run-local stone**, each 1..6 stone milestone is meaningful; six stones is an exceptionally rare maximal achievement with a LARGE reward and a SPECIAL stone-based ending/action. Their physical stone instances wipe with run; confirmed milestones remain in achievement/history only.
- Stone conquest is OPTIONAL; NORMAL success can involve physically looting and securing money, and an independent, genuinely valuable EVACUATION objective. A player may accomplish multiple routes before departing. Do not force six kills or PvP. Boss-awakening is an ecosystem event not the only global apocalypse cause; killing boss does not halt the 14-day expiry.
- Six boss zones grow materially harder (weather/exposure/monster strength/mobility/damage) and richer; distinct mitigation/route/knowledge is required; NOT six reskinned HP gates. Boss destruction is along physical traversed paths, not an omniscient strike against homes. Offline Ragnarok effects require warnings, physical causal simulation, bounded damage and a feasible recovery path; no unavoidable entire-home erasure while app is closed.

## 5. Two-layer economy / immutable custody
- **Run-only:** items, Spares, research notes, bought ordinary items, crafting, refining, physical item recycling/disassembly. **Cross-run:** currency balance accumulated only after authenticated successful DEPOSIT of physically looted **Ragnarok currency**. Currency itself drops only from special rare POI CHESTS active during the last three days, with exceptional concentration in deep six-boss areas. Evacuation/stone rewards MUST be physically realizable through special Ragnarok vault/chest sources before custody, NEVER minted by a summary score or a free global balance reward.
- Physical currency obtained from chest occupies real inventory/cargo; death before deposit means potential bag recovery or loss. Home stash at run end does NOT secure it. The player may make MULTIPLE trips to the geographically fixed accessible secure deposit points and keep playing after each successful deposit. SaveService must own unique source IDs and atomic idempotent chest/transfer/deposit receipts; no reload/reroll/recycle farm. No double-debit/credit.
- After reset, secured balance remains in profile; it does NOT spawn inventory goods. Physical FIXED-LOCATION VENDING MACHINES and DISASSEMBLY/RECYCLING MACHINES have genuine trader/salvage roles. Player travels to vendors and chooses to spend or save; vending stocks, prices and progression safeguards protect exploration and rare Core surprise. No mandatory NPC merchant conversation; optional bounty boards/global market are NOT silently core.
- Escaping successfully gives distinct material value (rare evacuated-vault money, eligible secured carried cash), distinct immutable season outcome and story payoff, not just a free extra score. Six-stone path earns milestones 1–6 and an exceptional capstone access to rare physical treasure and separate narrative consequence; reward is earned through real actions.
