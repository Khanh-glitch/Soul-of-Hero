## 1. Emotional/player promise — hard product gate
SOH is an OFFLINE-FIRST SINGLE-PLAYER top-down pixel survival action RPG with an OPTIONAL ONLINE GLOBAL LEADERBOARD (no online requirement for ordinary survival) preserving the deep 2019–2020 Last Day Rules/Last Island BEHAVIORS (not its PvP/grief, monetization or every later patch), replacing found firearms with original-Gold-fidelity charged physical Skill Cores.
- **Quiet freedom is a primary success state.** A knife/basic-melee player can enjoy walking, roadside scavenging, POI perimeter, growing, building and ordinary exploration WITHOUT a Core, boss or forced quest.
- **The rare discovery moment matters:** finding a usable scarce Core should feel like unexpectedly acquiring a powerful gun, desirable to TRY right away. USING, STORING and researching a physical Core for RUN-LOCAL replacement knowledge are genuinely different choices. Do not require prior research to try a found Core.
- **A home is a meaningful place:** a physically constructed single-storey room, one simple fading roof, beds, storage, stations, farm, comfort, preparation, return and offline completion; not a menu/prefab icon.
- **Material, knowledge and geographic exploration progress reinforce each other.** Discover locked/high-hazard sites before being able to conquer them; prepare and revisit. An ordinary quiet session must yield valuable progress and a visible next goal.
- **World breadth beats fake grind.** After a typical ~14-real-day season much of the meaningful map/POI/rare Core/research remains untouched, but the player has experienced a FEW distinctive, delightful discoveries. Do not inflate playtime with timers, blank sites, mandatory repetitive farms, arbitrary level locks or scripted repetitive boss raids. Several independent aims remain enticing.
- **Offline return creates anticipation:** already-funded bounded farming, forging, research or other physical processes can finish while away. World chronology and weather are continuous; supplies/fuel/time genuinely limit outputs. Do not grant items for unprepared work.
- **No PvP, compulsory raids or unattended omniscient monsters.** Physical threats, footprints, terrain, hazard, distance, risk/recovery and advance warning create solo consequences without arbitrary grief.
- **Gold VFX is source-of-truth:** full original timing/particles/enemy/body/terrain effect, readable 720p/2K/4K; no imitative redraw or omitted environmental stages.
- **Full-E2 experience before FIRST creator playtest.** Internal engineering slices B0/B1/G-R are NOT the first invited full game.

## 3. Run lifecycle — 14-day maximum, optional early exit
- `run_start_utc` establishes a maximum of 1,209,600 elapsed real seconds (14 days); expiry continues offline. It is NOT a requirement to keep the application open or stay in a run for all 14 days.
- Player may voluntarily END/ABANDON any run at any point, with clear confirmation and disclosure of lost unbanked progress, then start a new run at a new safe random spawn. Manual abandon is NOT successful evacuation or a six-stone victory.
- Normal game exit merely closes the app, NOT the run; ordinary death respawns within the same run via owned bed/appropriate default and a single recoverable bag, NOT season end.
- On run closure, a single A0-authoritative atomic finalize archives immutable history/earned achievements; deletes/reinitializes all run gameplay: world/POI/boss/harvest state, structures, stations, crops, inventory, worn gear, physical Core, learned formulas/research, missions, map discovery and unbanked currency. The ONLY enduring product benefits: proven `profile.achievements`, `profile.run_history`, and **`profile.secured_ragnarok_currency`**. Do not invent persistent recipe, Core, base, permanent power attributes, inherited item, or season score conversion.
- Winning evacuation means the avatar physically LEAVES the island: **DEFAULT DESIGN LOCK:** the evacuation interaction itself FINALIZES the current run immediately after awarding only legitimately earned and physically collected evacuation treasure/custody; player can choose when to perform it during the eligible endgame, not be online at day-14 midnight. Pre-deposit can happen many times and NEVER forces ending the run. This evacuation-end rule is a product design decision made under creator's delegated authority, NOT a quoted direct vote; show this consequence clearly in UX.
- At expiry while offline, finalize ONCE on next legitimate resume/settlement with recorded end boundary; do not invent rewards or simulate fraudulent money after expiry. Voluntary abandon never grants evacuation reward.

## 4. Ragnarok 72h event and endings — HARD LOCK
- Ragnarok occupies the LAST three 24-hour real days: days 12–13 are awakening and regional escalating climate/hazard/monster changes; day 14 awakened elder bosses extend destruction into the island. World remains playable and causal, not a sudden arena swap or all POIs forced closed. In-world clock/day-night also runs offline.
- Six elder bosses have ordinary weaker sealed/manifestation encounter states before the event and DISTINCT fully-awakened forms in six deep territories; each guardian yields one unique **run-local stone**, each 1..6 stone milestone is meaningful; six stones is an exceptionally rare maximal achievement with a LARGE reward and a SPECIAL stone-based ending/action. Their physical stone instances wipe with run; confirmed milestones remain in achievement/history only.
- Stone conquest is OPTIONAL; NORMAL success can involve physically looting and securing money, and an independent, genuinely valuable EVACUATION objective. A player may accomplish multiple routes before departing. Do not force six kills or PvP. Boss-awakening is an ecosystem event not the only global apocalypse cause; killing boss does not halt the 14-day expiry.
- Six boss zones grow materially harder (weather/exposure/monster strength/mobility/damage) and richer; distinct mitigation/route/knowledge is required; NOT six reskinned HP gates. Boss destruction is along physical traversed paths, not an omniscient strike against homes. Offline Ragnarok effects require warnings, physical causal simulation, bounded damage and a feasible recovery path; no unavoidable entire-home erasure while app is closed.

## 5. Two-layer economy / immutable custody — AMENDED DIRECTLY BY CREATOR 2026-11-01
- **Exclusive cross-run economic carry:** The ONLY economic item/value that crosses seasons is authenticated secured **RAGNAROK CURRENCY**. Other items, physical Skill Cores, learned recipes, placed buildings, and the escrow chest itself remain strictly RUN-LOCAL. The profile retains only existing `profile.achievements`, `profile.run_history`, `profile.secured_ragnarok_currency`. This supersedes the unapproved U4 proposal of persistent ordinary-item/Skill-Core vaults.
- **Source and risk unchanged:** Ragnarok currency originates ONLY from finite authenticated special POI CHESTS active in final72h (with exceptional concentration in six Elder territories); it initially exists as physically carried money in inventory/cart/bag and can be lost before deposit. No crafting/recycling/score/kill-point conversion mints cross-run money; no duplicate chest/receipt/reload claims.
- **ONE DEPOSIT INTERFACE: `Rương ký gửi` (escrow chest).** The creator explicitly REMOVES the separate fixed public cash deposit machines. The escrow chest is a **RUN-LOCAL PLACEABLE CHEST** for player-owned legal home/territory, purchasable **exclusively using Ragnarok currency**. It is NOT a chest that preserves equipment, Core, recipes, or other items between runs. The physical chest disappears on run closure; only authenticated currency previously deposited into the profile remains. Player may perform MANY lawful deposits without finalizing run. The chest placement, owned-territory legality, crafting/purchase location, final72 timing, vulnerability prior to deposit and costs are design/tuning interfaces, not invisible reward shortcuts.
- **FIRST-CHEST ACQUISITION BOOTSTRAP (working design, NOT separate creator vote):** first run starts with zero secured cash, so the first chest MUST be buyable with physically looted, **unsecured** Ragnarok currency before a bank deposit exists, using a real local vendor/point-of-sale transaction; subsequent purchases may debit lawfully available secured balance. Do not require an earlier deposit to purchase the first deposit chest; that would be a hard economy deadlock. Pricing/location/ownership must make a finite non-Elder first72 cash→chest→home→deposit route physically attainable. This implementation resolution requires product test and may be adjusted without changing creator's exclusive-currency/escrow decisions.
- **Deposit semantics proposed for proof:** placing money into the functional escrow chest completes an A0-owned idempotent atomic debit of real physical currency and credit to `profile.secured_ragnarok_currency` at that moment. Ordinary home chests DO NOT secure money. The escrow chest does not become a fourth persistent item; destroying or losing the RUN-LOCAL chest cannot undo a previously completed deposit. No two-container transfer, client UI or crash can double-create money. Other possible *delayed* escrow semantics are NOT currently authorized.
- **Shops:** physical fixed-location vending and disassembly/recycling machines remain real optional services, but are NOT deposit machines. Player must approach sellers and choose spending vs saving. Shop stock, prices and scarcity must preserve the initial melee/roadside and rare physical Core discoveries; no unlimited high-tier Core shortcut. New-run purchase creates RUN-LOCAL goods only.
- **Endgame and custody:** winning physical evacuation remains independently valuable and immediately finalizes the run; six stones remain optional. Under the exclusive escrow rule, **evacuation does not auto-convert unsecured inventory/home cash into profile currency**. Any special evacuation reward that is to cross runs as Ragnarok currency must have an authenticated physical special-chest source and actually pass the escrow transaction before finalization; history/achievement can record the evacuation outcome without manufacturing cash. Multiple deposit trips do NOT end run.
- **Status:** creator-confirmed existence, cash-only persistent scope and escrow-vs-fixed-machine substitution are PRODUCT LOCKS. Exact price, seller geography, chest attack/recovery, unlock timing, owner territory rules, first-purchase proof, deposit UX and final72 score economics are still DESIGN/TUNING and NOT native accepted. Do not let legacy fixed-bank references in old reviews override this amendment.

## 5A. Fourteen-day competitive score and optional online global leaderboard — CREATOR DIRECT 2026-11-01

Online connectivity is NOT required for ordinary gameplay, physical item acquisition, Ragnarok money, normal game exit or local achievements. Ranking is a separate optional online-facing consumer of verified run results, not PvP or gameplay server combat.
- **Score is principally earned in late-run confrontation and endgame accomplishments, especially six distinctive awakened Elder boss conquests.** Early days primarily build preparation, logistics, knowledge, rare Core capacity and shelter for late-run attempts; do NOT create meaningless passive score per login hour, basic farm quantity, house size, Spares expenditure or repetitive early monster kills. **Non-boss earned endgame achievements** must also be eligible to score (e.g. genuine finite high-risk POI objectives, secured Ragnarok rare-chest custody, legal independent evacuation), so combat is the MAIN source but not the only scoring path. Exact score weighting/tiers and eligibility remain DESIGN PROPOSALS requiring user/gameplay review; not automatically invented numbers.
- **Two distinct outputs, never conflate:** (1) `run_score` is a reproducible record of LEGITIMATE IN-RUN EVENT MILESTONES for ranking/history, it does NOT mint Ragnarok cash, Skill Cores, materials or persistent power; (2) physical Ragnarok money is earned only from authorized final72 special chests and transferred to profile only by the Rương ký gửi. Score must not increase from spending pre-existing persistent currency or repeated claims of the same boss, chest, stone, or POI.

Manual abandon is not equivalent to physically completing/evacuating. Offline-only/unverifiable runs may retain honest PERSONAL records but must not be labeled VERIFIED GLOBAL SCORES. A player may opt into a verified session when online and play locally; implementation specifics, startup ticket or server replay limitations are an OPEN TECHNICAL DESIGN, not silently solved. No false anti-cheat or perfect trust claim.
- **Progression aim:** secured currency makes later run-start investment options progressively more available, but no item/Core/recipe/permanent-stat automatically crosses seasons. A richer player can choose run-local purchases at accessible shops and still must undertake navigation, combat, hazard and full endgame challenge. Use real scarcity/economic tests to prevent the leaderboard from becoming simply a scoreboard for cumulative cash purchases.

### 11.1. Distinct levels; no feature proliferation

1. **Mechanic** = object/attribute, rules, action validity, cost, effect, persistence. Example: `Core` is one item with `cast` capability and `charges`; `forge` one placeable with `process` capability, `HP`, `fuel`, `input/output`. Neither is a unique system root.
2. **Interaction chain (coupling)** = lawful event and data exchange between K1–K8. Example: roadside physical salvage `source_id` → single item instance → storage → workbench → research transaction → run-local recipe → craftable tool.
3. **Gameplay** = choices available without a forced UI script. Example: use a found Core, store it, or study it while pursuing another journey. There is **NO popup** which mechanically forces "choose 1/3".
4. **Experience** = observable player response to the outcomes (unexpected find, self-chosen preparation, confidence, disappointment with intelligible cause, desire to revisit). It is a HUMAN/behavioral gate. A forced timer, high icon count, or a passed fixture is not that experience.
5. **Map contract** = physical constraints necessary to make the above causal chain possible at many plausible places; Map Lead does NOT invent item costs, scoring, source exclusivity, Core rules or monster AI.

### 11.5. MAP LEAD INTERFACE: geography as the final actuator, NEVER the gameplay author

**Map Lead receives inputs**, not permission to choose gameplay prices or quest requirements:

2. **Sources [L]:** at least three independent, identifiable source economies: natural (trees, stone, ore, food, etc.), artificial (roads, boxes, tool/scrap, abandoned POI), rare special (gates, Gold/Core, final72 cash). 3. **Roads and cargo [L]:** road salvage, footpaths, cart-traversable roads, necessary bridges/ferries where approved, safe/dangerous alternatives, loaded/unloaded return times. An actual cart must pass geometry, slope and bridge collision.
4. **Human home sites [L]:** many buildable player-chosen positions, with comparable fundamental resource opportunity rather than one mandated "best starting base". Actual geography, freight effort, monster habitat contact and footprints define tradeoffs. No artificial region chance to raid.
