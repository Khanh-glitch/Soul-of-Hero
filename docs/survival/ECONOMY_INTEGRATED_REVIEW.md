## Real linked changes

* **Freight cart:** an owned packed Hand Cart is still learned and made independently of workbench level. Place it, bring it and the player within three tiles of an owned level-2 workbench, pay **4 Mechanical Parts + 2 Iron Ingots + 3 Spares**, and reinforce that specific deployed cart. This is a station capability, not a newly learned recipe. It retains its physical cart-kit UID, existing arbitrary metadata, and all cargo, and upgrades from 24 to 32 slots and the overload threshold 240→360 kg. Weight still slows movement at every load, terrain still matters, and above the appropriate threshold the existing 0.4 overload multiplier applies. This is not a hard maximum or free cargo transfer. The test actually compares simulation velocity at 300 kg. Full cargo must be unloaded before packing. Packing, saving, drops, pickup and redeployment retain the same upgrade and identity; stale UID, missing inputs, wrong ownership, out-of-range/low-level station, repeated intent and repeat upgrade are rejected without payment. An old deployed cart with no saved kit identity must be emptied, packed and redeployed using the existing legacy fallback before it can be upgraded. Reinforcement has no refund and is lost if the run asset is lost/reset. No new durability/maintenance claim is made for carts.
* **Two real plot choices:** Sunroot remains 1 seed → 3 Sunroot + 2 seed after 300 funded wet seconds; Bitterleaf consumes 1 seed + 1 existing Bitterleaf → 3 Bitterleaf + 1 seed after 600 funded wet seconds. One unit of plot water funds 240 dry seconds, max stored 3; uncovered rain supplies water, and a real flask charge is consumed for each manual watering. The plot retains unused water across harvest/planting; full plots no longer waste a charge. Existing `garden` intents without a crop still plant Sunroot. An owned plot must be in reach; harvesting gives outputs to inventory or physical ground drops and successful intent IDs cannot replay. Chronological offline settlement uses the same funded growth, not a passive free gift. Competing crops occupy the same land and water; propagation consumes a real herb and time. Mature herbs have a distinct pixel color, with existing procedural art and existing item icons/text rather than imported assets.
* **Readiness and destination:** 2 Bitterleaf + one Mire Gland → one Purge Tonic, a learned workbench job, 10s; its actual consumable effect is −45 radiation and +5 thirst. Mire Toad has one guaranteed gland on death; wild Bitterleaf itself regrows, so farming is an alternative, not a mandatory route. Herb can instead become Field Medkit, Herb Roast or Root Broth. Sunroot competes for Root Broth or the separately learned Root Stew, not a fabricated preservation system. The outer Dead Foundry crate in newly created runs contains a finite 2 Mechanical Parts alongside scrap, Spares and a sword; its contents remain physical. Foundry radiation, optional access and threats remain; the pre-existing Filter Mask and tonic mitigate different parts of the risk, not immunity. Freight cargo can physically carry the found parts home. One focused integration test joins planted herb → tonic → foundry exposure → consuming tonic → taking the actual finite crate stock → moving that stock into a paid freight cart → saving. Position is injected for that test: it is **not** proof a person can safely travel this route or that the whole map is balanced. Non-boss alternatives include physical wrecks and the 2 Scrap + 1 Ingot → 2 parts bench batch; each 8-stock wreck has a provisional 8% parts chance per harvested unit and finite stock. Existing runs do not retroactively acquire the added crate contents.
* **Palm without reinterpreting old cells:** Salt Shale previously shadowed the original fallback Palm. New runs pin `sourceLayoutVersion: 2`; a deterministic additive overlay places 13 palms in shipping and 13 in dev_delta, exclusively on formerly empty sand, outside starts, POI/building/elder exclusions and existing static entity tiles. Original indexed source array/order, 154 shipping Salt Shale cells, terrain and geography remain unchanged. Old runs lacking the field remain v1, including existing player position, building occupancy, indexed depletion and burns; no sources are inserted into old saves. New palms use the same source/stock/fire/collision/harvest paths and ordinary indexed save deltas. Palm is finite: 4 wood units with 50% fiber bonus per unit, not a guaranteed fiber amount. Version 2 is not a save-format version; rolling a v2 run back into an older game build is **not supported/tested** and older code does not understand the overlay or freight upgrade. The overview map shading still uses the v1 source array; ordinary in-world rendering/collision uses the pinned overlay. No blanket navigation proof for every added palm.
* **Crafting focus:** categories, searchable known recipes, a dedicated selected-recipe view with named quantities, learned/station/level/queue/fuel/output status, and an expandable tree of known sub-recipes. Inventory is opened on demand as a replacement view and returns to the selected recipe; it no longer crowds the crafting view. Search does not expose unknown recipes, there is no arbitrary list cutoff, and details scroll. Fuel and full output can be shown as a pause/wait rather than falsely disallowing a queue; actual transaction rules remain authoritative. Garden/cart use existing contextual inventory because they involve physical transfers/choices. Existing research, paid repair/salvage, workbench 2, salvage tools, shell glass and broth are retained.

## Two exact example paths (batch rounding included)

These are paths, not requirements. They omit travel, gathering tool wear, combat, fuel while idle, failures, lost cargo, queue delays and a player's existing stock; timing may overlap. Values are TUNABLE.

1. **New cart to paid freight, ore route, directed knowledge:** Claim (10 wood + 5 stone), workbench (14 wood + 4 stone), furnace (4 wood + 18 stone), research table (10 wood + 6 Spares + 1 ingot); directed Hand Cart research costs 9 more Spares/45s, then physically collect/use the blueprint. Four Rope cost 12 fiber. Cart recipe costs 24 wood + 3 ingots + 4 rope and 25s workbench. Level-2 bench costs 2 parts + 1 ingot; freight costs 4 parts + 2 ingots + 3 Spares. Six parts need **three batches** (6 scrap + 3 ingots, 36s workbench). Total including buildings, research and minimum continuously-used furnace fuel: **67 wood (including 5 wood fuel), 27 stone, 12 fiber, 6 scrap, 10 iron ore → 10 ingots, 18 Spares**; furnace 100s, parts + cart workbench 61s, research 45s, plus four hand rope crafts. Bench/cart upgrades are immediate. Fuel burns only while a furnace job is working; this is a minimum for continuous work. Nine ore suffice if the research table already exists. Found parts replace exact batches in pairs; the finite outer factory crate can supply two, or wreck drops can help. Scrap smelting is an alternate 3 scrap per ingot, 12s each, with its own fuel/batch cost. A purchased/found cart or blueprint changes the calculation; neither station level nor the cart upgrade grants knowledge.
2. **One plot, dry-weather tonic + broth:** assume an initially refilled four-charge flask, 1 seed, 1 wild Bitterleaf and 1 gland. Grow Bitterleaf using three flask charges (600s consumes 2.5 of 3 water), harvest 3 herbs + 1 seed, retaining 0.5 plot water. Replant that seed as Sunroot, add **one** charge, wait 300s (uses 1.25 of 1.5 water), harvest 3 Sunroot + 2 seeds. Queue tonic with 2 herbs + gland at a workbench, 10s; queue base Root Broth with 2 Sunroot + the remaining herb at a burning campfire, 12s. Final outputs: one tonic, one broth, one Sunroot, two seeds and 0.25 plot water, with **four charges consumed total**, 900 funded seconds; the two stages cannot overlap in one plot. Rain can replace water, and two plots trade extra materials/land for concurrency. Constructing claim + plot + workbench + campfire costs 33 wood, 12 stone and 4 fiber; campfire needs at least one additional wood fuel if used only for the 12s job. If tonic is unknown, a directed tier-2 research adds table (10 wood + 6 Spares + 1 ingot) and 9 Spares/45s, with physical blueprint collection/use. If making the table ingot from ore also add a furnace (4 wood + 18 stone), 1 ore and at least one wood for 10s furnace work. Thus the example with all those buildings, research and minimum fuel totals **49 wood, 30 stone, 4 fiber, 15 Spares, 1 iron ore**, plus seed/herb/gland and the four charges. Existing buildings, weather and stock change it. Root Stew instead needs 2 Sunroot + 1 meat + 1 herb, 14s campfire and separate knowledge; it is a competing meal, not an extra output of the above plan.

Sinks and constraints prevent a claimed free loop: rain and wild regrowth are environment-funded renewable inputs, garden returns require real seed/herb, time, water and an occupied plot; neither crop mints cash or recipe knowledge. Spares compete with research, mask and freight, and parts compete with station/tool upgrades and repair. Existing cargo, equipment, jobs, drops, UID uniqueness and receipt ring/seen-intent validation remain in the same authority; no new clock, Save Manager or Core recharge was introduced. These focused tests do not prove every possible sequence exploit-free.
