## Selection: a working landscape, interrupted

Select **POI-08**, translated into the **Whitewater Assay Works**: a former civic installation that separated dangerous mineral residue from upland water, recovered useful salts and powered its own pumps. Its assay annex certified safe output. This purpose, name and every mechanism below are **proposed SOH fiction**, not historical Last Day Rules facts.

A1 could place it beside a suitable existing watercourse and former supply road. It offers optional salvage and specialized research opportunities without becoming the continent’s only water source or technology gateway. Settling channels and intake screens explain resource provenance.

The central decision is **what to make safe**. Restoring a damaged distributor can power either drainage of the residue gallery or extraction ventilation in the assay annex. Drainage reveals concentrated material but leaves the annex exposed; ventilation permits longer inspection while the gallery remains flooded. Switching remains reversible at an accessible dry station. Limited machinery capacity creates the conflict.

A dry inspection walk offers supplies, operating clues and retreat without credentials. A shorter maintenance culvert reaches the distributor through a warned exposure zone: protection buys time; careful routing reduces exposure but does not grant immunity. Neither approach bypasses the sealed assay collection’s credential. A maintenance authorization and a higher assay authorization represent different responsibilities, while power remains independently necessary.

A residue-encrusted custodian could carry assay authorization and salvage; the protected collection holds a **different physical specimen**. A noncombat service branch should offer an alternative authorization opportunity at a meaningful preparation cost. Boss loot therefore informs a later choice without owning every valuable discovery. Supplies support the current expedition; specimens support later research; learned knowledge persists separately; an opened inspection connection improves subsequent visits. Rare deposits may support optional Core crafting after A3 mapping. 

Top-down play makes channel boundaries, safe standing areas and telegraphed sweeping attacks simultaneously legible. Machinery changes usable space, giving ordinary movement and attacks tactical value. Any eventual Core interaction must use A4’s verified behavior unchanged; no particular Core is required.
