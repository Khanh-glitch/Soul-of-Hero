## Player capability: before → after

- **Before:** watered Sunroot/Bitterleaf always advanced at the same rate everywhere, including Frostcrown; rain was the only environment effect. Manual plots and a paid Tender both used that rule. A garden/Tender window opened alongside a large inventory window.
- **After:** every existing or new plot consults its own actual runtime cell biome, temperature, moisture and slope, plus current rain/storm and roof. Crops have distinct cold/heat sensitivity. This is a *rate*, not a prohibition: snow remains plantable and manual gardening and all other food sources remain options. At empty plots the displayed rate is Sunroot's; once planted it is that crop's. UI shows condition, biome, rate, frame and water. The 5/10-minute labels are base watered time at 100%, not promises of maturity time.
- **Optional preparation:** while within reach of an owned plot, pay 4 wood + 4 fiber + 1 glass + 1 Bitterleaf for an attached cold frame and raised soil. It adds 0.24 normalized temperature when local base temperature <0.4, and +0.20 soil quality, both capped. It blocks direct rain and rain cooling, so requires manual or pumped water; a separate uncovered barrel still collects rain. No electricity grid or machine is needed. Remove it to recover 2 wood and 2 fiber, not glass or herb; no repeat refunds. Garden destruction/reclaim does not turn it into portable free stock. Its small overlay marks the plot. It is run-local and not required.
- Garden/Tender uses **one** large panel at a time. Its inventory button replaces the current panel; X returns. Shift-click transfers inventory items into the Tender fuel drawer; take-all retrieves drawer items. Ordinary chest/station interactions are unchanged. On 640×360 text remains small and the panel still covers part of the scene; it no longer opens two large panels by default.

## Exact model and limits

`gardenEnvironment` reads the existing `getWorld().economyMap` cell; these **are conservative biome/latitude/elevation-derived proxies**, not an authored ecological survey or physically measured Celsius. Rates and threshold values are provisional design tuning. The crop-specific normalized comfortable ranges are Sunroot 0.22–0.82 and Bitterleaf 0.34–0.78. Outside the range, temperature changes the factor, with a 0.15 floor. Uncovered rain/storm subtracts 0.04 temperature. Biome quality starts at snow .65, sand .8, ash .55, toxic .4, hill .8, marsh .8 or otherwise 1; extreme cell moisture and slope can reduce it. Water stock still gates growth and is spent per real growing second (one unit per 240 seconds), not per growth percentage. Thus slower crops need more water per harvest. UI rate excludes future changes of weather and water shortage.

Actual raster fixture examples, dry: temperate grass (64,67) Sunroot ~99.7%; Frostcrown snow (159,17) Sunroot 33.8%, Bitterleaf 9.75%; framed snow Sunroot 85% and Bitterleaf 85%. Wet uncovered snow Sunroot is 23.4%. Rates vary with real cell inputs. The frame cannot magically cure all toxic/hot locations. Rain/snow descriptions are game proxies: current wet input is rain/storm, not a separately simulated frozen-water model.

Growth advancement and Tender maturity boundary share the same rate; both rain recharge and drought boundaries respect the frame. Existing chronological weather-slot splitting remains, with rain/storm recalculation at the next 9-minute slot. Manual and machine plots share the same rule. Existing saved crop, water, links, fuel and growth are preserved; the new rate applies **prospectively** after continuation. `coldFrame` is additive and optional on legacy saves, and is validated if present. Downgrading a framed save to old builds is unsupported. Legacy crops outside the two shipped choices keep their old growth fallback rather than being silently reinterpreted.

## Bounded 8-hour economics (controlled model, not player evidence)

Reproduce with `cd web && node scripts/home-ecology-report.mjs`; complete machine-readable inputs/results are in `HOME_ECOLOGY_8H.json`. One controlled 8-hour interval on real raster cells, same seed 313 and weather sequence; plot initially has **one already-paid planted Sunroot and 3 soil water**. Tender scenarios also start with a funded 12-unit barrel, 40 wood fuel and an empty 12-slot chest; resources/structures are fixture-injected, not collected by an agent. No interaction or resupply during absence. Manual row reports a ripe crop still requiring return and harvest. These numbers do not measure enjoyment, travel, survival, building effort or all weather.

| Location / choice | Output after 8h | Other observed state |
|---|---:|---|
| Temperate manual | 3 ripe Sunroot, uncollected | no fuel or automated replant |
| Temperate Tender | 147 Sunroot, 50 seeds in chest | 49 harvests; 40 wood burned; barrel 12; stops fuel-starved |
| Snow manual | 3 ripe Sunroot, uncollected | no fuel or automated replant |
| Snow Tender, open | 78 Sunroot, 26 seeds in chest | 26 harvests; 30 wood consumed, 8 s stored, 10 wood left; one crop 99.44% grown |
| Snow Tender, framed | 135 Sunroot, 46 seeds in chest | 45 harvests; 40 wood consumed, 4 s stored; stops fuel-starved |

This particular weather frequently refills the barrel. A dry/roofed barrel cannot be assumed to do so. Open temperate Sunroot at 100% consumes 1.25 water per crop; initial 3 soil + 12 barrel units support at most about 12 full crops before rain/refill (ignoring pump timing and residuals). Frame blocks direct plot rain, so its greater cold productivity can increase pump/fuel/water demand; one 12-unit barrel is not an infinite 8-hour supply. Weather, frame, alternate crop, output capacity, seed/input stock, fuel, walls and machine stops change results. Each harvest produces 3 Sunroot and 2 seeds; the next plant consumes 1 seed. The 147 roots alone weigh 44.1 kg and 50 seeds add 2.5 kg, so collection and player load matter. The chest is finite: roots stack to 30 and seeds to 40, and the machine pauses when its atomic output will not fit.

A **not-a-forecast** arithmetic illustration of the whole 336h window is 42 identical 8h cycles: 6,174 roots versus 126 ripe roots from one unattended manual plot, but only if every block were reset to the same generous initial conditions, the player repeatedly returned to harvest/empty/refill, supplied **1,680 wood** for that Tender and weather/resources remained equivalent. This is not a simulated 14-day campaign, an achievable guarantee, or an argument that the Tender pays for itself. One manual player can harvest/replant far more frequently when present, while away a single plot cannot auto-replant. Each machine adds physical inventory and haul work, and output might exceed food needs or waste scarce capacity. 336h includes final 72h Ragnarok, and survival, relocation and expiry can change whether any of this is useful.

Incremental machine cost is 12 wood, 2 iron ingots, 3 mechanical parts, 3 fasteners, 2 spares, plus a nearby level-2 workbench, plot, barrel, chest and claim. If nothing already exists, claim + bench + plot + barrel + chest + Tender alone cost 56 wood, 9 stone, 4 fiber, 1 rope and the machine materials; an optional furnace adds 4 wood and 18 stone, and bench upgrade costs 1 ingot + 2 parts. Parts can be crafted in batches of two for 2 scrap + 1 ingot; fasteners in batches of three for 1 scrap + 1 ingot after legitimate learning. Glass can be made from 2 stone + 2 shell and fuel at a furnace. The 2 spares and researched fasteners still need legitimate sources; none are conjured. These inputs compete with tools, transport, repairs, shelter and study; fuel and crafting also take time. Machine operation consumes 8/4/8 fuel-seconds per plant/pump/harvest, respectively; one wood supplies 20 seconds. Do not claim a universal break-even from the quantity comparison. The illustrative result suggests the Tender can save many repetitive returns for a player with an established, stocked home; it may be a poor early-run investment or unnecessary for someone foraging, cooking, exploring, manually gardening or ignoring agriculture. No yield/fuel buff was made merely to hit a payoff target.
