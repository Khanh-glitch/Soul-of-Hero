## B. Interconnected playable systems

* **Industrial acquisition and manufacture:** roadside wreck is a finite physical 8-stock scrap source; each yielded unit has an independent provisional 8% Mechanical Parts bonus (roughly 0.64 expected parts per complete wreck, ~49% chance at least one if probabilities are independent). Receipt, RNG, drop custody and normal pickup apply; nothing drops from an untouched node. Existing runs can harvest their remaining wreck stock. The existing foundry outer loot crate also adds two parts on *new runs* only; already-created inventories do not get retroactive free loot. Parts remain craftable: 2 scrap + 1 ingot → 2 parts at a level-1 workbench, 12s. These alternatives compete with tool repair, upgraded recipes, bench upgrade and scrap's other uses.
* **Technology:** level-2 bench costs existing 2 parts + 1 ingot, no speculative Spares upgrade charge. Research remains existing random (4 Spares/25s), directed (9/45s), physical specimen (30s) and found blueprints; output blueprint must be collected and used. Tier-2 Salvager Pick/Axe recipes must be learned separately, then require accessible owned level-2 bench and physical inputs. Each costs the corresponding Iron Pick/Axe + 2 parts + 2 Spares, 25s. A paid job reserves the actual input tool/UID and cancel returns it or drops it if necessary. A broken tool can be consumed in this expensive upgrade; it is not a free repair. Output is a new object. Spares compete with research, Filter Mask and other recipes. No Core recipe is newly gated.
* **Actual industrial effect:** Salvager Pick power stone/ore/scrap 9/8/7 versus iron pick 6/5/4, swing 410ms versus 430ms and durability 500 versus 400. Salvager Axe wood power 9 versus iron axe 6, swing 390ms versus 400ms and durability 500 versus 400. `harvestHit` applies power to real finite source HP/stock; number of units per swing is rounded by remaining HP and is not a guaranteed fixed drop count. Both tools can be repaired for 1 ingot + 1 part + 1 Spares. There is no salvage recipe that could reverse the tool upgrade for profit.
* **Equipment lifecycle:** only definitions with a repair rule survive wear at zero as the **same UID** with zero durability and cannot attack/harvest; workbench repair costs real materials and restores that UID. Unrepairable items still break under the prior rule; no Core charge restoration. Broken study/salvage still consume/reserve the real item; retry, stale UID, full-output, cancellation and destruction follow existing custody paths. UI marks broken items. Existing already-deleted items cannot be recreated.
* **Home and medicine:** one seed + two existing flask charges can water a planted garden long enough for 300s dry growth (one unit covers 240s; rain can supply water). Harvest yields 3 Sunroot and 2 seed under existing rules. Root Broth uses 2 crop + 1 Bitterleaf at a burning campfire for 12s → 1 actual food: +32 hunger/+16 thirst/+12 HP/+10 warmth. Sunroot also competes with Root Stew and Bitterleaf with medicine/Herb Roast. Campfire fuel burns continuously, even idle; offline settlement only advances funded jobs and output remains in the station. This is not preservation or automatic passive income. Existing Salted Roast is a hearty meal, not shelf-stable: misleading Raw Salt text was removed; no new spoilage clock is claimed.
* **Exploration and protection:** shore crabs drop 1 shell; 2 shell + 2 stone → 1 glass in a fueled furnace for 16s, an alternative to 1 Frost Crystal + 1 stone in 14s. Glass + 2 cloth + 3 Spares → Filter Mask at a workbench, 16s once known. Cloth costs 6 fiber each. The mask changes actual online foundry/toxic radiation accumulation (7→0.8 units/min under corresponding online conditions), and now reduces existing foundry-zone offline exposure by the same proportion; it does not grant immunity. No key, fuel, generator, hazard or boss gate was bypassed. The shell route does not prove every coast/foundry path is practically safe.
* **Building maintenance:** V (or Build panel) enters structure repair mode; click an owned physical structure within three tiles. Each press pays ceil(25% of each original build ingredient), restores at most 25% of its actual max HP, and is rejected for full/dead, out-of-range, other-owned or item-deployed escrow structures. It never revives a destroyed structure or refunds upgrade investments. No fictional raid, passive house repair, duplicate building or clock was added.

## C. Cost calculations and limitations

* **Early:** pick 3 wood + 4 stone + 1 rope (3 fiber), hand craft. H1 claim + workbench + furnace have base construction costs 28 wood + 27 stone total (10+14+4 wood; 5+4+18 stone); location and existing building restrictions apply. Excludes transport, tools and repair.
* **H2 from scratch, ore route:** one Salvager Pick and its level-2 station, assuming no items on hand and all recipes already known: 6 iron ore → 6 ingots (3 for iron pick, 1 for upgrade, 2 for two parts batches), 4 scrap, 2 wood for the iron pick, 1 hide → 1 leather, 2 Spares. Furnace work is 60s, at least 3 wood fuel if run continuously (20s/wood); workbench work 8+15+24+25 = 72s. The upgrade is immediate. Add H1 construction above; production times may overlap and omit travel, workbench upgrade itself and the cost of knowledge. The alternative all-scrap smelting consumes 18 additional scrap and 72s furnace work, requiring at least 4 wood fuel; total 22 scrap including parts inputs. Full transport/load, combat, losses and research time remain unmeasured.
* A **found batch of two parts** replaces one 2-scrap + 1-ingot + 12s parts job, not the cost of iron pick or the bench upgrade. If level 2 is already paid, the upgrade's 2 parts + 1 ingot are no longer required; reuse existing intermediate stock and batch rounding. The two industrial recipes require a separate 2 parts each.
* **Mask shell route:** two crabs at guaranteed 1 shell each, 2 stone, 12 fiber → 2 cloth, 3 Spares, one fueled furnace wood (20s capacity) and learned mask recipe. Work is 16s furnace plus 16s cloth plus 16s mask workbench, with furnace and workbench potentially concurrent. `gas_mask` knowledge may come from directed research (9 Spares, 45s), 35%-chance crawler optic specimen, random research, or a physical blueprint subject to its existing gate. Research table itself costs 10 wood + 6 Spares + 1 ingot and a claim. These are alternatives, not additive mandatory payments.
* Wreck's 0.7 Spares bonus is per stock unit: nominal 5.6 expected Spares per untouched 8-stock wreck before time, access and variance. A 9-Spare directed research can take roughly two such wrecks on expectation, not a guarantee. Added 8% part bonus is deliberately smaller; direct foundry crate is finite and hazardous to reach. No broader supply distribution or 14-day economy has been measured.

## D. UX, original art and historical sources

The existing in-game panels, not a separate Atlas dashboard, gained known-recipe search grouped by station; select a known recipe for its description and optionally expand known nested recipes, with per-batch quantities and cycle protection. It is **not** an aggregate shopping calculator: alternatives, carried stock, batch rounding and fuel must still be considered. Item info can reveal known downstream recipes only. Directed research is searchable and no longer limited to 14 results. Native confirmation dialogs were replaced by in-panel confirm/cancel for destructive study and dismantling. Cost icons now have names/amounts on hover; station, knowledge, level, fuel and input information remain distinct. Keyboard shortcuts no longer consume text typed into search; Escape first leaves the field. The text is still small at 640×360 and scrolling is necessary; no claim of final UX acceptance.

New Salvager Pick/Axe and parts pixel icons are original procedural sprites made in `web/src/game/art.ts`, using this game's existing drawing code; Root Broth reuses the existing original bowl motif with a different palette. No externally downloaded image was redistributed. The renderer, inventory and crafting display the same catalog icon.

Primary historical sources inspected directly: [2019-07-30 official FAQ](https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2019/0730/6973.html), Civilization Restart survival FAQ §§10–14: roadside barrels/worktables/drawing tables provide blueprints, and exhausted durable equipment can be repaired with specified materials at home. Adapted ideas: run-local physical blueprints, paid repair and components; SOH doesn't inherit their exact quantities or permanent rare blueprint rule. [2020-08-26 official Operation Base update](https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2020/0826/10634.html) expressly introduces a separate non-restarting personal base and cross-mode transport: rejected for SOH. Atlas identifies 2023 directed research as mode-specific, not evidence that 2019 used that exact UI. No external game UI or art is copied. Research is inspiration, not canon.
