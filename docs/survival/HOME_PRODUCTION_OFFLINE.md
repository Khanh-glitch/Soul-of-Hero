## Player capabilities

**Before:** manual Sunroot and Bitterleaf gardening, water charges/rain, finite barrels, timed paid station jobs, and manually collected outputs. Nothing harvested or replanted a garden autonomously.

**Now:** an optional owned **Garden Tender** is a built, destructible object, not a default plot upgrade. It costs 12 wood, 2 iron ingots, 3 mechanical parts, 3 fasteners and 2 Spares, on a claim and near the player’s level-2 workbench. The bench upgrade itself costs 2 mechanical parts and 1 ingot. These resources also compete with equipment/repair, reinforced carts, construction and research. Costs are tunable, not canon. The tender has a three-slot fuel drawer. The player explicitly selects one owned garden, rain barrel and small or large chest within three tiles, with an unobstructed sampled segment, chooses Sunroot or Bitterleaf, and starts/stops it. Each linked garden/barrel/chest is exclusive to one tender. A wall, removal, ownership change or out-of-range component interrupts the link; no arbitrary remote chest is searched.

The chest supplies real planting inputs and receives finite harvests; the barrel holds at most 12 water. Unroofed rain fills it, and the tender panel can pour one carried charged-flask/canteen unit into a nearby owned barrel (requires one whole unit of space). Rain can water the plot directly, while roofed plots do not receive rain. Machine operations use physical fuel: plant 8 seconds, pump 4 seconds, harvest 8 seconds of the existing item fuel values; leftover energy remains in the machine. A dry plot gets a paid one-unit pump from the linked barrel. Planting uses one unit of barrel water when the plot has less than one unit, otherwise its existing water is reused. Growing still uses the ordinary garden water/growth rules. Manual gardening remains available.

A complete harvest is inserted only if **all** of its output fits; a full chest leaves the ripe crop untouched. Harvest, then planting, then pumping happen in that order at a timestamp. Missing fuel, planting inputs, water, storage or links halt the relevant operation and are shown in the tender panel. Rain can still grow a crop naturally while the tender cannot pump. Auto-plant can wait even after a valid harvest; it never invents a seed or plant input. Reclaim/destruction preserves drawer contents as a physical bag; residual already-burned energy is lost, as for a removed station. Outputs remain in the linked chest until physically collected. The standard fuelled cooking and workbench systems consume collected produce normally.

**Example, paid per operation:** a Sunroot planting consumes one chest seed. A ripe plant yields 3 Sunroot + 2 seeds into that same chest; a subsequent replant consumes one of those actual seeds. In a dry 300-second crop the plot consumes 1.25 water units, requiring initial soil water plus a paid pump if necessary; rain, existing soil water and full storage change the exact sequence. Each harvest and each replant costs 8 fuel-seconds; a pump costs 4. Wood supplies 20 fuel-seconds and can alternatively build walls or fuel a fire. Bitterleaf planting consumes 1 seed + 1 Bitterleaf; harvest yields 3 Bitterleaf + 1 seed, which can be replanted or used for food/medicine. These are model mechanics and provisional costs, not a claim of good player balance.

## Chronology, custody and saves

The same scheduler handles online and offline advancement. It includes weather transitions, linked crop maturity/dryness and barrel rain-fill boundaries, station work and existing fire/terrain boundaries. Operations commit against actual current resources at the event time, not based on the state at login. Normal input synchronizes elapsed world time before applying the transaction. A full or disconnected machine cannot generate outputs. Tests compare one offline settlement against 120 smaller intervals including event order/times and against 400 actual online frames around maturity; they also test rain filling before planting, full storage, fuel/input/water interruption, wall and destruction, stop/restart, flask conservation, cooking output and the 336-hour wipe. Tests use controlled fixture setup and do not prove all weather/fire/destruction combinations.

The optional `farm` state is additive, with link IDs, enabled flag, crop choice and bounded stored energy validated on load. Old runs and gardens gain no controller or free stock; old map/source versions, cargo, queues and UIDs are not rewritten. An actual `a956c9e` production executable created an early browser save continued by the candidate, and a separate **controlled** old-engine/browser fixture continued a paid queued job, planted/watered garden, cart UID/cargo and source identity. This is stronger than a fresh-save check but not an exhaustive history/migration audit. A newly built tender survived same-browser save/reload. Older-build rollback of new structures is unsupported; back up browser storage.

## Evidence and limitations

`cd web && npx tsc --noEmit && npm test && npm run build && npm audit`: **153 tests / 16 files**, build and audit (zero reported vulnerabilities) passed before the final copy/status-wording change; TypeScript, all 11 focused home-production tests and build were rerun after it. A short unmodified-input fresh browser run moved and harvested a v3 bush. `e2e/home-production-smoke.mjs` uses a **controlled** teleported/material-supplied fixture but real paid build transactions and real E/select/start inputs; it verifies planting and same-browser reload. Four captured screenshots under `web/evidence/home-production/` were inspected at 640×360 and 1280×720. The panel fits its main controls but small text remains a readability concern and lower content scrolls.

The controller currently serves one plot, one barrel and one chest, not a general electric grid or station-to-station conveyor network. Crop growth currently models water and rain, not crop-specific temperature/season/sunlight; storms do not invent offline attacks or raids. The game does not replay all actor/creature movement, lightning or spread offline. The direct-segment link check is not a pipe/route simulation; damaged structures function until destroyed, and there is no separate machine wear. Automated tests are not human enjoyment evidence, and real natural gathering-to-machine economics, long-duration performance with many machines, independent CI, accessibility and player balancing remain open. The most useful next mechanics step is a tested physical local power/water distribution and station-input network with explicit connections, capacity/maintenance, chronological multi-consumer contention and paid transfers—only after player testing supports expanding the one-plot design. The separately authored Map Tower handoff and outstanding geography acceptance remain separate.

## Research adaptation

The official [2020-08-26 Last Island update](https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2020/0826/10634.html) describes paid facility construction, resource/time/queue investment, storage/workshop capacity and physical collection. These are useful economic principles, not SOH numbers. Its separate persistent base, inter-run transfers, permanent facilities, login rewards and PvP systems are explicitly **not** adopted; all SOH items, machines and crop production reset with the run.
