## 1. Deeply coupled gameplay — 8 overlapping domains

GEO+LOOT: Same stable whole-world geography; diverse regions and unspoiled discovery, rivers/roads/terrain/biome/temperature/cold/radiation/toxin/marsh/weather/day-night. Resource sources are asymmetric by region with attainable novice sources and actual sinks, so choosing route, house and outpost matters. Spawn must be randomly selected among a BROAD physically safe distribution of eligible cells on same continent, not only 3 hard-coded starts (three previously tested regions are fixtures). Physics, navigation, map metadata, camera, POI entrances, buildings and hazard visual cues agree with geography.

TRI-KNOWLEDGE: Distinguish item instance, physical unlearned blueprint, learned recipe and workstation capability. Spares/parts from accessible roadside provide gradual research without free rare items. There are three research methods: bounded random R&D, directed targeted research and sample-consuming reverse engineering. Shared intermediate material competition creates opportunity costs; newly learned recipe REVALUES old inventory and motivates returning to storage. Research and acquired formula are RUN-LOCAL, fully wiped with run. Search, recipe ingredient reverse-index and partial discovery Codex reveal causal uses without spoiling all POIs.

CORE+HERO: Authentic male/female player bodies, satisfying starter weak melee/knife. Found finite-use Core is a physical unique, independently useful and exciting SURPRISE: USE immediately vs STORE vs RESEARCH to learn run-local production, different choices, charges and scarce catalysts. A4 owns source-perfect Gold timeline, full world/terrain and enemy physical FX, telegraphs, cleanup, brightness management, proper top-down combat geometry/control, not a generic mana projectile. One committed cast charges once, canceled-before-effect does not, multistage families have explicit authored policy. Death bag, storage and save preserve Core UUID and actual uses.

PHYSICAL HOME+LOGISTICS: One floor foundation/door/window/wall/enclosed room with ONE fadeable roof, real collision, bed/death respawn, chest/station/smelter/forge/research/garden/fuel/comfort/energy/traps/repair. Room topology changes physical traversal, transport time, production throughput, base security and AI routes; upgrading station can shift the bottleneck from processing to fuel/inputs. Base siting and outpost/cart/pets/mounts/vehicle freight are meaningful when changing route return time, payload and exposure, not just vanity. Farm/forge/research jobs must be FUNDED before being allowed to run while offline; show meaningful, exact-once result upon return. Sheltered offline hunger may reduce to 1 HP but cannot alone kill, wilderness logoff carries warned risk. Provisions/season weather continue based on consistent clock.

POI ACCESS GRAPH: Place = physical region, actual authored entrance/stand/source, enemy, puzzle, credential, power circuit, hazard, routes, rooms and partial loot. Discovering a blocked room is useful; knowledge/map journal can create an independent future objective. Key and generator are separate conditions, alternate paths and prep matter, retry does not deadlock when an item is used. Finite chest/node source ownership and state prevent re-farming from reload. Boss rewards may give material AND knowledge/access clue; ordinary no-boss routes remain valuable. Distinguish player base, user outpost, authored POI and sealed Elder territory, not one generic building.

LIVING THREATS: Fair low-level threats and source-authored hostile/hazard producers; environment warning→prepare/protect/avoid/retreat; real 2D combat hitboxes/i-frames/animation/vision/nav/collision. Monsters can follow only unexpired player traces to home (including offline world chronology), never know home coordinates, with actual path/breach/repair consequences and safe recovery. Player-invoked finite defensive events are allowed with advance preparation, no compulsory surprise raids. Death creates ONE recoverable persistent bag, bed/default spawn within same run, exact item identity across two OS processes. Escape and successful avoidance are legitimate player outcomes.

TIME+GUIDANCE: One in-world day/night/weather/season clock that advances online and offline consistently; 14 real-day UTC maximum is different from fictional accelerated time. D057 run-local newcomer guidance/quest rewards must be truthful, optional/out-of-order after first run and never force all players into the same path. Day1–11 permit multiple independent goals: low-risk salvage, construction, garden, smelting, research, POI clues, combat, rare Core, transport. Three consecutive non-dungeon sessions must make actual progress; fun is not waiting through timer padding. An ordinary long run should leave much interesting terrain and systems unexplored, enabling voluntary repeat play.

RAGNAROK+CUSTODY: Last 72h days12–13 progressively alter weather/hazard/monsters and six Elder territories; day14 awakened Elder forms create distinct physical danger. Six unique stones milestones1–6; six-stone ending extraordinary OPTIONAL, never a gate to normal evacuation. Special rare Ragnarok chests ONLY spawn cross-run money, physically carried, risk loss, can be physically deposited at FIXED sites several times without exiting. Home stash/unbanked chest/score/recycling cannot mint profile money. Fixed-location vending/disassembly are real markets/physical machines with finite stock, sinks and travel choice after new run. Physical evacuation (separate from early ABANDON) ends the run on completion by locked default; expiry after 336h also ends run.
