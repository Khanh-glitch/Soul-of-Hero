## Exact example chains (known recipes unless noted)

1. **Level-2 bench + lined furnace + one field repair kit from scratch, ore route:** Claim 10 wood/5 stone, bench 14 wood/4 stone, furnace 4 wood/18 stone. Two fastener batches cost 2 ingots + 2 scrap and produce 6; two Mechanical Parts batches cost 2 ingots + 4 scrap and produce 4. Bench upgrade spends 2 parts + 1 ingot; lining spends 2 brick + 3 fasteners + 1 part; repair kit spends 2 fasteners + 1 cloth. Brick batch: 3 stone → 2 brick/10s furnace. Cloth: 6 fiber/8s bench. In total **5 iron ore, 6 scrap, 6 fiber, 30 stone, 31 wood** (28 construction + minimum 3 wood for 60 seconds of pre-lining furnace work), yielding one kit, one lined furnace, one spare fastener and one spare part. Work: 50s smelting + 10s brick; bench 20s fasteners + 24s parts + 8s cloth + 12s kit. Bench/lining upgrades are immediate. Requires separately learned fasteners, brick and kit; existing Old Mill brick blueprint, physical fastener/kit study or 9-Spares directed research per unknown recipe are alternatives, not additive mandatory costs. Costs exclude research table, travel, tool wear, risk and idle fuel.
2. **One pack plus one canteen, already level-2 bench:** two canvas batches consume 2 cloth, 2 salt and 2 herb → 4 canvas. Make cloth from 12 fiber; two ropes use another 6 fiber. Three leather use 3 hide; one fastener batch uses 1 ingot + 1 scrap → 3; one shell-glass batch uses 2 stone + 2 crab shells. Pack consumes 1 canvas, 2 leather, 2 fasteners, 2 rope; canteen consumes 2 canvas, 1 leather, 1 glass, 1 fastener. Outputs are pack + empty canteen + **1 canvas remaining**. Raw inputs **18 fiber, 2 salt, 2 herb, 3 hide, 2 shells, 2 stone, 1 ore, 1 scrap** plus at least 2 wood for 24s campfire work and 2 wood for 26s furnace work; bench work 92s (cloth 16 + leather 24 + fasteners 10 + pack 22 + canteen 20). Assumes the stations/level/knowledge already exist and no idle campfire fuel. Found canvas at Halcyon can replace a batch; rainfall/gardening can provide herbs but require their real seed/water/time. Fill the canteen from fresh water before the trip; crafting never supplies the eight charges. Knowledge, construction, travel and combat are separate costs.

The original UID and physical kit are validated before field repair; full/invalid targets, active actions, stale selections and duplicate intents fail without debit. Lining is saved on the existing owned furnace; legacy furnaces without the field behave as before. No new save manager, authority or clock. Canteen charge is persisted on its unique stack; output, reserved inputs, cancellation and full-output handling remain the existing job rules. New ordinary crate contents apply only to new runs; existing wreck stock is not refilled. Upgrading a running furnace affects only future work. Neither recipe/research nor upgrade duplicates cash, Cores, cargo or items. Like earlier v2 source/cart metadata, rollback to an older game build is not guaranteed. These focused checks cannot establish general exploit absence.
