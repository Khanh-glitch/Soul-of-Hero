## Locked by user (intent, not proof of runtime)
- **Near-complete behavioral transfer**, not “survival-inspired decoration”: actual verbs, resource source/sink, physical ownership and loot, road salvage/Spares, station tiers, knowledge, research, durability, housing, exploration, graduated POIs, power/access, risk/loss and reasons to return. A silent non-dungeon session still constitutes worthwhile play.
- Authored fully conceived continent, progressive **physical** access by hazard/resources/gear/enemies/terrain (no invisible level walls), ten original Chapter-1 dungeon exteriors in roughly the initial quarter, environmental/biome/civilization cause and geographic staging, no tile confetti. World is not a hallway connecting 10 dungeons.
- Freeform top-down physical home where Hero can enter, own a roofed room, store possessions, place facilities and activate bed. World collision/navigation/camera/save must make decisions real.
- Physical limited-use **Skill Core** as analogue of found firearm; owning/using it is separate from learning permanent reproduction recipes and gathering real future crafting ingredients. Exact use/repair/sacrifice policy still under discussion. Gold VFX entire original on enemies AND world/terrain must be retained, never replaced by a “similar” pixel effect; adequate contrast/readability on bright maps and 2K/4K.
- Quiet solo scavenging with basic melee must remain enjoyable **before** a Core. User recently clarified even a weak first SMG created a perceived identity/capability threshold **without shooting anyone** and while avoiding fights; new Core's ownership may be emotionally valuable before any cast. This is first-person qualitative testimony, **not** proof that a specific design solution works.
- At ~14-day-equivalent long-form play, only a few distinctive rare Core discoveries yet meaningful unknown rooms/recipes/POIs/routes remain, ≥3 compelling goals across ≥2 systems; desire to continue/restart must follow real world breadth, not timer padding. No forced home wipe, PvP grief, artificial recipe-count exactness, or clone of 2026 microtransactions.
- Exactly **two** initially selectable actual playable Male and Female Adventurer avatars for survival/combat (D045/D046); no dedicated gun-shooting frames required. HUD/UI/UX high quality are mandatory, not art pass deferred indefinitely. User delegates evidence-based pass/reject of proposals without repeated tiny approvals, not rights to falsify provenance.

## OPEN or UNDERCONSIDERED in genuine player-experience design
1. **Before/at/after the first Core:** safe low-danger loot enjoyable alone; cues of possible future abilities without spoiler or gate; weak found Core a new ownership identity even if permanently stored/never cast; rarity versus accessibility, quick equip, finite charge fear and decision not to use. Need novice observation, no assumed compulsory first fight.
2. **HUD information architecture:** exploration minimal, danger readable, Core physical identity/current and max charge/condition/ready semantics, source-aware pickup, quantity-selective stash, authentic A1 hint/permissions vs loot, research “use vs store vs sacrifice” explanation, map undiscovered vs discovered. Must cooperate with 720/2K/4K sharp Gold, two avatars and modal-safe controls. A6 V3 isolated is not a game UX acceptance.
3. **Solo risk ecology:** without PvP and without forcing combat, balance rare exciting sightings, optional evasion, hazards, telegraphed threats, protected early loops and worthwhile damage/loss; actual death bag recovery no teleport/dupes/softlock. Death timer, hunger/weather severity, durability/repair edge still OPEN.
4. **POI and loot cadence:** how a non-boss/newbie sees a superior but presently inaccessible room; partial low-risk progress, keys and power, source replenishment, no circular gates; how optional boss, puzzle, repair or stealth produce comparable attractive but differentiated goals; scarcity without arbitrary grind.
5. **Base as economy:** progression beyond safe roof/bed/chest: actual furnace/fuel, timed workbench, research, heat, farm, station layout, storage and preparation cost; home location and outpost utility must alter what journeys can be attempted and net success.
6. **Travel/time/world density:** authored full-continent JSON vs actual populated interactables; active play opportunity cost, procedural ecology vs causal authored biomes, cart/boat/pets later if proven useful; 14-day mystery requires playable branches rather than 14×45-minute simulated activity.
7. **Research governance:** per-family Core use debit/repair/research-sample consumption vs acquisition expectations; how old loot revalues; cosmetic item vs production knowledge; whether R&D random variant is fun or opaque friction; future NPC markets/guild permissions if solo.
8. **Secondary later-era system admission:** territory cabinet, vehicle, pet genetic fusion, group recipe sharing, 2025 special timed island, 2026 territory wars, multiplayer leaderboard/microtransactions must **not** be treated as 2019 baseline. Some are rejected, some later optional candidates, some deliberately NOT_REQUIRED_SOLO.
9. **Sensory/gamefeel/visual world:** authored Gold full lifecycle, original enemy/environment effect, readable map brightness adaptation, roof/fog lighting, audio/impact, UI feedback without forced popups or inventory friction. Native source correctness ≠ user delight.
10. **Player agency & pacing:** first independent evening home-first vs explorer-first, first promising unopened place, first self-chosen future journey, 3 consecutive sessions without dungeon where genuine goals advance, novice vs optimizer field evaluation.
