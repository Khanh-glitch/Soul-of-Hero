## 3. Actual V17 *research authority* read in code, and what it implies

At [engine.ts L1058–1089](https://github.com/Khanh-glitch/Soul-of-Hero/blob/b5c8004ca4e77be62484fb3cd296fe296d7de4c9/web/src/game/engine.ts#L1058-L1089):
- **Random Spares** pays a finite physical Spares quantity, queues a blueprint, prioritises unknown NON-Core recipes until they are exhausted, then permits Cores. Thus the random pool is not a generic uniform all-recipe unlock.
- **Directed Spares** pays higher Spares amount and targets a named unknown recipe but `permitsDirectedResearch` is `tier < 3`. Therefore rare tier-3 recipes are NOT universally acquired by just paying the directed price.
- **Specimen** consumes a real specific unique item or portion and queues the specific `ITEMS[key].specimen`, `CORE_STUDY[key]`, or `studyRecipe` output. This branch in current SOH does not charge additional Spares by itself; the historical Last Island Drawing Table report is NOT the same cost policy.
- Research queue emits **physical blueprint into research output**, requiring pickup and `useItem` to enter run-local knowledge. Knowledge, station level, paid ingredients, and physical item custody are separate axes. Research Table construction costs **wood 10 + Spares 6 + ingot 1**, in addition to own-home claim and geography. `WORKBENCH_UPGRADE` L2 costs parts 2 + ingot 1; L3 costs parts 6 + ingot 8 + fasteners 8 + Spares 12.
- From earlier documented SOH tuning, random is 4 Spares/25s, directed 9 Spares/45s, specimen 30s — these are SOH prototype rates, NOT original Last Island 150/300/600, and should be verified against BAL at exact HEAD before treating as stable numerical source.
- There is a distinct **sample cost vs present power** tradeoff: found item can be used/kept/studied (if authorized) but cannot simultaneously provide all three benefits. Code-correct UID and queue are not proof the alternative exists near a player's actual route.

## 4. A concrete *mechanical network* the old V18 prompt did not understand

**Car Wreck (finite 8 stock) → choice between Spares, Damaged Drive, Mechanical Parts, factory progress, tool repair and fuel**, not simply salvage→Fuel Can.

- [wreck-economy.ts](https://github.com/Khanh-glitch/Soul-of-Hero/blob/b5c8004ca4e77be62484fb3cd296fe296d7de4c9/web/src/game/wreck-economy.ts) keeps a versioned direct-v1 or recycler-v1 policy according to authorized physical Foundry Recycler. Direct-v1 removes damaged drive and changes Spares bonus to 0.7 per harvested stock unit; expected Spares from intact 8-stock wreck = **5.6**, subject to chance and stock.
- Recycler-v1 split source has **Spares 0.3** and **Damaged Drive 0.2** per stock unit (separate rolls): expectation **2.4 immediate Spares + 1.6 drives** per wreck. A drive can go to *Foundry Recycler* for **2 Spares** (potential +3.2, giving a nominal gross 5.6 Spares before processing costs) OR a workbench `recover_drive` recipe **1 Damaged Drive + 1 Scrap → 1 Mechanical Parts**, sacrificing that Spares route. Old/new models are **versioned**; not mixing both reward tables in a single run.
- Modern version-3 wreck harvest also independently rolls **35% Salvaged Oil** per finite stock unit. It does NOT make an oil well, crude pump, refinery or vehicle. First learned canister from this path needs at least 4 oil (one consumed sample + three recipe inputs), glass and furnace fuel/time. A new crate isn't entitled to materialize because a recipe was added.
- Thus allocation depends on local Foundry access, transport costs, research needs, parts-for-tools, tower/build costs, physical oil route and travel danger. A new power grid without this resource accounting is premature.
