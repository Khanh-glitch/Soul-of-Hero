## SOH gameplay appraisal
**Distinct value:** Not a boss→key→chest ladder. In `Whitewater Assay Works` (proposed POI08 adaptation), a dry safe partial salvage route supports repeat low-risk sorties, and a dangerous shortcut toward a reversible distributor permits limited scarce power for either **drainage** (valuable residue) or **ventilation** (assay specimen/knowledge). Two independent credential levels, protection vs exposure, optional noncombat authorization and world access potential are motivated by POI08, DEP03–05/31, JOURNEY11/12. Good alignment to original source differences, persistent base, limited rare Core crafting pathways; no claimed skill ID, map location or reward authorizations.
**Unresolved must become executable structural requirements in Unit2 (not flaws invalidating Unit1):**
1. **Fake choice risk:** toggling distributor could give both full route rewards immediately at negligible cost. Unit2 must show *bounded, legible* switching consequence (reclaimed pressure takes time, moving flood, temporary protection/resource burden, hazardous re-route or analogous real player constraint), not arbitrary locked-out permanent rewards or invented timers. Preserve reversible switching.
2. **Recovery/no softlock:** novice depleted gear, dropped key/corpse bag, power off in deep area, failed boss, reentry/reload and exit from any entered room. Avoid placing only necessary card/rescue lever behind its own gate. Build an explicit retreat path or flag `UNPROVEN` rather than assume it.
3. **Noncombat authorization credibility:** specify observable cost/route and whether alternative credential is one-time/reacquirable; don't trivialize boss or make stealth route another equivalent key under no threat.
4. **Resource/knowledge economy:** three distinct rewards—immediate salvage, physical specimen, permanent formula/access discovery—must not share one invalid A3 ID or become a one-source progression hard gate. Learn formula != own Core instance, and the original A4 Gold source remains unchanged.
5. **Graph ≠ space:** 8–12 semantic nodes are not real nav cells, 3D floors or a playable dungeon. Future Unit6 must prove top-down camera 640×360 constraints and A1 placement; no live-engine acceptance now.
