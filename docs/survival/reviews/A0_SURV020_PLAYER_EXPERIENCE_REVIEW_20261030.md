# A0 SURV-020: player experience review, 2026-10-30

**Verdict: NOT ACCEPTED.** This is an engineering/source review against the Atlas, not observation of an inexperienced player. The following distinguishes current interaction from aspirations; it does not award B0, B1/B2, visual, Gold, long-term replay or human-play gates.

## What is actually usable

- The native fresh-save input journey [40989573115](https://github.com/Khanh-glitch/Soul-of-Hero/actions/runs/40989573115), `54aacfa`, gathers resources before building, crafts a workbench via A3, makes and collects two cloth, then pays for an enclosed room with a door, chest and bed. It physically exits, harvests more oak, returns, stores the output, reloads and reconstructs the world, and physically retrieves exact oak and cloth. This demonstrates useful persistent storage/crafting and a route *through one scripted sequence*, not proof that new players discover it without coaching.
- A separate fixture-backed bed/death suite exercises a real GUI respawn and bag with injected death; it does not show a lethal expedition. Being able to roam and gather before building, as that normal-input sequence does, supports a limited explore-first action, but does not prove a second consequential progression path or an independently evaluated shelter-first/explore-first choice.
- A3's real paid recipe and station queue provide a real use for gathered inputs, but there is no demonstrated canonical dangerous POI, equipment preparation loop or durable reward for returning to one. Legacy discoveries exist; their existence does not establish the requested canonical POI economy or meaningful long-term goals. No artificial rewards, assumed enemy or fixed seam are counted.

## Cost and friction (measure before rebalancing)

- The tested 5×5 room uses 15 walls at 2 oak, one door at 2 oak + 1 scrap, one chest at 4 oak + 1 scrap and one bed at 4 oak + 1 scrap: **40 oak and 3 scrap**, in addition to the separately paid workbench and weave inputs. Catalog oak yields 2 per hit, at most 3 hits per node, so obtaining 40 new oak costs at least 20 successful harvest interactions under that catalog, ignoring travel and earlier stock. This is an arithmetic lower bound, not measured player time, a claim that the test performs exactly 20, or evidence that the pacing is acceptable. Obtain timed novice play and compare smaller viable shelters and routes before changing prices.
- The chest currently chooses a whole-bag stash or whole-chest take, not item/quantity selection. It protects A3 instances instead of moving them; generic unique-instance storage is unsupported. Source follow-up `d775348` lets a unique-only bag retrieve ordinary chest materials; its combined post-reload bed fixture initially failed. At `03a39f3f`, [A6 composition 40990690168](https://github.com/Khanh-glitch/Soul-of-Hero/actions/runs/40990690168) passed all 113 bed checks after separately checking chest contents, death-bag exclusion and the freshly reconstructed production A3 adapter's ownership audit. This is fixture-funded, not normal-input unique acquisition. This is an actual player-agency limitation, not a complete inventory UI.
- A player carrying both ordinary materials and a protected instance still gets stash first, with no choice to retrieve only a desired item. Full-bag/full-chest and duplicate cases are fixture-tested, but do not prove that an inexperienced player understands the direction, limitations or how to plan a trip. A6-owned UI remains isolated; avoid inventing a second inventory authority.
- The production build strip and world prompts expose actions, but the automation knows piece bindings, valid geometry, where to gather, and how to operate the scrolling workbench panel. Small 640×360 review thumbnails showed the later layout correction, not original-resolution readability or an unprompted human tutorial. Native 720p/2K/4K frames were captured in fixtures, but original full-resolution frames were not available for human review.

## Gate to meaningfully improve the product

1. CT/A1 must decide an actual collision/navigation/camera/road-compatible connector for the original coordinates; only then can the canonical Waystation and Relay actions, rewards, cooldowns and return trip be implemented/tested. Do not carve the legacy cliff, translate the POIs or create a second world.
2. CT/authorized owner must specify and mount a real reachable overworld threat and lifecycle. Then test an uninterrupted fresh-save input route with preparation, genuine damage/death, player-selected respawn, physical bag recovery, home return and exact save/queue/structure/item conservation. Dungeon-owned spikes or merely instantiating an enemy class do not fill this gap.
3. Design an authorized item-selective storage experience over existing SaveService/A3 authority, including full bag, multiple chests, unique custody and modal held-until-release behavior. It must remain possible to retrieve what was stored without arbitrary item grants or a hidden UI-only inventory.
4. Run novice human counterfactuals and visual review: at minimum explore-before-shelter and shelter-before-explore, discoverability, elapsed/interaction costs, inventory comprehension, a reason to prepare/return, 1:1 720p/2K/4K and normal Web entry. Establish actual multi-session worthwhile goals and rare-Core/Gold fidelity separately; the Atlas replay gates remain untested.

Evidence limits: no human play, no real threat/death journey, no canonical POI mount, no fully selective/unique chest interaction, no production Web journey, no original-resolution visual verdict, no A4 Gold verification. The general baseline CI remains red and historic performance failures remain unexplained even when later runs pass. PR #21 remains draft/HOLD.
