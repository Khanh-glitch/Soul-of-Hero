## C. Recommended stats architecture: indicator ≠ simulation variable ≠ gameplay consequence
| Variable | What SOH should do (CT proposal; not locked) | Why choices become material | UI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `health_hp` | Real HP; hit/starvation/dehydration/exposure can reduce; treatment, cover, camp comfort recover | food, gear, risk, rescue and death bag matter | ALWAYS |
| `satiety` | Sustenance decreases when active and some time; reaching critical impairs recovery then HP; food species differ in food+hydration and preparation | hunting/foraging/cooking/garden becomes a choice, not cosmetic | ALWAYS |
| `hydration` | thirst increases on sprint/harvest/combat; distinct freshwater/saltwater; bottle, collector, filtering, food tradeoffs; severe deficit lowers capability/HP | choose water source / route / time/carrying items / house by water | ALWAYS |
| `exposure_by_type` | **Contextual** cold/heat/radiation/toxin (environment profile), cumulative with clear mitigation clothing/mask/heat/route; no default all-purpose poison bar | gear and biome access, weather and shelter determine destinations | CONTEXTUAL |
| `energy_stamina` | **CONDITIONAL TEST**, fast-regenerating active combat/sprint/dodge energy if genuinely useful; do NOT lock persistent exhaustion/sleep, and NEVER double charge every Gold Core by stamina+charges without test | combat pacing and physical travel effort, only if agency survives | CONTEXT or NO HUD |
| `gear_durability` | physical tool/armor/weapon wear with source-specific repair/scrap sinks and reliable failure hints; separate Core charge custody from tool durability | repair vs replace vs learn recipe, risk of far expedition | ITEM UI/alert |
| `inventory_capacity` | capacity/stack weight and vehicle cargo; choose space, value/risk, home/forward camp and cart/mount; whether numeric kilograms or slots is NOT fixed | distance, transport, multiple trips and choosing what to leave behind | BAG UI |
| `structure_integrity` | HP/repair of walls, gates, doors, traps; fair monster pathing/trace, visible consequences, no free teleport raid | base architecture, defense and repair meaningful | WHEN ATTACKED/BUILD |
| `comfort_heat` | local base heat from fire/furnace/furniture/roof; modest HP/needs recovery or reduced recovery costs (not magic infinite energy) | living at home between risky trips worth stopping | LOCAL BUFF |
| `wetness` | **DERIVED CONDITIONAL status**, rain raises exposure/fuel use, washing footprints, fills collectors, irrigates crops | weather, footprints and home have intersecting functions | ICON only if relevant |
| `disease_infection` | **CONDITIONAL event/debuff**, e.g. tainted water, poisonous wound or special region. Don't make a persistent general disease bar before it offers distinct medical decisions | medicine/research/hazard route has role | SHORT status icon |
| `run_deadline_utc` | real calendar 14-day countdown including offline; separate from all live needs | creates opportunity costs, seasons and restart interest | VISIBLE TIMER, separate |

Core persistent visible HUD at most **HP + hunger + thirst**, plus combat-specific energy if test proves needed. Temperature/toxin/wetness conditions should be **contextual**. Item wear and cargo are real mechanics but not always a HUD bar. Do not add 8 constant meters that drown AVFX/map.

## D. Cross-system cause → decision → feedback ("must create a meaningful activity")
1. LOGGING/MINING: tool and hydration/food costs; gain wood/ore, feed shelter, fuel, station and advanced gear. Test whether a short no-Core loot run yields net meaningful alternative objectives, not always tax due to water loss.
2. WALK/EXPLORE: travel uses time/cargo/provisions; water source and biome access; far POI worth risk; cart/mount expands radius, but home placement determines route cost. Same stable map with multiple legit homes.
3. HUNT/FIGHT: risk of HP loss, bleeding/poison as enemy-specific debuff, food/material/core drops, gear damage and optional footstep traces; avoid general stamina making Gold first cast an impossible expense.
4. COOK/EAT: raw/dried/cooked food changes satiety/hydration, stack life and maybe short high-quality buff; salt/drying and river water have tradeoffs; cooking stations matter for 14-day preparation.
5. BUILD/REST/REPAIR: room, one simple roof, functional doors, warm hearth, bed, water collector, garden, storage, repair walls; value from shorter healing/food trips and legitimate monster siege route control.
6. WEATHER: rain/wind/snow/heat may modify local exposure, water collection, farm output, fuel/stored food, scent trail lifetime and threat visibility; every negative weather effect should have counterplay.
7. HAZARD/POI: toxin/radiation exposure tied to region + mask consumables; earn rare physical finds and information; different route for prepared equipment, and safe retreat; not arbitrary level/skill gates.
8. DEATH: correct same bag, spatial marker, bed respawn; medical/food/weather/gear planning reduces risk; no forced offline app death until user chooses separately.
9. TRACE/MONSTER: physical player track ages and terrain/weather modifies signal; certain monsters track towards unattended home while app active, attack obstacle, armor/door HP can be repaired; voluntary summon challenges capped by world population, cost and one-event rewards, never free infinite loot from the same group.
10. RESEARCH/CORE: found rare skill Core immediately usable/interesting; shelter/food investment gives safer preparation/return with prized physical Core; death loss motivates studying/crafting, no enforced unused hoarding.

## E. Quantitative/implementation decision gates, NOT fabricated game-original constants
