# M1–M4 gameplay integration handoff

All values below are SOH adaptation/tuning unless specifically sourced. The web New Run is the scope; fixture tests are not a human playthrough.

## M1: optional electrical workbench

At a level-2 workbench install a module for 3 Mechanical Parts, 3 Fasteners, 4 Spares and 2 Iron Ingots. Link a player-owned Field Generator within three tiles and with an unobstructed physical line; enable the module. A generator defaults to defense priority; its panel offers production priority. An active queued job competes for capacity with enabled linked turrets that have ammunition; a zero-ammo turret is not a load. Offline non-firing turrets do not occupy capacity, while an enabled generator retains its idle fuel use. Selected jobs run at the provisional 2× speed; otherwise work is manual at 1×. Capacity two needs the existing paid generator reinforcement. Each allocated load uses fuel; an enabled generator idles at one load. The simulation splits at job and fuel boundaries; linked generator removal clears links. The paid installation, links and priority persist. No requirement for power is added to existing recipes.

## M2: Territory Cabinet — provisional, NOT_FINISHED

The existing `claim` is now the Territory Cabinet. Ordinary construction is allowed at legal physical sites before any cabinet; unattended or unfunded structures lose max-HP-scaled health over two hours, or sooner if already damaged. A newly built cabinet requires a player-owned intact foundation. The nearest supported cabinet governs each structure within **25 SOH gameplay tiles**, ties by ID; this is an explicit gameplay adaptation, not a conversion of LI's 25 metres. Existing bare-ground claims are grandfathered and gain 24 empty physical slots on settlement; old linked chests and item UIDs stay where they are. Legacy per-hit reserve repair has been retired.

Put actual materials in the cabinet. The provisional rate is 25% of ordinary material construction costs per 24 hours, pooled over covered structures. Materials include wood, stone, fiber, rope, brick, iron ingot, scrap, glass, Spares, Mechanical Parts and Fasteners; item-deployed escrow chests and unique equipment are not renewable ingredients. Whole physical items are debited upfront into a saved fractional credit, which is consumed over time and displayed separately. No ingredient can go negative. If any pooled requirement runs out, structures start decaying; funding again stops decay but does not heal. Reclaiming/destroying the cabinet forfeits prepaid credit; its actual slots spill through ordinary custody. Open the cabinet to see its stock, covered count, rates, prepaid credit and forecast. **Repair eligible structures** previews the per-object manual repair cost and spends actual cabinet stock for a quarter-health repair; it does not resurrect or repair excluded item-deployed structures. Manual repair remains available. A destroyed foundation removes the cabinet and roof on it through the normal wreckage path. You can reclaim the cabinet first, then its foundation. Escrow still requires actual owned coverage.

The offline step splits at funded depletion and actual zero-HP boundaries. Return text reports material debits, lost structures and physical remaining stock; physical impacts continue to use their existing evidence rules. Overlap, 335/336-hour ledger arithmetic, zero-boundary collapse and legacy custody have controlled fixture coverage. Rendered range dots are attached to the cabinet sprite, so the world-space ring can disappear when the cabinet itself is offscreen; the cabinet panel has a local coverage map.

**Provenance and limits:** insufficient cabinet stock causing collapse is in the official 2019 FAQ: https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2019/0730/6973.html . An early player guide describes inspecting upkeep and damage after exhaustion: https://www.taptap.cn/moment/15201304955586452 . A later 2022 mirror describes 24 slots, foundation and 25 metres, without establishing the 2019 baseline or SOH conversion: https://www.aspxhome.com/youxi/2022/399674.htm . The official 2024 update mentions a territory repair command: https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2024/0731/13921.html . The 25 gameplay tiles, 25%-per-day upkeep and two-hour full-HP collapse are explicitly provisional SOH adaptations. The 2026 zombie-trial spacing and tier rules were not imported. The 9game source returned a CAPTCHA and was not independently verified.

## M3: existing physical progression

The research table now exposes directed targets, blueprint outcome, station/level, recipe time, craft ingredients, research time and Spares cost. It still uses existing random/directed/specimen modes, physical queue reservations, cancellation and physical blueprint pickup/use. A Damaged Drive can be reserved at the workbench with one Scrap for one Mechanical Parts, or individually reserved at the Foundry Recycler for two Spares; it cannot be in both places. Spares compete for research, the existing finite trader offers and machinery. Mechanical Parts have existing equipment, workbench, generator, cart and module sinks. Tests cover custody and destructive exclusivity but are not a natural journey.

## M4: leaving and returning

The menu estimates depletion and survival from current HP/hunger/thirst under **neutral** exposure, warning that the estimate excludes other hazards and expiry. The return report includes actual hunger and thirst changes, physical production, evidenced impacts, upkeep debits and structure loss. A sheltered character cannot die from hunger alone; thirst, radiation and actual nearby fire can still kill. Cold shelter is still the pre-existing simplified offline model: sheltered areas suppress the zone-cold calculation, rather than simulating online warmth and temporary cold exposure. Creature combat without reconstructible trace evidence remains unresolved, not invented.

**Provenance:** the official LI 2020-08-26 update states that offline 100 hunger points last 24 hours: https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2020/0826/10634.html . That article describes its own mode; applying the numerical baseline to SOH is an adaptation, not a claim of identical rules. LI's 2020-04-29 update introduces thirst but does not give a drain rate: https://hero.yingxiong.com/info/2020/0429/9792.html . SOH therefore provisionally uses thirst at 1.5× the hunger rate (100 in 16 hours), preserving the prior online rate ratio, **not** a historical LI rate. Existing offline damage/environment factor remains. Real saved timestamps determine elapsed time; no supplies are created.

A source already burning within the online 18-pixel radius causes stationary offline-body fire exposure. This is a provisional continuous approximation of the existing 6 damage per roughly 900 ms with armor; it respects committed burnout, spread and rain boundaries but does not reconstruct hit knockback, invulnerability frames or full physical combat. It is not exact online fire parity. Time is never fabricated and rain does not damage houses. Values are centralized in `web/src/game/balance.v1.json`.

## Verification and remaining acceptance

- **NOT_FINISHED:** no full natural, real-input New Run acceptance journey yet. The attempted run started and moved using UI input but did not successfully collect the construction materials. The separate partial smoke covers fresh start, ordinary movement, forecast menu, save and reopen, with no QA grants or state injection; see `web/e2e/territory-partial-smoke.mjs` and `web/evidence/territory/current-menu-640x360.png`. - Still required: gather real resources → foundation → wall/roof and see unprotected decay → cabinet on foundation → transfer wood/stone → view coverage and duration → pay repair → exhaust upkeep and observe HP → save/relaunch and inspect actual report → exercise zero-ammo turret versus powered bench, research and offline survival, all naturally and at 640×360. Do not substitute fixture tests or the old `SOH-GAMEPLAY-M1-M4.html` release for this proof; that release predates the cabinet directive.
- The 2026-11-05 run of the prior full suite was 288/293 with five legacy placement assumptions; after fixture corrections, all 30 tests in those four previously failing files passed. The final targeted regression run passed 67 tests across seven files; TypeScript and the final production build passed. Two later build attempts were killed under memory pressure with the preview running; after stopping that preview, the final build succeeded with a 600 MB Node heap, and the preview was restarted. The full suite was not rerun after those corrections.
- Shelter/exposure effects of a naturally collapsing roof, true 640×360 cabinet usability, and broader save histories still need real-input validation. The fractional credit is intentionally forfeited when a cabinet is destroyed or reclaimed. Offline fire remains an approximation as documented above; no fabricated raids, weather damage or free supplies are introduced.
