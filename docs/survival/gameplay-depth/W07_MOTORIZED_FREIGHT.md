# W07 — powered freight, oil and fuel (Web gameplay handoff)

**Status:** implemented on this PR's Web gameplay branch; PR #44 stays **OPEN / DRAFT / HOLD**, not a Map acceptance or a release gate pass. One optional utility motorbike is a physical, run-local investment, not a starting gift or a quest. Bible K1/K4/K7/K8, Creator decisions and existing custody/clock authority take precedence over this adaptation. No Map, A3, native or VFX owner branch was changed. All rates, capacities and SOH recipes below are **SOH_PROVISIONAL** and reversible; LI fuel rate, handling, wear, cargo, recovery and real-world-to-tile conversions are **ORIGINAL_UNKNOWN**.

## Player loop and existing authorities

- Directed research of the motorbike kit costs the existing 9 physical Spares and 45 seconds; take and use the resulting blueprint. Frame and Motor Drive are known intermediate recipes. At Workbench II, frame costs 3 Iron Ingots + 2 Mechanical Parts + 2 Spares (25 s); drive costs 2 Iron Ingots + 2 Mechanical Parts + 2 Fasteners + 3 Spares (25 s). At **Workbench III**, the learned kit costs frame + drive + 2 Iron Ingots + 2 Fasteners (40 s). This excludes the real costs of gathering, research station, upgrading the workbench, furnace and fuel; each crafting job uses the existing reservation/output custody. Workbench III follows the approved advanced-industry principle, independent of the recipe's tier-2 directed-research permission. No pre-granted kit.
- Place the packed kit near the player on eligible clear ground; placement consumes the exact stack and preserves its UID as the deployed item. Interact with the parked vehicle for a real 12-slot, 80-weight cargo container; right-click inventory/cargo to transfer through normal UID/weight/custody rules. Mount from the panel, drive WASD, exit with F to reachable dry ground. Cannot pull a cart while mounted; mounted combat, rolling and Core actions are blocked. Building requires dismounting. A parked vehicle blocks players and new construction. There is intentionally **no vehicle packing/remote recall**: it and its cargo remain at their actual location.
- Add physical `fuel_can` from inventory: a whole can adds 100 units only when that entire amount fits the 300-unit tank. Generator and motorbike consume the **same inventory item**, not mirrored fuel accounts. The existing W06 wreck salvage can produce finite oil/cans/parts; a learned `refine_salvaged_oil` uses 3 Salvaged Oil + 1 Glass in a **fuelled furnace**, 18 seconds, producing one real Fuel Canister. Existing workbench fuel recipe and actual found cans remain alternatives; this is not an implemented oil pump or a standalone industrial refinery.
- Driving debits tank fuel and vehicle condition only for actual displacement, with load and rough-terrain penalties; a tank or HP at zero stops motion. Displace in <=3-pixel collision steps; no cost for a blocked movement. Dismount and pay 1 Mechanical Part + 1 Fastener for up to 50 HP repair, retaining the same UID. The tank, cargo, HP, odometer, direction and rider/position persist; parking or offline time does not burn drive fuel. Death dismounts and leaves the vehicle/cargo at the death position; normal run end/abandon wipes run assets. The existing bounded receipt ring records deploy, mount, exit (including position/odometer/fuel/HP), refuel, repair and transfers; replayed command IDs cannot repeat costs or outputs. Local snapshots/checksums are not protection against wholesale deliberate rollback.
- Walking is fuel-free and can use narrower paths, carts have existing physical cargo and no motor-fuel draw, and the bike has paid research/assembly/maintenance/fuel and a 12-slot, 80-weight container (the existing basic cart holds more: 24 slots / 240 weight). On provisional eligible roads it is faster than walking/empty cart, but terrain, load and obstacles matter. This is a mechanism and a short same-road comparison, **not** a proven optimum, a three-home journey study or a 336-hour economy balance result.

Provisional tuning lives in `web/src/game/balance.v1.json` (`motorbike`, rates in world pixels/second or per pixel; tile=16 pixels), while physical recipes follow the existing `catalog.ts` recipe authority. Current full-HP empty speeds: road 125, ordinary ground 52, forest/marsh/hill/snow 35; cargo load reduces speed and increases consumption. Base fuel 0.05/pixel, rough multiplier 1.5 and full-load multiplier 1.5; base wear 0.002 HP/pixel with analogous rough/load factors; radius 6, slope maximum 0.6. These are **not** historical LI measurements. Full details, including load and status multipliers, are the code's source of truth.

## Portable geometry contract — explicit Map / A3 / native handoff

The Web type is `VehicleGeometry` in `web/src/game/world.ts`; consumer is `motorbike.ts`. A provider supplies `version: 'vehicle-geometry-v1'`, the same `mapId` and `mapRevision` as the bound economy map, `width`, `height`, `tileSize`, and five row-major arrays of exactly `width * height`: `passable`, `road`, `slope`, `parking`, `exit`. Boolean masks authorize a vehicle footprint, road-speed surface, parked footprint, and dry reachable player exit respectively; `slope` is a finite normalized per-cell value. Runtime checks map identity, revision, shape and expected Web dimensions, then rejects excessive slope, water/shallow/river/ice/lava, blocked collision, other carts/bikes and missing reachable exits. All four corners plus center of a 6-pixel-radius footprint are sampled, including at <=3-pixel movement substeps. Runtime construction/doors/obstacles remain authoritative in addition to masks. A safe exit must remain available at deployment, mount and every movement step.

**This is a gameplay-side proposal, not a Map-authorized certificate or an implemented external package interchange format.** Generated Shipping/Dev Web currently derives masks from its existing `cargoPassable`, `walkable`, `road` and slope data; those masks have not been certified for width, bridges, turning, elevation or full-route connectivity. Veyra/imported layout currently supplies no `vehicleGeometry`; deployment fails closed before charging the kit. We did not add roads or change Map geography. Existing vehicle-bearing saves also need a compatible geometry provider; a future map migration must not simply delete/relocate their cargo.

**Map owner:** review/version the footprint and coordinate units; deliver certified stable masks and map identity/revision as part of the accepted map package, including road/bridge/choke width, slope/elevation, water boundaries, parking/loading, dry exit and alternate approaches; specify dynamic doors, player-built obstructions, clearance changes, seams, revision migration and invalidation. Supply reproducible narrow-bridge, width, edge and connectivity cases for actual routes. Decide whether this interface and 6-pixel radius are appropriate; they are not Creator-accepted SI dimensions. Until explicitly adapted and verified, imported maps remain disabled for deployment.

**A3/economy owner:** validate scarcity and legitimate source availability against its actual published map, shared canister economy, fuel/repair/upgrade opportunity costs and walking/cart/motor net useful delivered cargo after travel/risk/maintenance across multiple routes and play styles. Tune `balance.v1.json` and existing recipe authority with evidence; do not copy an LI rate into SOH or grant a vehicle to resolve a blocked map. Oil extraction/refinery integration needs authorized sites, genuine power, stock, elapsed time and physical output.

**Native owner:** port the same versioned map identity and footprint/exit semantics, deployed item UID and exclusive cargo custody, whole-can debits, actual-distance fuel/wear, receipts/replay, mounted controls/death and snapshot migrations. Share deterministic collision, boundary, transaction and save fixtures on an agreed exact source SHA; no native implementation or cross-owner parity is claimed here. Resolve schema and map package handoff before accepting W07 for that runtime.

## Evidence / known limits

- `web/tests/motorbike.test.ts`: nine **controlled** cases: paid research, timed intermediates, level-III gate and assembly; UID/cargo/fuel/repair/save; empty fuel and collision; death/run wipe; actual wreck oil through funded furnace to vehicle; weight/swaps/invalid tank saves; short same-road walking/cart/empty/loaded comparison; imported Veyra fail-closed and replay; actual Foundry loot through cargo to repair. Tests place stations, stock or player proximity deliberately; they do not establish natural progression. The obstacle case uses a constructed wall, not every terrain, bridge or gate.
- `web/e2e/w07-motorbike-fixture.mjs` and `web/evidence/w07-motorbike-fixture/trace.json` plus 640×360 screenshots: a **controlled** placed vehicle, fuel, ingots and road proximity; genuine browser E, mouse/right-click, WASD and F load/refuel/drive/park/unload and save/reopen. Trace preserves vehicle and ingot UIDs and captures decreasing fuel/HP. Screenshot/proximity is not evidence of paying for or reaching the vehicle naturally, Map owner approval, a full expedition, or replay across a real 336-hour run.
- Targeted W07 + oil/power/production/loot/world-economy regressions, TypeScript and production build are run as recorded in the PR comment. Old saves are handled by optional fields and existing recipe compatibility; representative targeted validation is not a proof of all legacy snapshots or offline conditions. Explicit reverse-overweight swap, every mounted intent, all source layouts and extended balancing still merit further integration tests. A local checksum cannot prevent malicious full rollback.
