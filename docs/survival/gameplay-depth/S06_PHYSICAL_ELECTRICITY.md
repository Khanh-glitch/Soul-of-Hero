## Build, operation and custody

Build menu → Defense. With locally obtained materials and a reachable legal tile, build a new field generator, adjacent cable tiles and optional nodes. Orthogonal adjacency only: a generator port connects to a cable/switch/plate, continuous powered nodes connect to the consumer port. No diagonal, proximity-only jump, consumer relay, POI relay, blocked/water tile or wall crossing. One component per object/wall tile; existing floor layer may coexist. Route around physical obstacles rather than assuming a wall conduit. Existing build masks, protected corridors, reach, inventory, identity, repair, destruction, reclaim and territory upkeep apply; `need:claim` retains the project's existing optional cabinet coverage/decay behavior, not a new free privilege. Unclaimed components can decay. Entity UIDs and per-tile build receipts survive save.

| Physical component | Cost per paid build (SOH_PROVISIONAL) | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cable | 1 iron ingot, 1 fiber | conducting tile |
| Manual switch | 1 iron ingot, 1 fasteners, 1 spares | E/F toggles branch; starts off |
| Pressure switch | 1 iron ingot, 1 mechanical parts, 1 fasteners | conducts only with the living player within 7 world units of center |
| Powered door | 4 iron ingot, 2 mechanical parts, 3 fasteners, 3 spares | blocking wall-layer consumer |

Costs are removed by the existing build transaction; replay is rejected. Iron ingots are furnace-smelted from iron ore, fiber comes from plants, mechanical parts may be found or made at the bench, and fasteners come from found parts or the learned bench recipe; spares remain physical run-local salvage/loot. Those acquisition paths are existing systems; this feature does not grant materials or invent a new item economy. Repair uses existing quarter-HP/quarter-cost rounded-up policy; reclaim and destruction use existing physical salvage/custody rules.

Place a powered door **while standing outside**; the opposite cardinal direction is its explicit interior handle side. An allocated powered door can be opened from either side. Without allocated power only the interior handle opens it, with a four-second minimum release; an unpowered open door closes once the release has expired and the doorway is clear. It never closes on the living player, a live creature, cart or vehicle. Existing collision cache reflects opening/closing. Do not assume the handle knows an enclosed room: the player chooses orientation at placement and must leave a physically reachable exit route. Door allocation uses the lowest stable ID among physically connected wired generators, even if that generator is off; there is no automatic failover. Shut a branch off or disconnect/re-route to change that deterministic choice.

A wired generator shares its unchanged finite fuel with the real enabled armed turrets, active paid industrial jobs, and connected doors. Defense priority ranks armed turret, then door, then production; production priority ranks active workbench, then door, then turret; ties use stable entity UID. One load normally, two after the existing paid reinforcement. Wired links may exceed capacity: losing consumers remain connected but unallocated. Generator idles at one load and burns one unit per allocated load. Turret still requires real arrows and existing authorization/LOS and never fires or takes capacity offline. Industrial jobs retain reserved input/output custody and proceed at existing manual speed when unpowered. Existing dropdowns show physically connected generators, retaining the currently selected ID when temporarily disconnected.

### Compatibility, time and boundaries

A generator lacking `network` is **legacy** and retains its pre-S06 proximity/line-of-sight and turret-link-count rule; no fuel, UID, ammo, links, jobs or structures are migrated or gifted. A nearby player may explicitly confirm one-way conversion in its panel. Converted and newly built generators require paid physical wiring; pre-existing link IDs are retained but cannot conduct without a route. New switch and door state are validated, including versions, cardinal orientation and positions; graph topology is derived from physical entities after load. The switch is local/replay-protected. The pressure switch observes only the actual living player's position; during offline settlement that saved position is stationary, not fabricated movement. `advanceWorld` splits at existing fuel/job/territory/weather events plus interior-release deadline, synchronizing door state at boundaries; no full-frame offline actor replay. Territory loss, broken/destroyed or blocked nodes sever routes. Run ending wipes all circuit entities along with the rest of the run.

S05 POI circuits have independent map-authorized contracts: home wires cannot connect to POI generators or doors or override cards/access. Imported Veyra POI access remains fail-closed absent its Map contract. No Veyra geography, A3, or native adapter was authored here. More triggers, lighting/traps, wiring through walls, power meters, multi-generator failover, full closed-room validation, broader balancing and natural-play acceptance remain open.
