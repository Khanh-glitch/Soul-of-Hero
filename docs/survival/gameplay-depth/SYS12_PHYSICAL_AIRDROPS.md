## Contract and lifecycle

New runs on `shipping_fresh` and `dev_delta_fresh` pin `airdrop-v1`, the run ID, committed seed, original `startedAt`, and exact map digest. Legacy saves without the additive field remain valid and do not receive a retroactive event schedule; no structures, UIDs, stock or paid jobs are migrated. Imported Veyra/WMP/package maps are **disabled**, even when another owner provides a spawn or vehicle contract. They require a Map-owned, versioned `airdrop-landing-v1` (or equivalent certified) adapter before enabling. The built-in mask adapter is provisional, not Map-certified.

- Up to 14 signals: `startedAt + 8h + index × 24h + deterministic 0–20min jitter`. Event hash includes run ID, committed seed, start, map digest and version/index. These are original run wall-clock timestamps, independent of launch/session and deliberately not an invented six-hour schedule. The run expires at 336h under the existing single clock.
- Signal → 120s warning → 60s visible descent → landed/locked → 180s unlock → accessible → partially looted → empty, or expire exactly 90 minutes after landing. The signal reports one of nine coarse compass sectors, not exact coordinates, item contents, or named undiscovered POIs. Nearby interaction shows the lock countdown. Descent and landed crate are drawn in world space; landed/locked/open crates block the real collision grid.
- `advanceWorld` splits online and bounded offline integration at each event boundary alongside weather, stations, power, territory and ecology. Chronological receipts record signal, skipped, descent, landed, access, partial, empty, aborted and expiry. Offline reports describe missed/expired stock; **no items are credited** without physical withdrawal. Event objects and their take-only `loot` containers are removed when empty/expired; they never use static-loot respawn.
- At signal, derive legal candidates from real built-in Web map terrain and economy masks. Center must be walkable/buildable, outdoor dry land, slope ≤0.35, and outside roads/protection; the 3×3 clearance excludes protected cells, road/public corridors, authored source cells, static entities and building margins. A four-neighbor flood of the live `blockGrid` from the saved spawn establishes a plausible route, with current player structures and blockers. Seeded ranking chooses among valid cells; none → skip without a container or stock. At actual landing, recheck terrain, dynamic grid, player structures/entities, temporary trees, player and living creatures; obstruction → abort without minting. This is a conservative route to the saved spawn at signal, **not** proof of a player's later travel, current door access, or an owner-certified slope/eligibility raster.
- Only at a successful landing are three UID-backed stacks created: 2 mechanical parts + 2 fasteners + one equal-weight choice of 1 fuel can, 2 refined energy, 2 iron ingots or 1 iron sword. One bounded tier of useful industrial material plus a variable utility/combat tier; no exclusive Core, essence, Ragnarok Cash, profile currency, or unique technology. Fourteen events cap total fixed stock at 28 of each material and at most 14 option stacks. Existing public W06 stock and its strict source validation remain separate. No proof of universal economy balance or all possible arbitrage cycles is claimed.
- Normal E dispatches `openAirdrop` and denies locked/out-of-reach use. Existing `loot` custody prevents deposit/merge/swap into the crate; all other withdrawals remain range/line-of-sight checked. Remainders keep their UIDs and quantities through reload; partial state survives until the exact expiry. Saving validates run and map pins, due index, phases/times, a real eligible static cell, exclusive container ownership, slot quantities against the deterministic reward, and generic UID uniqueness. Neither signal/descent nor an aborted landing contains rewards.

## Evidence and choices

The final production build fixture in `web/e2e/s12-airdrop-fixture.mjs` uses a controlled clock and relocates the player **after** successful landing (for the separate descent image, relocation is only for that visual frame); creatures are suppressed. It never grants an airdrop item. Ordinary E locked denial, E open, and right-click partial withdrawal are real browser input. This is not natural travel, combat, gate traversal, or player-balance evidence. It may trigger normal nearby map discovery from the disclosed relocation; the airdrop warning itself does not discover POIs. Other possible uses are existing systems, not completed demonstrations: explorer may ignore/race the optional sector; base/industry can save parts/fasteners for bench/generator upgrades or repair; refined energy has existing Core consumers; sword can be used, researched or recycled; fuel can be spent on the existing generator.
