# W06 / S11 — authored loot ecology (SOH adaptation)

**Status:** implemented for new Shipping Fresh and Dev Delta Fresh Web runs; focused tests and controlled UI evidence, **not** a natural access, full-run balance, Map acceptance, or S12 completion claim. PR #44 remains draft/HOLD. Bible K1/K3/K4/C02 and existing custody/Creator decisions govern this implementation. No Map master, WMP, A0/A3/A7/AVFX, geography or original cache positions were changed.

## Scope and versioned source contract

`w06-v1` is pinned at new run creation, with source identity `layout:POI:tile`, run seed and a persisted per-site generation cycle. Its two additional ordinary stacks are selected by a stable independent hash, not world RNG. Existing authored stock remains first, including unique creator Core bodies and their UIDs. `loot.roll` receipts identify each initial/replenished slot and physical UID; the normal 250-receipt ring is bounded, not an immutable audit server. Existing unpinned saves keep their old materialization and cooldown rules. No generated slots are assigned to unknown POIs or sites whose authored stock would threaten the 8-slot capacity.

| Class / eligible existing Web site | Additional ordinary stock, each cycle | Refill after last item (SOH provisional) | Real downstream use / choice |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Toolbox: Foundry exterior, the Trading Post bow-blueprint crate, Dev Delta Maintenance Cache | 2 Spares + either 2 Fasteners or 1 Field Repair Kit | 24 h | Spares fund directed research/Recycler economy; Fasteners compete between repairs, workbench upgrades, production and specimen research; a kit repairs one carried tool. The original bow blueprint remains finite. |
| Supply: other Trading Post, Mill, exterior Lighthouse, Evac caches | 1 Bandage + either 2 Berries or 2 Bitterleaf | 12 h | Heal immediately, eat, or save Bitterleaf for recipes and treatment. Original foods/medical stock remains as authored. |
| Military: Watchtower and Halcyon wreck POI caches | 4 Basic Energy + either 4 Arrows or 2 Spares | 24 h | Load a real Core for combat; Arrows can feed bows/turrets; Spares support research/industry. Watchtower keycard is one-time. |
| Advanced military: Foundry Vault, Lighthouse top, Dev Delta Relay Vault Cache | 1 Refined Energy + either 1 Mechanical Part or 2 Spares | 48 h | Refined Energy loads a Core technique, or can be combined with compatible essence for Signature; parts feed upgrades. Deep-site original Core bodies, credentials and blueprints remain one-time. |

The two bonus stacks are **additions**, not replacement of the site's authored material. Each table chooses one of its two options; quantities and timings above are provisional SOH balancing choices, **not LI drop rates or historical room timers**. Existing renewable authored resource/food entries also return; other exceptional entries, `damaged_drive`, blueprints, keycards, cash, Core bodies and special equipment do not. This is not a promise that every individual source contains every member of its class, nor a universal guarantee that a player has a Core or a bench. Generated repair kits are ordinary consumables; they can also be studied through the existing research rules. Existing crafting and research remain alternative acquisition routes.

A physical public container is take-only. Partial stock and its UIDs persist indefinitely; only final withdrawal/use/drop retires it and schedules a timer. The existing online/offline settlement creates a single replacement at expiry if unblocked, with incremented cycle and fresh UIDs; it does not refill an open partial box. Persistence authenticates site, cycle and exact class roll alongside original stock, rejecting inflated/forged contents and rare stock in renewed W06 boxes. Existing rollback of *all* local save copies is not prevented by a local checksum and is not an anti-cheat guarantee.

## S12 handoff: no invented airdrop

No airdrop was spawned. Before SYS12-C03 can be implemented, Map must certify persistent, identifiable outdoor landing cells/areas and their terrain, slope/ground and vertical clearance; exclude water, walls, roofs/interiors, unsafe terrain, existing protected/POI structures and critical road/cargo/retreat paths; specify how player-built foundations, other entities and changing occupancy invalidate or defer a landing. Provide navigable approaches/retreats and visibility/marker anchors, with stable source/map identity and deterministic eligible-site selection. Gameplay still needs a separately approved finite run-clock schedule, physical landing/open/expiry lifecycle, stock/UID/custody policy, UI/audio/VFX and legitimate enemy perception. No 2019 PvP/store flare, six-hour or ten-minute historical value is adopted as SOH canon.
