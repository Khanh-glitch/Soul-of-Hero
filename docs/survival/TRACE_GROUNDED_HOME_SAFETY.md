## What exists and what changed

The starting game already made run-owned footprints from collision-resolved player movement, linked identifiable marks, decayed them by terrain/weather, and let creatures sense marks, remember player sightings, and path locally. It also already had walls, doors, charge and other attack styles, and offline weather/production/exposure integration. Those are not claimed as new. Previously a tracker could acquire a linked successor without seeing or being close to it; a wandering or melee creature could bash structures just because pathfinding collided; and source swings did not check intervening walls. The UI announced a nearby creature's internal tracking state even if the player could not see it. In-flight projectiles and hazards were module globals, not saved.

- A creature must first encounter a live identified mark within its 20-unit sense radius and line of sight. At the remembered mark it can advance only to its live, timestamp-ordered successor in the same chain, at most 24 units from its predecessor, and only when that successor itself is locally visible and in sensing range. The creature moves toward the mark rather than moving its position to the next mark. It loses a missing, expired or inaccessible continuation. Crossing a different trail does not magically transfer knowledge. Later independent physical encounter of a mark is possible; legacy marks without identities are not retroactively linked.
- Collision/pathfinding remains local. Wandering and ordinary melee cannot randomly damage structures. A charge-capable boar tracking an actually sensed mark can hit a blocking structure it physically collides with; a committed charge can hit a structure in its path. Elders retain their own physical collision attack while they have valid knowledge. These are not guarantees of a raid: a closed door can hide the next mark, a wolf cannot bash it just by wandering, and a boar must actually reach a barrier. Direct pursuit after a real player sighting remains possible. Player options include changing direction, clearing or avoiding nearby animals, waiting for evidence to fade, using doors and terrain, or accepting risk; none is mandatory.
- Grid-line traversal checks crossed cells and corner contacts. Player source/torch selection checks intervening blockers and the source tile before spending durability or changing stock/fire. Committed close-range creature hits respect intervening walls; fast projectiles sweep through their path rather than skipping a wall in one frame. Homing projectiles do not acquire hidden live player coordinates through cover. This does not assert that every magical area effect is wall-shielded.
- The prep menu shows shelter, current weather, and a rounded wet-hours outlook over up to the next 24 hours from the **existing global seeded schedule**, not a regional climate forecast. It explains trace/weather and the offline policy without reading creature minds. The hidden `Something is following your tracks` indicator was removed. Footprints have a slightly larger, higher-contrast snow mark; perception rules are unchanged by rendering.

## Offline and save contract

`settle` continues existing ordered weather, trace decay, fire, garden/barrel/Tender, station, exposure and run-end integration. It does **not** replay creature locomotion, attacks, spawns, lightning or a speculative raid. Creature positions and committed actor attack/tell state are preserved; stale last-known sight (>4 seconds) and trace knowledge whose physical mark expired are cleared. Additive `combat` save data preserves committed projectiles/hazards and their timers at the last saved physical state; they do not age or hit during offline settlement and resume in live play. Older saves without this optional field load without inventing prior projectiles. Validating the new field fails closed on malformed effects. As with all local saves, unsaved frames between the most recent snapshot and abrupt termination are not reconstructed. Visual FX are not saved.

The return report reports elapsed time, current local shelter/weather and visible nearby marks, actual exposure/production notes where applicable, and explicitly says actors did not move or attack while away. The world is live behind the report after return. This is a conservative **unresolved** policy, not a claim of full offline creature replay or an assurance of safety. No home, chore, feeding ritual, or anti-raid ritual is required.

## Evidence and boundaries

`web/e2e/trace-home-smoke.mjs` generates the footprint chain with actual browser `D` input on the existing shipping raster, then creates controlled saved encounter fixtures: a wolf and a three-cell wall/door near the route. The broken case removes one generated mark (a controlled analogue of an expired predecessor); the unrelated-home case removes the chain near the wolf. The subsequent `D` input, live simulation and screenshots are real. `observations.json` records positions, knowledge and HP through the QA inspection hook; it is evidence of state, not a player-facing warning. Example from the inspected run:

| Controlled scenario | Live observation |
| --- | --- |
| Connected trail | Wolf moved from ~842 to ~862 and held the locally encountered successor; door stayed 140 HP. |
| Broken trail | Wolf stopped tracking and ignored the broken chain; door stayed 140 HP. |
| Unrelated, undiscovered home | Wolf wandering against the closed door had no trace knowledge; door stayed 140 HP. |

Captured and inspected both 640×360 and 1280×720: `web/evidence/trace-home/{valid-trail,broken-trail,undiscovered-home,preparation,return-report}-*.png`. The 640px marks are visible but small, especially amid snow and weather; readability needs human review. `trace-door-smoke.mjs` uses a controlled generated-tree/door fixture and real mouse + E input: a closed door blocks the torch without wear/fire, opening it permits the same hit and ignition; captures are `door-{blocks,open}-torch-*`. This demonstrates a real input boundary, not naturally earned materials or a naturally built house. `trace-return-smoke.mjs` shows the actual Continue/report path with a controlled two-minute old save. `trace-combat-save-smoke.mjs` uses the real save-and-quit/reload UI and a simulated two-minute clock jump with controlled in-flight effects. Focused unit checks additionally cover a boar sensing a mark before a door closes, physical impact, a missing successor, expiry, ordinary wandering, wall/corner tool attacks, and save isolation.

Automated evidence is not human enjoyment or 336-hour balance evidence. Prioritize human tests of trail readability on grass/snow/rain, useful route choices, whether charge impact is understandable and fair, door/corner feel, return-report reading under live pressure, and whether the deliberately conservative offline policy feels appropriate. Tuning (sense radius, 24-unit link cap, weather decay, structure damage) is provisional. QA fixtures do not establish a naturally reached home economy or a full natural creature-to-home playthrough.

## Explicit dependencies

No geography, raster dimensions, source compiler, legacy v1/v2 source identity, economy, farming, or creator-owned map semantics were rewritten. MAP-GEO-02 is a delivered authoring handoff, not runtime Veyra integration; MAP-SCALE-01 and the separate MAP-GEO-01 dependency remain outside this change. No invented conversion scale, biome legend, slope units, climate values or weather geography is implied. The seeded global outlook is the current prototype's schedule, not evidence of Veyra's climate.
