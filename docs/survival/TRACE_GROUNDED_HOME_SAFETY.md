## Causal mechanics and player choices

The existing game already created linked, weather-decaying marks from real collision-resolved movement, creature perception, structures, weather and offline production. A creature now encounters an identified live mark only within its 20-unit sense radius and line of sight; it moves physically to it and can continue only along a timestamp-ordered, visible, sensed successor of the same chain, at most 24 units from its predecessor. It does not inspect a hidden successor or a home location. Legacy unidentified marks are not invented into a chain. A later independent physical encounter is possible even after a chain is broken.

Evidenced tracking also continues outside the player activation radius. A charge-capable creature following its last sensed mark can physically impact a reachable closed barrier in the mark's direction, using real structure HP, collision, destruction and `struct.hit` receipts. The direction is inferred only from the already sensed mark, not a hidden destination. Committed charges and Elders retain their existing physical impact capabilities; ordinary wandering or melee does not randomly bash houses. Real spikes damage a creature on contact, take wear and are destroyed when worn out. Removing a threat, changing or breaking a route, waiting for weather/expiry, doors, terrain or defenses can lead to different results; none is a mandatory logout chore or guarantees immunity.

Physical source/torch swings require a clear line through grid cells and corners. Close-range creature hits respect intervening walls; fast projectiles sweep rather than skip walls, and homing does not acquire hidden player coordinates through cover. Not every magical area effect is asserted to be wall-shielded. The prep menu shows local shelter/current weather and a rounded wet-hours estimate from the existing global seeded schedule, not regional Veyra climate or hidden creature knowledge. The old omniscient tracking indicator is removed. Footprint marks, real impact feedback, damaged-structure bars and nearby damaged-target HP are visible in the game.

## Offline, chronology and save limits

`settle` interleaves existing weather/trace decay, fire, production, shelter and exposure with physical 50 ms-or-smaller trace-actor steps. A candidate must already exist at its saved position and have trace knowledge or physically sense an identified mark **at the start of settlement**. At most 24 candidates and 2,400 actor frames are processed per settlement (at most 120 seconds; weather and other boundaries may use shorter frames). Actual contacts can change/destroy owned structures, including spike wear; the return report lists actual offline HP changes or destruction. Shelter is recalculated after destruction. Weather and trace lifetime are processed chronologically, including rain/storm decay before a later impact. No creatures are spawned, teleported or directed toward a hidden base.

Player sight, pending combat/projectiles/hazards, nearby fire, unsupported actor states, knockback, Elders and exhausted candidate/frame budgets are not resolved as trace-only encounters. Those supported candidates are deferred at their last justified physical position; over-cap and previously committed sight/combat/knockback actors also get an additive, persisted `offlineUnresolvedAt` checkpoint. Later remote/offline ticks do not treat their uncertain interval as resolved; normal nearby live play resumes from the checkpoint. Non-candidate roaming is not replayed, and a creature that could first discover evidence only by wandering later during the same offline interval is not introduced as a new candidate. Trace expiry and stale sight can still clear knowledge. Additive `combat` data retains committed projectile/hazard state without offline hits; older saves lacking either optional field remain valid. Abrupt termination can lose unsaved frames; cosmetic FX are not saved.

This is a bounded approximation, **not** a full dynamic-world replay. A general solution requires a persisted, deterministic event timeline/scheduler for all actor movement and perception, combat and environmental interactions (including newly encountered evidence) with bounded catch-up and explicit ordering/checkpoint semantics. It cannot safely be replaced by invented paths, attacks, random raids or assumed player activity. The existing offline world rules have their own limits, such as no lightning replay. A safe-looking home is not a promise about future live encounters. The report is a snapshot of settlement; the world runs behind it after return and can cause additional live damage.

## Inspected short demonstrations

`web/e2e/trace-home-consequences.mjs` builds an actual paid, claimed, roofed and walled house with a door via existing dispatch/build receipts on the existing shipping map. A real browser D-key walk generates 10 linked marks through the open door, then E closes it. The scenario injects an existing **already provoked boar** at a controlled position; it is not a natural encounter or a full survival playthrough. In the broken case the fixture removes one generated mark as a controlled analogue of a missing/expired link; the safe case removes the chain. Each uses live browser simulation; offline advances a controlled clock before Continue. `web/evidence/trace-consequences/observations.json` records state and receipts via the QA hook (not a player-facing AI warning).

| Scenario | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Connected trail, live | Boar moved from approximately x=682 to x=758; real paid door HP 140 → 127, with `struct.hit` receipt. |
| Broken link | No impact, door remains 140 HP in this controlled interval. |
| Undiscovered home | No trace knowledge or impact, door remains 140 HP in this controlled interval. |
| Connected trail, offline | Return report records real door damage during elapsed time; further live impacts can occur behind the report. |

Inspected `valid`, `broken`, `safe` and `offline-impact` captures at both 640×360 and 1280×720 under `web/evidence/trace-consequences/`. At native 640, footprints and approaching boar are visible but small in snow; the valid case shows the nearby door's actual 127/140 HP, while the report's damage line is legible. This is automated correctness evidence, not a claim of enjoyment or balance. Human review is still needed across terrain/weather and for fairness and live return pressure.

Earlier `web/e2e/trace-home-smoke.mjs` and `web/evidence/trace-home/` show a wolf approaching, broken/undiscovered cases and HP 140 throughout; they do **not** demonstrate an impact. `trace-door-smoke.mjs` uses real mouse/E input with a controlled door/tree fixture to demonstrate torch obstruction/opening. `trace-return-smoke.mjs` demonstrates Continue/report with an old saved timestamp. `trace-combat-save-smoke.mjs` exercises save/quit/reload with controlled committed effects. Unit tests cover physical impact and destruction, spike defense, wet decay at a weather boundary, partitioned chronology, remote tracking, broken/expired marks, candidate cap/checkpoint serialization, unresolved combat, old saves and existing collisions.
